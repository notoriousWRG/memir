The present age. Mortals rebuild in the ruins of what the Age of Ruin destroyed, rediscovering lost knowledge while trying to avoid the mistakes that brought the last civilization down. Factions vie for dominance in the absence of clear divine guidance. The Bifröst's severing means the realms are still partially isolated, their reconnection a slow and contested process.

Beneath the surface, the prophecies run their course. Whispers of Loki's chains straining reach across the Heartlands. The Circle works in shadow. The Frostbound Covenant tightens its grip on northern politics. And somewhere in the Shattered Vale, Fenrir tests his chains. The Age of Rebirth has not yet become the next ruin. Whether it does depends on who acts first, and how.
