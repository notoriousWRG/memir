---
title: The Age of Ruin
type: history-entry
era: The Age of Ruin
period: Cataclysm and Collapse
---

When Fenrir's chains broke, he rose from his imprisonment and tore through the civilizations of the Arcane Age. Cities were flattened. Seas rolled. Skies darkened. No gods came to aid mortals — either as punishment for their arrogance or because they feared the unbound wolf.

The world's great civilizations were overrun or undone. The Bifröst was severed. The realms isolated themselves from one another. Mortals, united in desperation, forged Gleipnir Reborn from impossible components and re-bound Fenrir in a final battle that reshaped Midgard's very geography. The jagged peaks, flooded valleys, and broken plains of the Shattered Vale are the scars of that fight. Many heroes died securing the chain. The age they saved became the rubble the next age had to build from.
