Fenrir's rampage at the end of the Arcane Age broke the Shattered Vale into its present form — jagged mountains, deep chasms, barren plains, and rivers of wild magical energy that are traces of Ymir's Breath flowing uncontrolled through the wounds in the earth. The people who survived and rebuilt here became the Vale: fiercely independent, hardened by necessity, organized into wandering warrior clans that honor the heroes who fought to re-bind the Great Wolf.

The Vale is where the second chain holds. Somewhere in its depths, Fenrir waits.
