A tavern in the lower ward of Vel's Rest, built slightly into the hillside so that one end of the common room is a foot lower than the other. The hanging lantern above the bar lists the same direction.

The owner, Pell, asks no questions and remembers everything. The back room is for conversations that don't happen.
