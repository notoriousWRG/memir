A toll bridge over the River Vel where the Thornmarch Road crosses into the lowlands. The planks are perpetually damp. The toll house leans slightly east, as though trying to peer at the road ahead of its occupant.

Travelers who know the Crossing know that the toll is never quite what it says on the post, and that the keeper keeps two ledgers.
