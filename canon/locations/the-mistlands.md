A swampy region cloaked in perpetual supernatural mist that shifts and reconfigures, making orientation a skill rather than a given. Travelers report seeing Hel's shadow through the fog, guiding the dead toward Helheim. The people of the Mistlands are secretive, spiritually focused, and deeply expert in death — burial rites, divination, communion with ancestors. They are sought out from across Midgard when someone needs help understanding what comes after, or needs to ensure the dead stay that way.
