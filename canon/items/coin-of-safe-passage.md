title: Coin of Safe Passage
type: item
rarity: uncommon
attunement: false
item_type: wondrous item
---

A dull bronze coin stamped on one face with a bridge and on the other with a closed eye. Carried by those who have a standing arrangement with a particular gate, toll, or checkpoint keeper.

When shown to the keeper of the specific post it was issued for, it functions as a wordless acknowledgment of the arrangement — no inspection, no questions. It has no magic. The power is entirely social, and entirely dependent on the arrangement remaining intact.

Maren Dusk carries three of these. Serren issued all of them.
