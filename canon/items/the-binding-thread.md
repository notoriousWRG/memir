---
title: The Binding Thread
type: item
rarity: rare
attunement: true
item_type: wondrous item
---

A length of grey silk thread, always exactly as long as needed. When two willing creatures each hold one end and speak a sworn oath aloud, the thread tightens once and then goes slack. If either party breaks the oath, they know it — a cold sensation at the wrist that lasts until the debt is settled.

The thread can be used once per oath. It doesn't enforce anything. It only witnesses.
