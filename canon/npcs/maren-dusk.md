Maren moves goods that aren't supposed to move — herbs the guild taxes to death, letters the crown isn't meant to see, occasionally people. She has an arrangement with the toll keeper at Ashveil Crossing that costs her a tenth of each shipment's value and keeps her out of the garrison's ledger.

She doesn't think of herself as a criminal. She thinks of herself as someone who solved the problem the law created.
