Responsible for conducting the rituals and ceremonies by which The Circle empowers the children of Loki. Elara's specific focus is Liriel's infused items — objects whose life-force rituals capture the soul of a sacrificed individual and contain traces of Hel's power, usable to increase Hel's influence in Midgard.
