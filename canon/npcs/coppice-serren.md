Serren has kept the Ashveil toll post for eleven years. He knows every trader on the Thornmarch Road by wagon wheel. He keeps two ledgers — the official one for the garrison commander and the real one, which records the arrangement with Maren Dusk and three others he'd rather not name.

He's not corrupt by nature. He got into the arrangement because a cold winter left him short on what the garrison expected, and now the arrangement is simply the shape of his life. He would like, someday, for it not to be.
