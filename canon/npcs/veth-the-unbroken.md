Veth has fought under six different banners and outlived three employers. The "Unbroken" started as a taunt after she survived a battle that killed everyone else in her unit; she adopted it before anyone could decide whether it was an insult.

She takes work that doesn't require her to pretend she cares about the cause. She cares about getting paid, not dying, and occasionally the people she's traveling with. In that order.
