---
title: Idunn
type: god
domains:
  - Youth
  - Immortality
  - Rejuvenation
  - Apples
allegiance: Aesir
symbol: A golden apple
---

Keeper of the golden apples that sustain the gods' immortality, Idunn holds something irreplaceable. Without her, the gods age. Her followers seek longevity, healing, and the renewal of what has begun to fail. She is revered most quietly — not through battle or proclamation, but through the careful preservation of what sustains life.
