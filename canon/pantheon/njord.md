symbol: A ship or waves
---

God of the sea, Njord is the patron of all who make their living on the water — sailors, fishermen, and merchants of the Sapphire Coast above all. Ship blessings performed before every departure invoke his name. He is a god of prosperity as much as navigation: a good catch, a fair trade, a voyage completed. His followers understand that the sea feeds and kills in equal measure, and they honor Njord because they have no other choice.
