Called the Lady of Mercy, Nienna is the goddess who witnesses suffering without flinching and teaches that grief is not weakness but the measure of what mattered. Her followers are healers, caregivers, and those seeking solace — anyone who has had to keep going anyway. She stands apart from both the Aesir and the Antagonist factions, bound only to the work of compassion.
