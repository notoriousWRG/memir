---

Goddess of marriage, motherhood, and prophecy, Frigg is the guardian of the household and the bond between partners. She sees the future but rarely speaks of it. Her followers are mothers, wives, and homemakers who find the sacred in the ordinary — the thread pulled tight, the hearth kept, the family held together.
