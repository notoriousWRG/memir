---
title: Frey
type: god
domains:
  - Fertility
  - Prosperity
  - Sunlight
  - Agriculture
allegiance: Vanir
symbol: A golden boar
---

God of fertility, prosperity, and sunlight, Frey is the patron of abundant harvests and good fortune. His blessing is invoked over fields and markets alike. Along with Freyja, he is said to bless the Verdant Glades. His followers are farmers seeking good harvests, merchants seeking good trades, and anyone seeking abundance in a world that offers neither reliably.
