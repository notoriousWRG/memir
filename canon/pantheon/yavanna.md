title: Yavanna
type: god
domains:
  - Nature

  - Harvest
  - Protection of Flora
allegiance: Vanir
symbol: A flowering tree
---

Called the Giver of Fruits, Yavanna watches over every seed that becomes a field and every field that feeds a people. She is a goddess of patience and deep time, revered by druids, farmers, and those who understand that the world is alive and sustains itself only through care. Her followers protect growing things.
