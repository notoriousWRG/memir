title: Thor

allegiance: Aesir
symbol: Mjolnir (his hammer)
---

God of thunder and strength, Thor is the protector of mortals and a champion of Asgard. He is worshipped by warriors who seek strength in battle, farmers who need the rains to come, and sailors who need the storms to pass. The prophecy of Jormungandr foretells that Thor will slay the World Serpent at the end of days — but will himself fall nine steps later, dead from its venom.
