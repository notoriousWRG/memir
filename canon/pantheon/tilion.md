The Hunter of the Moon, Tilion guides those who move through darkness — navigators reading the night sky, hunters who track by moonlight, travelers who have no other landmark to follow. He stands apart from the divine factions, bound to the arc of the moon rather than to any side of the old wars. His followers are hunters, navigators, and anyone who needs a fixed point when everything else shifts.
