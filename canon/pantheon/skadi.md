Goddess of winter and vengeance, Skadi rules the frozen reaches of Niflheim. She wields Frostreaver, an enchanted glaive of unbreakable ice that freezes everything it touches. Her wrath is slow and deliberate — she views emotion as weakness and control as the only legitimate form of power. She seeks vengeance for her father's imprisonment and the banishment of her siblings, and she considers the chaotic fires of Muspelheim an existential affront. The Frostbound Covenant serve her vision on Midgard, infiltrating institutions and removing whoever threatens her reach.
