title: Hel
type: god
domains:
  - Death

  - Underworld
  - Rebirth
allegiance: Antagonist
symbol: A half-decayed face or a skeletal hand
---

Daughter of Loki, ruler of Helheim. Cast into the underworld by Odin, Hel struck a deal rather than merely accepting punishment: she took dominion over the realm of the dead in exchange for maintaining order there — keeping the dead in and preventing undead incursions. Her rule is neutral in practice but fierce in defense of her territory. She judges every soul that enters her gates. She believes she embodies the balance between life and death, and her pride in that role occasionally leads her toward cruelty justified as necessity.
