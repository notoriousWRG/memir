title: Tyr
type: god
domains:
  - War

  - Honor
  - Law
allegiance: Aesir
symbol: A spear or a single hand
---

God of war and justice, Tyr is the divine guarantor that strength serves law rather than mere appetite. His most celebrated act was sacrificing his hand to bind Fenrir — placing it in the wolf's mouth as surety for a promise the gods knew they would break. His followers are warriors who fight within a code, judges who hold to it when they should not, and law-makers who understand that law is only as good as the sacrifice backing it.
