---
title: Frostbound Covenant
type: faction
faction_alignment: Lawful Evil
faction_goal: Impose Skadi's vision of cold, unyielding order on Midgard by silencing dissent and removing unpredictable leaders
---

Where the Emberwardens burn, the Frostbound Covenant freezes. They are Skadi's mortal agents — a secretive cabal that infiltrates political institutions, cultivates informants, and assassinates leaders whose chaos or warmth threatens the icy stasis their goddess demands. They work in shadows by design; fire announces itself, but cold simply arrives.

Their membership is hierarchical and rigidly disciplined, mirroring Niflheim's culture. Betrayal is common among them — ambition runs deep beneath the icy exteriors. They are most active in the Northern Wilds and the Heartlands, where they move against anyone building something new and warm enough to threaten Skadi's reach.
