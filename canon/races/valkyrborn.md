The Valkyrborn are daughters of mortal fathers and celestial Valkyries, bearing a spark of divine purpose. Raised among mortals yet touched by destiny, they carry the burden of greatness and the sorrow of sacrifice. Most are female; rare male descendants — called Skaldborn — serve as soul-guides and lorekeepers. Valkyrborn live up to 300 years and stand six to seven feet tall. Those who prove themselves through deeds of valor and compassion may be called to the Trials of Ascension — and if they pass, they must choose between joining the Valkyrie in Asgard or remaining as eternal champions in the mortal realm. Not all are called. Not all ascend. But those who do are forever honored.
