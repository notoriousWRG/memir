The Fenirun carry the blood of Fenrir's curse — descendants of the mages who accidentally shattered the Great Wolf's chains during the Arcane Age and were marked by his power as consequence. They bear subtle wolf-like traits: sharp eyes, fangs, wiry muscle, and occasionally patches of fur during moments of intense emotion or rage. They are faster and more acutely perceptive than most, and can access something like a berserker's fury. But the bloodline also carries instability — the line between heightened emotion and losing control is thinner than they would like. They are often feared and misunderstood in society, the living reminder that the Arcane Age's hubris had consequences that lasted.
