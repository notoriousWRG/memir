The Jotunborn carry the blood of the frost giants of Jotunheim — large and imposing, with frost-kissed skin ranging from pale blue to icy white, and a presence that can chill the air around them. They mature similarly to humans but live longer, often reaching 150 years, and stand seven to eight feet tall. Their heritage is both power and burden: they are resistant to cold but vulnerable to fire, adapted for mountain altitudes but often viewed with wariness by smaller folk. Some warrior clans in the Shattered Vale claim Jotunborn descent, seeing the giant bloodline as a mark of endurance rather than shame.
