---
title: Draugr
type: race
lineage: Undead (returned dead)
homebrew: true
---

Draugar are the returned dead — not raised by necromancy but drawn back by will, vengeance, or divine command. Their skin is pale and corpse-like, their eyes alive with spectral energy. They do not need to breathe, eat, or drink, and require only four hours of motionless rest. They are considered Undead rather than Humanoid, which makes them effective against certain threats and unwelcome in certain places. Most Draugar who walk among the living are carrying something unfinished: a purpose not yet fulfilled, a wrong not yet righted, or simply the bewildering fact of a second chance they didn't ask for.
