Tiny, fierce, and Fey. The Wee Folk stand one to two feet tall and weigh around thirty to forty pounds, but their size has never once indicated their willingness to fight. They are creatures of extraordinary courage — or possibly extraordinary recklessness, depending on your perspective — and they treat improvised weapons as a natural extension of their worldview. Their culture values strength, loyalty, and a good brawl, often simultaneously. They speak Common and Sylvan, though most of their speech is peppered with dialect and slang thick enough to require translation. "Bigjobs" who underestimate the Wee Folk do so exactly once.
