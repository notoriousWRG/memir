The Great Wolf, son of Loki and firstborn of the age of chaos. Fenrir was bound twice — once by the gods in the primordial era, and again by mortal hands after arcanists accidentally shattered his divine fetters during the Arcane Age. The second binding, accomplished through Gleipnir Reborn, cost dozens of heroes their lives and reshaped the landscape of Midgard — the jagged mountains, flooded valleys, and broken plains of the Shattered Vale are all attributed to his rampage.

He lies now in the depths of the Shattered Vale. When storms tear across the land, the superstitious say it is Fenrir testing his chains. The prophecy is clear: the chains will break again, and this time no mortal hands will hold him.
