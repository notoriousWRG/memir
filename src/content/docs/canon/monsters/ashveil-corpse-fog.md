When the ash-fog comes off the river before nightfall at the Crossing, it sometimes carries company. The Corpse-Fog is not a creature in any conventional sense — it's a distributed presence, the accumulated weight of travelers who died at the bridge and weren't given proper rites.

It doesn't attack. It follows. It repeats the last thing each person it follows said before they died at the Crossing, in their own voice. If a traveler hears their own voice coming from the fog, they understand without being told what it means.

Dispelled by dawn. Immune to fire damage while over water.
