The Blood-Hound of Helheim, Garm is the monstrous guardian of the boundary between the living world and the dead. His howl is an omen of betrayal and the onset of battles; when mercenaries report hearing a hound that wasn't there the night before a massacre, they invoke his name. He is associated with the chaotic spilling of blood that breaks oaths and signals the fall of kingdoms.
