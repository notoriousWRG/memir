A construct built to guard a specific threshold — a door, a vault, a road marker — that has long since lost the thing it was guarding. The Hollow Warden still stands its post. It still challenges travelers. It has simply forgotten why, and so has developed its own answer.

The answer is usually wrong.

Hollow Wardens are not hostile by default. They are procedurally correct. They will let you pass if you answer the challenge. The challenge may be a riddle, a password, a proof of identity, or something it invented to fill the void. You won't know which until you're standing in front of it.
