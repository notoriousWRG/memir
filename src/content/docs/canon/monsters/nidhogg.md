The Devouring Dragon, Nidhogg gnaws ceaselessly at the roots of Yggdrasil from below. His hunger is theological as much as literal — every bite is an act of spite against the gods who built the world from Ymir's corpse. The duergar revere him as a dark patron deity, seeing their own merciless exploitation of Svartalfheim's depths as devotion to his hunger. Shrines of a dragon gnawing at roots mark the deepest corners of the dwarven underrealm.
