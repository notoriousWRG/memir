The World Serpent, cast into the oceans of Midgard after the divine war that ended the Age of Discord. Jormungandr encircles the world beneath the waves, his body long enough to swallow the horizon. Every storm is attributed to his thrashing tail; coastal peoples cast offerings into the sea to keep him quiescent. The prophecy is fixed: he will rise to bring about the world's end, and Thor will slay him — but the god of thunder will take nine steps before falling dead from the serpent's venom.
