title: The Age of Discord
type: history-entry
era: The Age of Discord
period: War of the Gods
---

The age that ended divine unity. Loki, long an ally of Odin, had been secretly fathering children of immense power. Odin's prophetic vision showed those children ending the world. The confrontation that followed became war: Loki's children — Jormungandr, Fenrir, Hel, Skadi, Váli — revealed themselves and joined the battle, causing destruction across the realms.

After prolonged conflict, the forces of Asgard prevailed. Loki was bound in dwarven chains. His children were scattered: Jormungandr cast into Midgard's oceans, Fenrir bound in an isolated wilderness, Hel sent to rule the underworld, Skadi exiled to Niflheim, Váli condemned to shadow. The divine war left lasting scars — on the gods, on mortal civilization, and on the prophecies that now govern the future.
