---
title: The Age of Harmony
type: history-entry
era: The Age of Harmony
period: Age of the Gods
---

A golden age in which the gods worked in concert to guide mortals and strengthen the realms. Asgard was completed, the divine order established, and magic — Ymir's Breath — was gifted to mortals. The Bifröst was built, linking Asgard to Midgard and extending the gods' reach across creation. Mortal civilizations flourished under divine patronage. It is the age all subsequent eras are measured against: what was lost when it ended, and whether it can ever be recovered.
