title: The Primordial Age
type: history-entry
era: The Primordial Age
period: Age of Creation
---

Before time was measured, the universe was Ginnungagap — a yawning abyss of infinite darkness and potential. From it emerged two opposing forces: Niflheim, realm of ice and mist, and Muspelheim, realm of fire. Where they met, ice melted into the form of Ymir, the first giant, nourished by the cosmic cow Audhumla. From the salty ice she licked emerged Buri, first of the gods.

Buri's descendants — Odin, Vili, and Ve — rose as the champions of order. They slew Ymir in a titanic battle and forged the world from what remained: his flesh became the earth of Midgard, his bones the mountains, his blood the seas, his skull the sky. From tree trunks they fashioned the first humans, Ask and Embla, and set them in Midgard under Yggdrasil's roots. The World Tree itself grew from the residual energy of fire, frost, and the gods' first acts, branching into every realm and becoming the cosmic connective tissue of all that exists.
