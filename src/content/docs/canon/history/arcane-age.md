---
title: The Arcane Age
type: history-entry
era: The Arcane Age
period: Age of Enlightenment
---

With the gods withdrawn after the war, mortals began to harness Ymir's Breath on their own terms. Magical academies rose. Ancient runes were rediscovered. Powerful artifacts were forged in Svartalfheim. Towers of magic pierced the skies of Midgard. Mortal civilization reached heights that, at their peak, rivaled the divine.

The age ended in hubris. A coven of arcanists found ancient runes capable of unmaking the gods' bindings — and used them. Fenrir's chains shattered. The Age of Arcanum collapsed into the Age of Ruin.
