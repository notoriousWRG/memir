title: Emberwardens
type: faction
faction_alignment: Chaotic Evil
faction_goal: Spread Surtur's doctrine of cleansing fire across Midgard and prepare the ground for Muspelheim's conquest
---

The Emberwardens are Surtur's mortal cult — berserkers, fire mages, and zealots who see fire as freedom and Skadi's icy order as the ultimate oppression. They set forests and cities alight not out of pure malice but out of genuine theological conviction: destruction is the precondition of renewal, and stagnation is the only true death.

Their ranks are filled with people who believe, deeply and dangerously, that what they're doing is right. They revere Surtur as the god who will burn the world clean. When the Emberwardens arrive, they don't sneak. They carry torches.
