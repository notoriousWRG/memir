---
title: The Circle
type: faction
faction_alignment: Chaotic Evil
faction_goal: Hasten Ragnarok by empowering Loki's children and securing dominion in the new world order
---

The Circle was founded by individuals who discovered ancient prophecies foretelling Ragnarok and interpreted them not as a warning but as a mandate. Their symbol is a serpent encircling the world — Jormungandr himself.

Their structure is hierarchical and secretive. The Inner Circle interprets prophecy and directs action. Membership is granted only to the most loyal and devoted. The organization conducts dark rituals, pursues artifact retrieval (particularly items infused with divine power), and infiltrates political, religious, and magical institutions to sow discord. They have operated human trafficking out of at least one known location — a smuggling front run through a farming coalition in Edook.

Key members include Viktor Helstrom (the Grand Serpent, their master strategist), Elara Frostweaver (High Priestess, ritual conductor), and Thane Bloodthorn (Enforcer, who removes obstacles by force). The Circle believes that when Loki and his children reshape the world, those who served will rule alongside them.
