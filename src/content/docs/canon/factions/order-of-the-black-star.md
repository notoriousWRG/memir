---
title: Order of the Black Star
type: faction
faction_alignment: Neutral Evil
faction_goal: Acquire power through magic, eliminate opposition, and exploit ancient and forbidden lore for personal dominion
---

A secretive brotherhood of evil wizards united not by any divine agenda but by the simpler desire to take what they want and destroy what opposes them. The Order of the Black Star conducts dangerous experiments, pursues forbidden lore, and places agents in many institutions across Ældor. Their primary antagonist is the Silver Lantern Club — a faction of good-aligned spellcasters and adventurers who specifically oppose their schemes.

Unlike the Circle or the Frostbound Covenant, the Order has no eschatological ambitions. They are not trying to end or reshape the world. They are trying to own as much of it as possible before anyone notices.
