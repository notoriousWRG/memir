title: Loki
type: god
domains:
  - Chaos
  - Deception
  - Trickery
  - Darkness
allegiance: Antagonist
symbol: A twisted serpent or a shattered mask
---

God of chaos and deception, Loki began as a chaotic ally to Odin before his hidden children and mounting deceptions brought the Age of Harmony to an end. His war against Asgard was ultimately unsuccessful — he was bound in unbreakable chains forged by the dwarves, his children scattered to the far reaches of the realms. He remains imprisoned. The Circle works toward his release. Prophecy holds that he will escape, and that when he does, it will not go well for anyone.
