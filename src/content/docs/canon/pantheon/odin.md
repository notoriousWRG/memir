The All-Father, first among the gods of Asgard. Odin shaped the world from Ymir's body alongside Vili and Ve, fashioned the first humans, and built the divine order that the Age of Harmony rested upon. His ravens, Huginn and Muninn — Thought and Memory — fly across the realms and return to tell him what they have seen. His followers are warriors, scholars, and seers. It was Odin's prophetic vision of Loki's children ending the world that sparked the divine war that closed the Age of Harmony.
