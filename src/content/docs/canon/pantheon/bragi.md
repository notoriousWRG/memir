---
title: Bragi
type: god
domains:
  - Poetry
  - Music
  - Eloquence
  - Storytelling
allegiance: Aesir
symbol: A harp or a quill
---

God of poetry, music, and eloquent speech, Bragi is the divine patron of bards and storytellers. He is the keeper of the tales that hold the ages together — the Song of Creation, the legends of Fenrir, the cautionary myth of Loki's betrayal. Without Bragi's followers, the history of Ældor would exist only as ruins. His followers are bards, poets, and storytellers who understand that memory is a form of power.
