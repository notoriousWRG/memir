---

King of Fire and ruler of Muspelheim, Surtur is the embodiment of fire's dual nature — relentless destruction paired with the promise of renewal. He carries Dawnbreaker, a flaming greatsword that burns with the fires of creation and destruction alike. Unlike Skadi, who craves control, Surtur revels in unbridled freedom and encourages his followers to embrace their passions without restraint. His ambitions extend to conquering Midgard, with the Emberwardens carrying his doctrine of cleansing fire into mortal lands.
