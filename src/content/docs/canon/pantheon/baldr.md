God of light and purity, Baldr is beloved among the gods and mortals alike. He represents the possibility of goodness persisting in a world that seems designed to extinguish it. His followers — healers, poets, those seeking forgiveness — come to him with hope. The legend of his death and eventual rebirth is one of Ældor's most enduring myths.
