---
title: Freyja
type: god
domains:
  - Love
  - Beauty
  - Fertility
  - War
  - Magic
allegiance: Vanir
symbol: A necklace (Brísingamen), a chariot pulled by cats
---

Freyja is a goddess of contradictions held in perfect tension: love and war, beauty and magic. She is the most widely worshipped of the Vanir among mortals, sought by lovers and warriors and practitioners of seiðr alike. The Verdant Glades are said to bear her blessing alongside Frey's. Her followers are lovers, warriors, and magicians.
