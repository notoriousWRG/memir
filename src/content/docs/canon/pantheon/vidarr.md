---
title: Víðarr
type: god
domains:
  - Forests
  - Vengeance
  - Silence
  - Survival
allegiance: Aesir
symbol: A boot or a wolf
---

Son of Odin, Víðarr is a god of few words and steady purpose. He dwells in deep forests and rarely acts — but when he does, the act is final. His followers are rangers, avengers, and survivalists who understand that the most dangerous force is one that waits.
