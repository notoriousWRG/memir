---
title: Váli
type: god
domains:
  - Discord
  - Madness
  - Nightmares
  - Fear
allegiance: Antagonist
symbol: A broken mirror or a fractured mask
---

Born from Loki's chaotic essence, Váli thrives on discord and madness. Banished to a shadowy realm after the divine war, he spreads fear and nightmares from exile, manipulating mortals to sow chaos. When someone acts without reason — when betrayal has no motive, when violence has no aim — Váli's name is invoked as both explanation and warning.
