An untamed expanse of dense forests and snow-covered tundra where Skadi's influence is felt in every killing cold. The people of the Northern Wilds organize in tight-knit clans with strong traditions of storytelling and ancestral reverence. They leave offerings for Skadi not out of worship but out of negotiation — appease her and survive the winter; fail and the clan loses members to the cold.

Their warriors are known across Midgard for ferocity born not of aggression but of a lifetime spent in a landscape that kills the careless.
