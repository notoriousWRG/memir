The Heartlands was once the center of the Arcane Age's grand civilization. Towers of magic stood here. The ruins are still everywhere — crumbling spires, smashed libraries, strange formations where old enchantments have been overgrown by a century of farmland. Small towns and villages have grown up in the rubble, and the Heartlanders have become pragmatic farmers and cautious scholars who believe in progress as strongly as they believe in not repeating what progress did last time.

Festivals celebrating harvests and learning are common. The scholars here know what the Arcane Age was and what it cost.
