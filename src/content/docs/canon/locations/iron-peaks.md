A range of towering metallic-colored mountains that gleam under sunlight, the Iron Peaks sit above some of the richest ore veins in Midgard and above the entrance points to Svartalfheim's Underway network. Dwarven influence is strong here — the human miners and smiths of the peaks have absorbed dwarven traditions over generations of trade, creating a shared culture of craftsmanship and artistry that values what the hands can make above bloodline or origin.

The paths through the peaks are narrow and treacherous, and the predatory mountain creatures don't distinguish between traveler and settler.
