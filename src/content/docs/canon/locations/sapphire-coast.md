Coastal cliffs, sandy beaches, and turquoise waters punctuated by fishing villages and bustling trade hubs. The Sapphire Coast's people are seafarers, fishermen, and merchants — pragmatic worshippers of Njord who know that the sea feeds them and can also kill them. Ship blessings are performed at every departure and arrival, and offerings are cast into the water regularly.

When the seas rage, the coast folk don't blame weather. They blame the serpent's tail.
