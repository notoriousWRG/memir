A lush and enchanted forest said to be blessed by Frey and Freyja, filled with ancient trees, shimmering rivers, and magical flora. The inhabitants are deeply connected to nature — druidic traditions, sustainable living, and a strong oral culture that passes down the myths of divine blessing through story rather than text. The Verdant Glades does not welcome exploitation. It does welcome those willing to listen.
