A volcanic wasteland where Muspelheim's influence bleeds into Midgard — rivers of lava, craggy terrain, blackened skies. Only nomadic groups traverse it regularly, trading the rare volcanic minerals the place produces. Those nomads are considered near-mythic guides by anyone planning to cross Ashenhold for any reason; they know which paths are currently passable and which have become lava since last week.

The Emberwardens are most active here, viewing the volcanic landscape as a sacred preview of what all of Midgard will become.
