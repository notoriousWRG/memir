A tightly-scoped one-shot about loyalties, leverage, and what people do when a quiet arrangement stops being quiet.

Three NPCs, two locations, one decision point that branches the ending. The garrison commander is a threat but never appears directly. Everything is intermediaries and pressure.

**Tone:** Political and personal. Not a dungeon crawl. The danger is paperwork that ends lives.
