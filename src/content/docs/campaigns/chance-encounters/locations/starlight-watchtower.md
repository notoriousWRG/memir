An ancient observatory built by the Astral Seekers, a society dedicated to harnessing the power of stars and celestial events. They believed starlight held pure, primordial magic capable of revealing the secrets of fate, time, and reality. Their downfall came when they attempted to harness the power of a falling star — the experiment resulted in a catastrophic release of unstable energy, creating a permanent magical anomaly around the observatory.

The anomaly distorts time, space, and memory, making the site unpredictable and dangerous. Time loops, memory echoes, and gravitational distortions make it nearly impossible for ordinary adventurers to explore safely. The remnants of the Astral Seekers' magic still protect the site through arcane constructs, star-forged guardians, and illusionary wards.

## Connection to the party

[[lady-grimhook]] contracted [[artcoth]] to retrieve the [[pendant-of-stars]] from this location. She considers the party both capable and expendable — a calculated risk. Her personal connection to the Watchtower runs deeper than the contract implies (DC 16 Insight: quiet grief behind the sharpness; DC 18 Arcana: she was there when the Pendant was lost).

Grimhook's interest in the Watchtower extends beyond the Pendant. The observatory may be a nexus of astral or starlight magic — an attempt to pierce the veil and open direct communion with Niflheim or frozen stars.
