Half-Orc paladin. Searching for lineage and divine purpose. Has a connection to [[baldr]], the Norse god of light and purity — possibly descended from divine heritage — but is not yet aware of its full weight.

## Plot threads

**The Baldr thread.** The divine connection is established but unexplored. Shah doesn't yet know the depth of what it means for his path.

**The charm incident.** During the Frostveil arc, Shah was charmed by a war-mage and turned on [[coriac]] before breaking free. He broke it himself. That moment — the control, the rupture, the recovery — lives in the party's shared history.

**Living ladder.** Pattern of physical rescue: catches himself on rigging, uses rope to haul Grimvald back from overboard during the landfall grounding. Lit a torch as a secondary beacon in the fog. Quiet heroism, no announcement. That's Shah Doh.
