Warlock who believes his patron is Odin. It is not. His patron is Feanoro, a corrupted seer in service of [[nidhogg]]. That discrepancy is kept from the party and is a live source of inner conflict. Wields a soul-drinking sword that captures and sacrifices souls for power. Secretly performed a Severance Ritual, sacrificing a soul from an enchanted orb.

## Plot threads

**The false patron.** Gareth believes he's chosen by Odin. The statue he and the party encountered in the Underdark temple bore a resemblance to him — a moment that seeded a question neither he nor the party has fully answered. The real patron's agenda is unknown.

**The anchor.** In the party's running dynamic, Gareth was in charge of the anchor. The anchor is gone. That's where the ship sits right now.

**Invisibility pair.** Casus made him invisible before the draugr fight. He has a lit sword and is currently unseen — breaking that invisibility will register as a flash of light in the fog, brief and significant.

**Artcoth connection.** Travels with [[artcoth]]; the nature of that relationship (allies, co-conspirators) is part of both their histories.
