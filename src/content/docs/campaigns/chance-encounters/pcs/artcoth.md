## Plot threads

**The gloves.** Found a pair of magic gloves in the beach wreckage during the landfall chaos. Mechanics TBD. The migration plan flags: *the gloves should be usable in the same session Artcoth found them.*

**Venn and Artcoth.** Artcoth had friction with [[elara-venn]] earlier in the arc, then earned credit with a clutch save involving her. She's watching. One more public misstep shifts crew loyalty.

**Coordinator.** Instinct under pressure: call out positions, make tactical sense of chaos. First thing he did when the ship hit the shallows was start directing.
