## Arc 1: The Frostveil

The party formed in the Underdark, surviving the Iron Spine's duergar politics and the labyrinthine dark. Their defining act: a pact with Shalindra the aboleth, trading a debt for passage. Casus joined mid-arc in the duergar city library. They surfaced changed — bonded by the particular trust that comes from owing each other their lives underground.

## Arc 2: The Storm That Remembers

A deliberate, intelligent storm gathered the White Gull and drew the party toward Ariel's island — center of an old conflict between a bound storm-spirit and the former shipmate who bound her. The party sailed out of Ravencrest, navigated the storm crossing, and landed hard without an anchor. The arc is ongoing.
