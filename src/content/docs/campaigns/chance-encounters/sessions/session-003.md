*Arc 1 session numbering PROVISIONAL.*

The tunnels open into the underground lake beneath [[duergar-city|the duergar city]]. The party is stranded on a stone island in the middle of dark water — walls too distant to swim to, the water too black to see into.

[[shalindra-aboleth]] surfaces.

She calls herself the King-slayer. She holds the signet ring of the ancient dwarven monarch on her fang as a trophy — the same monarch whose city the duergar now occupy. Her communication is entirely telepathic: a deep resonance described as sending shivers down the spine. The duergar above are *"vermin who dared to skirt the edge of my dominion, too frightened to submit to my influence."* She has inhabited this lake long enough that contempt has calcified into something close to boredom.

The offer comes framed as a gift. Safe passage through the underwater passage into the duergar city, in exchange for the party clearing the infestation above. [[coriac]] negotiates with flattery. Shalindra grants the pact as a condescending mercy.

She demonstrates what leverage looks like: she commands a gaunt, hollow-eyed duergar thrall to slit his own throat. The point is made without ambiguity.

She hands over the dwarven signet ring — the key to the underwater passage. The surviving thrall is assigned as a guide. *"Do not disappoint me."*

The pact is entered.
