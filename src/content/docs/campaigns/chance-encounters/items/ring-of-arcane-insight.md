One of the six [[liriel-baenre]] Frostveil artifacts. Currently held by [[lady-grimhook]], taken from Eamon the Seer. Grimhook uses it to detect intent to betray in those she deals with.

[[casus]]'s origin quest centers on this item — his connection to Liriel's legacy runs through it.

## Powers (when attuned)

- Advantage on Arcana checks; *Comprehend Languages* always active
- Spell save DC +1; regain one expended spell slot (3rd level or lower) after a long rest
- Cast *Legend Lore* once per week

## Corruption

- **Stage 1:** Nightmares reveal forbidden truths; 1 level of exhaustion on a failed save after rest
- **Stage 2:** Attunee becomes obsessed with dangerous magical research
- **Stage 3:** Compulsively casts risky or forbidden spells — may trigger corruption events
