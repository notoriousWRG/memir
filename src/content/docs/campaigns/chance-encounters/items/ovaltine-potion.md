An experimental latent-magic potion brewed by [[ovaltine-jenkins]]. Currently held by [[casus]].

On a high Arcana check (DC 25), the potion is understood to trigger what's already magically present — it doesn't add power, it amplifies or reveals ambient magic in the environment. On [[ariels-island]], the island is saturated with [[ariel]]'s presence; using it here could react to her specifically, giving Casus a vision, a physical sensation, or calling something's attention to him.

The 05-13 plan notes this as a held threat: *"Reacts to latent magical fields. The island is saturated with Ariel's presence. Using it here could have significant and personalized effects."*

One dose. Irreversible once consumed.
