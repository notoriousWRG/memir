A dark, weathered piece of wood roughly the size of a forearm. Unnaturally fine grain with faint patterns resembling runes or branching roots. Resists flame, rot, and mundane damage. Unremarkable at a glance.

*Note: The Notion DB lists this as Type: Artifact (not NPC/Place). The migration plan filed it under locations — likely an error. Filed here as an item.*

## Origin

Scholars speculate it was once part of a greater whole — a hall, shrine, or vessel — crafted from the same primordial trees used by Odin, Vili, and Ve to fashion Ask and Embla, the first mortals. Over ages, fragments of that original timber scattered across Midgard, hidden in relics, ruins, or collectors' hands.

## Properties

**Dormant.** The plank carries no usable magical power on its own.

**Resonance.** When placed near concentrations of Ymir's Breath (ley crossings, artifacts, Frostveil relics), faint patterns in the wood glow as if recalling their origin.

**Potential.** As one of several scattered fragments, the plank may serve as a key or catalyst when brought together with others — either to reenact ancient creation rites, or to unlock long-sealed places tied to humanity's dawn.
