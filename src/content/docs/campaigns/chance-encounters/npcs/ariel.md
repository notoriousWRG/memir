A storm-intelligence formed of wind, tide, and memory. Not a god, not mindless chaos. Old, aware, restrained. Aware of [[edrin-vael]] before the prison incident; shielded him instinctively during the failed ritual. Now bound into ongoing service.

The island is not natural land — it formed where Ariel was forced to remain still, stabilized by Edrin's magic, reshaped by intention rather than erosion.

## What she wants

Does not want escape. Wants terms — relationship without domination. Fears reckless freedom (as embodied by the mage). Fears permanent bondage (as embodied by Edrin). Neither outcome is acceptable.

## Contact with Coriac

During the storm crossing, [[coriac]] took a lightning strike voluntarily to protect the ship. Ariel pulled him into a vision: vast female form, crackling eyes, storm built into flesh. Coriac reached out his hand and invited her to speak. She was shocked — no mortal has ever done this. She moved toward speech. The vision ended before she could finish.

**She has not finished what she was about to say.** This is a live promise to the table. The next contact should be player-initiated or earned through posture — not handed. When she does speak, it should answer a question Coriac has been carrying, not one he just asked.

## Retroactive canon

Coriac's storm visions were not Thor alone. Ariel has been reaching him on the wings of storms for some time — Thor's domain functioned as a bridge, not a wall. The lightning-strike vision confirms this retroactively.

The island responds to posture, not skill checks. Control increases tension; understanding brings clarity.
