Master enchanter from a noble lineage of magical scholars. A prodigy who surpassed her peers and turned to manipulation and darker paths in pursuit of power. Loyal and influential member of the Order of the Black Star. Dangerous ally; treacherous enemy.

## Motivation

Grimhook is attempting to harness the [[liriel-baenre]] Frostveil artifacts as conduits to Niflheim's essence. She considers Liriel's use of soulbinding magic crude and chaotic — she wants to perfect it, binding souls with surgical precision instead of emotional fervor. Her ideal future mirrors Niflheim: lawful control, static order, no room for chaos.

The Order of the Black Star does not share her ambitions; they view the Frostveil artifacts as fuel or toys, not world-reshaping instruments. Their contempt for her leads them to send agents after her or the party more to annoy and punish than to win anything strategic.

## Connection to the party

Contracted [[artcoth]] (primary contact) to retrieve the Pendant of Stars from the Starlight Watchtower. Contract is enforced with a soulbinding sigil. Reward: 2,000 gp, a vault item, or a favor — each player chose on completion.

Her Ring of Arcane Insight detects intent to betray. DC 12 Insight reveals she's personally connected to the Watchtower; DC 16 reveals quiet grief behind the sharpness; DC 18 Arcana reveals she was there when the Pendant was lost.

## Secrets

Possesses the Cloak of Starry Veil (Frostveil artifact). The Ring of Arcane Insight she wields was taken from Eamon the Seer. Her connection to the Watchtower is not purely strategic — there is grief in it.

A serpent tattoo coils around her ring finger, described as "old loyalties, like old tattoos, fade — but never disappear."
