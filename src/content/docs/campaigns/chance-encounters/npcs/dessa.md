Tall, deliberate, sun-weathered. Keeps her hands occupied — tying knots, unwinding them, retying. It's not nervousness; it's how she thinks. When she's about to change the subject, she finishes the knot she's working on first.

Knew [[coriac]] years ago — not as a peer, but as a constant. The kind of person who was always there, reliable, never remarkable until you needed remarkable. Warm with him in the way of someone who has thought about this reunion and decided not to perform it.

## On the island

Leads a small group of survivors who have been here long enough to stop being scared and start being organized. Watched the draugr fight from the treeline and did not intervene — her reasoning: "We've learned not to spend ourselves on the beach. The beach takes everyone eventually. What matters is who's left standing when it's done."

She wants to go inland and find what the island is holding. She believes something can be done about [[edrin-vael]] and has been waiting for someone with actual reach.

## What she won't say yet

She thinks the captain of their previous ship made a choice that got people killed. She's not ready to blame anyone by name. Any direct question about Edrin gets redirected — she gives real information about the storm patterns and draugr behavior instead. A second conversation, or a moment of real honesty from the party, unlocks the rest.
