Dark shaman, master of blood magic and soul manipulation. Once the trusted advisor to [[liriel-baenre]]. Co-developed the Frostveil artifacts. Specialized in soulbinding rituals, blood sacrifice, and corrupted elemental spirits.

## History

Originally a surface-integrated duergar, banished after attempting to usurp a surface king. Returned beneath the city years later, enacting hauntings, abductions, and mind control. Eventually led a devastating attack that collapsed the city's defenses and forced an evacuation. The king and his royal guard stayed behind — fate unknown.

Fled to a stronghold in the Iron Spine after [[liriel-baenre]]'s fall. Poisoned the surrounding land. Fought a final battle against a group of adventurers; collapsed the mines in a final act of defiance, killing at least one hero. **Presumed dead — no body was ever recovered.**

## Casus's thread

[[casus]] entered the Iron Spine seeking Zal'goroth's stronghold, looking for clues to the location of the Ring of Arcane Insight. That search led him into the duergar city and eventually to the party.

## Legacy

Some believe Zal'goroth's spirit still dwells in the ruins beneath the Iron Spine. Others whisper that his mind lives on in soul jars, or that his body was bound into a skeletal form during his final ritual. His name is still invoked by fringe cultists and deepfolk who follow "the Old Ways."
