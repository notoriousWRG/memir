Haunted by a past decision: pressed forward in a storm, lost a crew member. That event shaped his governing principle: *"If the sea resists, I yield."* His condition for the voyage to Ariel's island was explicit — if the wind turns them away, they turn back. Responds to honesty; shuts down to manipulation.

[[artcoth]] returned a stolen letter to him. That act built the party's most honest relationship with him. The captain suspects who took it but never confirmed.

## Grimvald's Suggestion

[[grimvald]] cast Suggestion on the captain, and the captain pressed on against his own stated condition as a result. This has not been acknowledged at the table. The captain does not know magic was involved. If he surfaces on the island and pieces it together — or if someone tells him — the fallout will be severe.

## Current status

Missing since the grounding of the White Gull. No body found. The island has ways of holding people.

Tom Rell (young deckhand) is known to ask "Is this like before?" when a storm builds — a line that hits the captain hard.
