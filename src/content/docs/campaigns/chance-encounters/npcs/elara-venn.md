First Mate of the White Gull. Crew loyalty proxy — she watches how the party treats the captain and acts accordingly. Her trust is hard-won and easily lost.

## History with Artcoth

Early friction with [[artcoth]] over his conduct, then earned credit when he made a clutch save involving her. Her posture softened after that. One more public misstep shifts crew loyalty.

## Current status

Returned to the White Gull with [[wrenn]] after the party made shore, to assess hull damage and prevent further harm to the crew still aboard. Off-map for now. Loyal, competent, watching how this ends for the captain.
