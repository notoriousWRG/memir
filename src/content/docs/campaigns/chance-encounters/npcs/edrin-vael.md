Former shipmate of the party. Coriac's closest conversational and spiritual peer before the break. Believed storms could be reasoned with, not merely endured.

## The break

Chose to work with a dark, nature-based mage despite warnings. Their combined rituals began endangering innocent people in a nearby town. The party intervened and captured both Edrin and the mage. Both were imprisoned together. The mage was later found dead in the cell. Official narrative was unclear; suspicion lingered. Edrin escaped during the aftermath.

**DM truth:** The mage attempted a dangerous binding ritual in the cell. It catastrophically failed; the mage died to magical backlash. Edrin survived due to partial protection from [[ariel]], a storm-entity already aware of him.

## Now

Replaced experimentation with control. Binds Ariel out of fear of ungoverned power — sees himself as responsible, not cruel. Believes restraint caused the tragedy, not ambition.

He does not yet know that Ariel reached [[coriac]] voluntarily. When he learns this, it will destabilize his entire justification.

He assumes Coriac will understand the necessity of control. He does not regret choosing the mage's certainty over Coriac's faith.
