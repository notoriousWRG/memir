## Premise

[The Tempest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tempest), reskinned in Norse myth.

[[edrin-vael|Edrin Vael]] is **Prospero**: a storm-mage, once Coriac's closest peer, who believed storms could be reasoned with — then watched ambition kill people, and replaced experimentation with *control*. Marooned on an island that is itself a bound storm-spirit, [[ariel|Ariel]] — wind, tide, and memory — he holds her in service out of fear of ungoverned power. Around them drift the crews his tempest dragged in: the watching draugr, the survivors of Group A, the vanished crew of Group B, the missing captain of the White Gull.

It is "an isle full of noises, sounds and sweet airs, that give delight and hurt not" — and also of grief, bondage, denial, and a reckoning that has been waiting for someone with reach to arrive. The party arrived.

---

## Tone — One Island, Three Strings

**The problem this fixes:** the arc had been playing one note — dread. Everything listened, dissolved, grieved, or controlled, all in the same minor key. *The Tempest* is a **tragicomedy**; it holds weight *and* chaos *and* wonder at once. So does this island.

Three strings, always present, **modulated — never hard-cut:**

- **Weight** — the dark spine. Bondage, grief, control, cost. Kept whole. This is the undertow under everything.
- **Mischief** — absurd conspiracies, drunken schemes, the island's pranks and misdirections, and the chaos the *players* cause. The release valve.
- **Wonder** — beauty that delights and (at first) doesn't hurt: invisible music, a phantom feast, a masque of figures in the fog.

**The anti-whiplash rule (read this before every session):** every scene has a *dominant* string but carries a *trace* of the others. Comedy here always has an undertow; dread always has a wry edge; wonder warms or curdles, but only when **motivated** — the island reflects the party's posture (control breeds tension, understanding breeds clarity; see [[ariels-island]]), it never jump-cuts to a scare for shock value. Transitions are bridged in the fiction, not slammed.

**What holds the variety together** is the relational throughline (see §The Spine). Tone can range widely as long as *who these people are to each other* stays the constant. That's what keeps it one story instead of mood swings.

## The Island as Stage

The isle is two things at once: "full of noises… that give delight and hurt not," and a prison that holds people. Both are true in every region. Keyed to the sub-locations already defined in [[ariels-island]] — each tagged with the string it leans into:

- **The beach** — *Weight.* Black sand, wrecks, the watching draugr offshore. The sour win. (Behind the party now.)
- **Group A's camp** — *Weight / relational.* [[dessa]], [[marek]], [[ollo]]. The honest conversation that hasn't happened yet.
- **The Spar Field** — *Wonder → unease.* Standing spars that sing; moss pointing inland. Opens as beauty, tightens into a puzzle and a threshold.
- **The Hollowed Hull** — *Mischief, sad undertow.* Group B's last camp. The absurd court *and* the dread payoff share this ground.
- **The Circled Zone** — *Weight.* The fog won't enter; the stone frame; Ariel bound "the way a river exists in its banks." The reckoning. Don't let the party cross the threshold prematurely.
- **The Lone Captain's Position** — *Relational.* Elevated, exposed. If a captain is on the isle, he's here.

## The Spine — weight that must land

These are the load-bearing beats. Tone can play freely *around* them; these don't get fudged.

- **Ariel's terms.** She does not want escape — she wants relationship without domination, and fears both reckless freedom (the dead mage) and permanent bondage (Edrin). Her unfinished sentence to [[coriac]] is a live promise to the table: earned through posture, never handed.
- **Edrin's destabilization.** He assumes Coriac will understand the necessity of control. He does *not* know Ariel reached Coriac **voluntarily.** When he learns it, his entire justification cracks. ([[the-binding-thread]] is the thematic key here — an oath that *witnesses* but does not *enforce*, the opposite of what Edrin built.)
- **Grimvald's Suggestion.** The captain pressed on against his own stated condition because [[grimvald]] charmed him. Unacknowledged at the table. A powder keg if the captain surfaces and pieces it together.
- **Relational stakes (in place of romance).** The emotional tension is *relational*, not romantic:
  - **Coriac and his past** — Dessa knew him "as a constant"; Edrin was his spiritual peer before the break; the captain(s) at the treeline. What does Coriac owe the people he left behind, and the ones he's about to find?
  - **The party as a forming family** — who they protect, who they spend, who they become when the island tests their posture. The island reads this and responds to it.
- **The cost of *how* they leave.** Not whether — how. The anchor is gone; leaving requires a solution they don't have. The *manner* of their departure (did they dominate the island or come to terms with it?) is the ledger entry this arc is building toward.

## The Chaos — the fun

The new material. All of it carries an undertow, none of it whiplashes.

- **Split Group B.** The crew that walked inland and vanished resolves *both* ways, in the same place (the Hollowed Hull), bridged by the survivors' denial so the tonal shift is motivated:
  - **The clowns** — a handful survived and went gloriously native. Drunk on something the island fermented, they've crowned themselves and are plotting to "overthrow" the isle's true power (Edrin) — a doomed Stephano/Trinculo conspiracy. Played for real laughs. The undertow: the delusion is how they survived the thing that took the others.
  - **The transformed** — the rest didn't make it, or didn't stay people. The dread payoff. The clowns won't look directly at what happened to their friends; the party will.
- **Puzzles.**
  - **The singing spars** — a sound puzzle built on the Spar Field's existing tones. Read the pattern, play it back, and the moss-direction resolves into a true path inland.
  - **A riddle-gate** — reuse [[hollow-warden]] (the construct that forgot what it guards and *invented its own challenge*). Edrin set it on a threshold long ago; it no longer remembers why. Comic, tragic, and a genuine puzzle — its challenge may be a riddle, a password, or something it made up.
  - **The binding** at the Circled Zone — a memory/oath puzzle, not a fight. How Ariel is held, and what "terms" would actually look like.
- **Wonder set-pieces.** A phantom banquet or a masque in the fog — figures, music, a feast that delights, staged by Ariel (willingly or under Edrin's hand). Beautiful first. It twists only if the party's posture earns the twist (the vanishing banquet of Act 3).
- **Monsters.** Norse skins by default (per the `session-prep` theme guardrails) — draugr, storm-touched things, the transformed crew. Homebrew mechanics tied to storm, memory, and binding are welcome and flagged with one clean table-ready rule.

## Suggested Flow — a modulating spine

*A path, not a rail* — the same scaffolding `session-prep` uses. Keep the geography (Spar Field → Hollowed Hull → Circled Zone) but re-tone it so the night **breathes** instead of descending monotonically into dread. **Edit this freely.**

1. **Threshold** *(Wonder → unease).* The Spar Field opens as delight — the isle singing. The sound puzzle. Then the [[hollow-warden]] riddle-gate barring the way deeper: funny, then unsettling, then a real lock to solve.
2. **The Absurd Court** *(Mischief, sad undertow).* Stumble into the Group-B clowns' ridiculous kingdom and their doomed conspiracy — the comedy peak. In the same ground, the evidence of the transformed crew the clowns won't acknowledge — the dread, bridged by their denial.
3. **The Isle's Wonder** *(Wonder, then motivated twist).* A phantom banquet or masque. Coriac's relational stakes surface here — the old crew, a captain glimpsed, a sentence almost finished. Beauty that means something, that can curdle if pushed.
4. **The Reckoning** *(Weight, chaos crashing in).* The Circled Zone — Ariel, Edrin, the moral climax. Don't keep the strings in separate scenes here: let the clowns' doomed plot **collide into** the reckoning so chaos and weight fuse, the way *The Tempest* gathers every thread into Act 5. The cost of *how* they leave is decided in this room.
