---
title: Campaigns
description: Stories activated by a specific party.
---

A campaign is a story run by a specific party. It holds sessions, PC records, the consequence ledger, and any divergences from the underlying story or canon. The most mutable layer.

Overrides record campaign-specific changes to canon entities without touching canon itself.
