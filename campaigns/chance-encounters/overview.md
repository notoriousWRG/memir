## Arc 1: The Frostveil (Sessions 1–12)

The party left Stormwreck Island — Calendula's player had said farewell and the cloister arc was closed — and reoriented toward Edook and Ravencrest. Runara gave each member a pearl for the road.

Back on the surface, they were hired by a Circle operative named Cole (real name: Nicholas) to retrieve a ruby ring from Lady Grimhook's manor in Ravencrest — inscribed inside with the name *Liriel, "song of the Night."* Jimmy Quickhands, an old associate of Artcoth's, introduced them to the job and laid out the approaches. The gala at Lord Thorne's estate became the window.

At the gala, Thorne used the Rod of Rulership to publicly humiliate Grimhook before his guests — he made her kneel and recite words of admiration she didn't mean. The party attempted to heist the rod during the chaos that followed. They failed: the animated armor in the gallery put Gareth down and the rod wasn't retrieved. What they had done, without knowing it, was disable the gallery's defenses long enough for Grimhook's own agent to get in and out. She emerged from the crowd holding the rod. She broke its tip against the floor, told the assembled guests it was a prop and that Thorne had staged a bit of theatre to announce their new business partnership, then used its remaining influence to make Thorne confirm the lie. She tousled his hair on the way out.

She hired the party afterward — directly, eyes open, because they'd proven themselves useful precisely by being watched rather than effective. The commission: enchanted bracers belonging to Zal'goroth, a dark shaman of blood magic and soul manipulation in exile somewhere in the Ironspine Mountains. She did not explain why she wanted them.

The Circle wasn't finished. On the Ironspine road, Jimmy Quickhands intercepted the party with a half-orc named Gern and a halfling named Hellen in the trees. He wanted the ring. Artcoth gave the signal. The fight ended with Gern dead and Jimmy dead at 140 feet, an arrow through him in the tree line. His body had an encrypted note from Coal/Nicholas: *"Enough excuses. I've been patient enough. Take care of them."* The Circle now knows the party didn't deliver, and Jimmy is gone.

Into the Iron Spine. The underdark opened beneath them: bioluminescent fungi, compressed silence, tunnels that had been shaped and partially abandoned over centuries. A war mage held a bridge deeper in — Shah Doh was charmed. The tunnels opened into a lightless underground lake. Shalindra the aboleth surfaced. She called herself the King-slayer and wore the signet ring of the ancient dwarven monarch on her fang. She offered safe passage into the duergar city above in exchange for the party clearing the infestation she wanted gone — demonstrated what leverage looked like by commanding a duergar thrall to slit his own throat, then handed over the signet ring as a key to the underwater passage. The pact was entered.

The signet ring opened the passage. The duergar city above was the ancient dwarven capital rebuilt: functional spaces enlarged, ornament stripped out, ceilings lowered. Casus joined the party in the library — having arrived separately, his route unclear. The arc's combat resolution came at the Soul Forge: Zal'goroth was defeated. Ovaltine played a role in the escape. They surfaced and delivered to Grimhook. She pointed them toward the next commission: the Pendant of Stars.

Whether the pact with Shalindra was fulfilled to her satisfaction is unresolved.

## Arc 2: The Storm That Remembers (Sessions 13–present)

Downtime in Ravencrest between arcs. Ovaltine's shop produced a rift — small, not sustained, but a threshold crossed, with the party present. Grimhook visited and the arrangement was formalized into a contract.

The party needed a headquarters. Two properties: Hobsworth House (a planar curiosity shop structured like a residence, rooms that don't connect the same way twice) and Bellwether House (genuinely haunted, the kind of haunting that reads the people moving through it and forms opinions). The party claimed a headquarters. Coriac received a message in a bottle from the sea — an SOS that found him specifically, contents not fully resolved. Grimhook formalized the Pendant of Stars terms: what she wants, what the party receives, and what happens if the pendant ends up elsewhere.

The White Gull was acquired — Grimhook's contract facilitated it. The captain's psychology was established as a factor from the start: his relationship to the sea, the crew, and this particular voyage has texture that the party will need to understand to manage him. Grimvald had a "one more mile" moment early in the crossing that held the crew together.

The storm came on deliberately. It intensified in phases — weather that started as bad and became something with intention, something directing them rather than obstructing them. Phase 3 carried the White Gull toward Ariel's island. The shore approached through rain and fog, a twisted grove visible inland, the trees' silhouettes not bending the way wind bends things. Items found in or near the storm crossing: Artcoth's Gloves of the Tide (beach wreckage), Shah Doh's Shield of Storm's Mercy, Gareth's Stormglass Shard, Grimvald's Storm Notation, Coriac's Warden's Bone.

The White Gull grounded on black sand. Ariel's presence saturates the island. She began a sentence to Coriac that she did not finish.

Five Storm Draugr emerged from fog and surf.
