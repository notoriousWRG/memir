An ancient dwarven city now occupied by duergar, built above a vast underground dark lake. The original dwarven rulers were defeated by [[shalindra]] the aboleth; [[zal-goroth]] later seized the city, poisoned the surrounding land, and bent the minds of those nearby before his presumed final battle.

The duergar now hold it as a military and religious outpost — a militarized faction controlling the streets and a smaller religious sect conducting rituals at Zal'goroth's temple.

## Arc 1 history

The party entered via a passage beneath the dark lake using a dwarven signet ring retrieved from Shalindra's fang. They navigated the city through ventilation shafts, narrowly escaped duergar forces, discovered the treasure room (unlocked by Artcoth), and solved the founding-king statue puzzle. [[casus]] was found here — hiding in the abandoned library, researching Zal'goroth's history.

[[gareth]] encountered a statue in the temple's side corridor that bore a resemblance to his patron. That recognition has not been resolved.

## Notable features

**The dark lake** — Shalindra's domain. A vast underground lake; the aboleth's eyes follow movement beneath the surface.

**The throne room** — Ancient dwarven kings carved in the walls. The statue puzzle triggered a ventilation shaft escape route.

**The library** — Abandoned. Where Casus made his Tiny Hut and where the party met him. Contains historical records of Zal'goroth's conquest.

**The temple** — Active duergar religious site, focused on Zal'goroth worship. The statue Gareth recognized is in a side corridor.
