Once the grand, grieving home of the Bellwether family — an alchemist's estate at the edge of Ravencrest, abandoned and feared after Dr. Bellwether's soul-ritual destroyed his family from the inside. The party took it as their headquarters: in exchange for recovering the original deed, the city guilds signed it over free and clear, no back taxes owed.

They didn't just claim it. They **cleansed** it — gathered the shattered soul of [[eleanor-bellwether]] and set her free. The house remembers that, and is grateful.

## The house now

The walls no longer knock. The candlelight that burned in dark windows has gone quiet and ordinary. What remains is a home that is *almost* at peace — gentle hauntings instead of grief: a lullaby down the stairwell, chairs tidied by no one, the smell of lilac drifting through a closed room, the word *thank you* forming in frost on a cold morning.

That's [[amelia]], the ghost-child who stayed. She keeps the house warm the only ways she knows, watches over those sleeping under its roof, and is grateful, playful, and ever-creepy in equal measure.
