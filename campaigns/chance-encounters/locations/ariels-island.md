The island is not natural land. It formed where [[ariel]] was forced to remain still — stabilized by [[edrin-vael]]'s magic, reshaped by intention rather than erosion. The fog doesn't enter the inland clearing where Ariel is bound. The trees at its edge lean away rather than toward. The air coming out is several degrees warmer than the surrounding island and smells of ozone.

## Behavior

The island responds to posture. Players who try to dominate, intimidate, or extract will feel it resist. Players who observe, ask, or yield will find it cooperative. The fog here isn't weather — it moves between the trees at a consistent height, like water at low tide. Sound behaves differently: surf disappears behind you faster than it should.

## Key locations

**The beach** — Black sand, wrecked ships, fog. The draugr come from the water, never from inland. The island absorbs them when they dissolve. As of session 18 the beach fight is over; the surviving draugr watch from offshore rather than landing.

**Group A's camp** — Inland of the wrecks, behind a crude alarm perimeter of rope and bone strung at ankle height. Home to the survivors [[dessa]], [[marek]], and [[ollo]]. A fire, organized scarcity, people who have been here long enough to stop being scared. Some of their crew walked inland weeks ago and never came back; their old captain is reportedly sighted at the treeline.

**The Spar Field** — Just past the treeline. Masts and spars from multiple wrecks at different angles; moss grows in the direction of the island's center. Difficult terrain. Wind through the standing spars produces low, sustained tones — not music, not voices, but the threshold between them.

**The Hollowed Hull** — Mid-island. An overturned ship, keel-up, that forms a shelter; door cut and reinforced. Evidence of habitation — fire pit still warm. This is Group B's last camp.

**The Circled Zone** — The fog does not enter it. The trees lean away. At the center, a stone frame roughly 12 feet tall stands unsupported; the stone is the color of storm clouds. Ariel exists inside it the way a river exists in its banks — bound, not imprisoned. Do not enter this zone prematurely. The air pressure changes; ears pop; fog from the clearing pushes outward against anyone who tries to cross the threshold.

**The Lone Captain's Position** — Elevated ground, sheltered but exposed. If the captain is on the island, he is here.
