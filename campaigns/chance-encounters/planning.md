## Established S25

- The brood died on the green to Shah's last blow. **Only radiant stops its regrowth**: Shah's Divine Smite, Dame Hervor's blade, and Sölvi's sunlit strike on the brood itself, which turned incoming damage radiant. The fountain is wrecked. The **tide-spawn water elemental survived**, released Sölvi, and withdrew to the fountain; the water is receding but not gone. See [[serpent-brood]].
- Coriac learned through Channel Divinity that the brood descends from Jörmungandr. It now knows he serves Thor.
- Coal walked out at the start of the fight ("this is your business, friend, not mine"). Artcoth tried to pull him in and failed. No exit device was shown.
- Hellen and two thugs came through an upstairs window. Ovaltine, invisible, fought them and was beaten up. Artcoth ran off the thugs and chased Hellen. **Hellen got nothing** (and may have dropped something, undecided), and both thugs escaped. Her seal matches the amphora shards.
- Casus is **gone** (Unmoored 4/4). He asked Ovaltine for an anchor and got none. He left the **Obsidian Cypher, a vial of Edrin's bear blood, and a sealed letter** on the stairs and vanished without an afterimage. See [[casus-leaves-three-things]].
- Gareth's mental alarm from Ovaltine was a one-night measure, not a fixture. Hervor and Shah traded smirks; Sölvi owes Coriac for Part Water. Sölvi, Hervor, and Shah all serve Baldr; Coriac alone serves Thor.

### Island Climax Design
*Decided. The Reckoning is designed. This is the record.*

**What was decided:**
1. The captain walked to the Circled Zone on purpose — tied to the storm by trauma, not faith. He's done. He calls for the storm; Ariel receives him gently. His goodbye plays first (Suggestion reckoning, "Elara will know what to do").
2. Edrin steps forward after. His argument: control as protection. His crack: Coriac is proof the thing he called impossible isn't. He gets there himself if pressed.
3. **Freedom clock** — 4 stages; Ariel attacks under compulsion while the party works the triggers. Attacking Edrin tightens the bind. Reaching Ariel/cracking Edrin's justification/Coriac standing in it advances the clock.
4. At 4/4: the frame shatters. Ariel speaks. The Stilled pass at peace in the Hollowed Hull. Edrin exits (dropped or exiled).
5. Aftermath: the island lets them go. Elara becomes captain. The bond between Coriac and Ariel is established.

Two kinds of storm relationship run through the night: Coriac by faith, the captain by trauma. The captain is what the wrong relationship with the storm costs. Coriac's arc is the other path.

**Status:** Captain scene played S20. Edrin stepped forward S20. Reckoning resolved S21 — freedom clock 4/4, Edrin killed in bear form, Ariel free. Falling action (item 5 above) not yet played — carried into S22.
