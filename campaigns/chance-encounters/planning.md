## Scene Stakes

Round 2 of the Storm Draugr fight is about to start. The party is scattered: Coriac isolated at the waterline with Draugr D and E on him; Draugr C behind Grimvald and Shah Doh; Draugr A fled toward the treeline and hasn't come back. The White Gull is grounded. The captain is gone — no body, no signal. Ariel's presence saturates the island; the fight is happening inside her attention. The party doesn't know yet that there's no anchor.

What happens if they do nothing: Draugr A returns from the treeline. The island does not intervene in the fight. Coriac takes another hit. The captain stays gone.

## Active Clocks

| Clock | Advance trigger | Stage | Max outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Captain's window | Each round of combat where no one looks for him; each long rest on the island | 1 / 4 | He's absorbed — the island has him and won't give him back the same way |
| Ariel's communication | Coriac misses an opening or acts in a way that closes the space | 1 / 3 | She withdraws. The sentence goes unfinished. She doesn't offer again this arc. |
| Draugr A reentry | Every 2 rounds of combat | 1 / 2 | Draugr A returns from the treeline — alone, slower, having been somewhere specific |
| Elara + Wrenn decision | Each in-world day the party spends on the island with no contact | 1 / 3 | They make a call without the party — raise anchor (there isn't one), signal the coast, or try to leave alone |

## NPC Wants Tonight

| NPC | Want tonight | Fear tonight | Tell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ariel | Finish the sentence to Coriac | Being heard but not understood; being reduced to the storm she's bound in | The air goes still when she recedes; cold breath on the neck when she's present |
| The island | Keep the party here longer — it finds them interesting | Them leaving before the captain situation resolves | The tide does not cooperate; paths close; the fog thickens at the edges |
| Draugr A | Unknown — it went somewhere | — | It returned slower and alone. It saw something. |
| Elara Venn | Know if the party is coming back | Being left on a grounded ship with no answers and no authority to act | She'll send a signal if she thinks they're in trouble; she'll wait if she thinks they're in control |

## Trigger Table

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Party searches the beach after combat | They find the captain's hat. It's dry. |
| Captain's hat is found | He went somewhere voluntarily — not swept out, not drowned. Somewhere on the island. |
| Coriac reaches back toward Ariel — actively, not passively | She finishes the sentence. |
| Grimvald comes clean about the Suggestion | Cold fury, not hot rage. The captain is a man who prides himself on free will. He doesn't yell — he goes quiet and starts doing his own accounting. |
| Casus uses the Ovaltine potion on the island | Ariel interacts with it directly. Don't plan the outcome in advance; let it happen at the table. The reaction is proportional to what Casus intends. |
| Party tries to leave without resolving the anchor | The tide does not cooperate. They can try, but the island makes it difficult. This isn't punishment — it's the clock. |
| Draugr A returns | It approaches the party but does not attack. It went to where the captain is. It can lead them there if they follow. |
| Artcoth puts on the gloves and tries to use them | Decide now: pick one ability, make it specific to Ariel's domain (water, memory, storm), and commit to it. Don't improvise mid-session. |

## Open DM Questions

- What does Ariel actually want to say to Coriac? Decide before session — don't improvise it at the table.
- What do Artcoth's gloves do? Pick one thing, water/storm/memory-flavored, before session opens.
- Where exactly is the captain? Island interior? Underground? Decide where Draugr A leads.
- If the captain resurfaces, does he know something was done to him — or does he just feel it?
