Warlock who believes his patron is Odin. It is not. His patron is Feanoro, a corrupted seer once in service of Odin who fell to [[nidhogg]]'s influence while delving too deep into Yggdrasil's roots. Feanoro approached Gareth in his moment of desperation — separated from his parents, surrounded by cloaked men in Odin's temple — and posed as a divine messenger. Gareth didn't know the difference. He still doesn't.

Wields [[sword-of-shadow-and-flame]], a blade given by Feanoro as encouragement. Has secretly performed a Severance Ritual, sacrificing a soul from an enchanted orb for power.

## Plot threads

**The false patron.** Gareth believes he's chosen by Odin. The statue he and the party encountered in the Underdark temple bore a resemblance to him — a moment that seeded a question neither he nor the party has fully answered. Feanoro will exploit any doubt that surfaces rather than let it resolve.

**The sword.** [[sword-of-shadow-and-flame]] gains charges from kills. Using soul charges requires a Wisdom save; failures advance a corruption track (aggressive → goes for the kill → attacks nearest creature regardless of alignment). Feanoro has appeared over the bodies of Gareth's kills — not threatening. Watching. The sword is working.

**Feanoro's visions.** Recurring: a vast cavern with a throne carved from earth and obsidian, Feanoro in elven form with scarred features and burning eyes. The dreams push toward fealty. One vision after a kill in the Ironspine Mountains: Feanoro gloried over the bodies, satisfied. He finds Gareth's violence aesthetically pleasing.

**The reforge path.** The sword in its current form is Feanoro's claim on Gareth. The path to breaking that runs through Odin — genuine acknowledgment of the deception, and a divine act of remaking the blade under different authority. The command word would change. The sword would become something Gareth owns rather than something that owns him. This has not happened yet.

**The anchor.** In the party's running dynamic, Gareth was in charge of the anchor. The anchor is gone. That's where the ship sits right now.

**Invisibility pair.** Casus made him invisible before the draugr fight. He has a lit sword and is currently unseen — breaking that invisibility will register as a flash of light in the fog, brief and significant.

**Artcoth connection.** Travels with [[artcoth]]; the nature of that relationship (allies, co-conspirators) is part of both their histories.
