Sea-faring elf. Spent his first century at sea — sailing, fishing, dock work. Devoted to [[thor]]. Served as Boatswain on the *Fair Wind* before the Chance Encounters arc began. Wields [[mjolnirs-fury]] — a warhammer taken from a dead paladin of Thor in the underdark after the party avenged that paladin's death. Thor's blessing transferred with it. During the storm crossing to [[ariels-island]] Coriac took a direct lightning strike to protect the ship; that act crystallized the blessing into recognition, and a rune of protection seared into the handle where he held it. Also carries the [[forge-shard]], an unfinished blessing from Thor's temple that completes itself through a specific roleplay act on the island. And [[charlie-bones]], an undead rat taken from a necromantic sorcerer in the underdark.

## Plot threads

**Ariel and the unfinished sentence.** During the storm crossing, Coriac voluntarily took a lightning strike to protect the ship. As a result, Ariel pulled him into a vision: vast female form, crackling eyes, storm built into flesh. He reached out his hand and invited her to speak. She was shocked — no mortal has ever done this. She moved toward speech. The vision ended before she could finish. She has not finished what she was about to say.

**Retroactive canon.** Coriac's storm visions were not Thor alone. Ariel has been reaching him on the wings of storms for some time — Thor's domain functioned as a bridge, not a wall. The lightning-strike vision confirms this retroactively.

**Coriac's old crew.** He knew [[dessa]] and the group she's sheltering with years ago. That reunion is waiting at the treeline.

**The Underdark.** During the Frostveil arc, Shah Doh was charmed by a war-mage's control and turned on Coriac before breaking free. That moment sits in the party's history.

**Current position.** Isolated at the waterline, two draugr adjacent, at the start of Round 2.
