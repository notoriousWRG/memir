Sea-faring elf. Spent his first century at sea — sailing, fishing, dock work. Devoted to [[thor]]. Served as Boatswain on the *Fair Wind* before the Chance Encounters arc began. Wields [[mjolnirs-fury]] — a warhammer taken from a dead paladin of Thor in the underdark after the party avenged that paladin's death. Thor's blessing transferred with it. During the storm crossing to [[ariels-island]] Coriac took a direct lightning strike to protect the ship; that act crystallized the blessing into recognition, and a rune of protection seared into the handle where he held it. Also carries the [[forge-shard]], a blessing from Thor's temple that completed itself S22 when Ariel struck it with her own lightning — see Plot threads. And [[charlie-bones]], an undead rat taken from a necromantic sorcerer in the underdark.

## Plot threads

**Ariel and the bond — RESOLVED, S22.** During the storm crossing, Coriac voluntarily took a lightning strike to protect the ship. Ariel pulled him into a vision: vast female form, crackling eyes, storm built into flesh. He reached out his hand and invited her to speak — no mortal had ever done this. She spoke one line aloud in S20 ("The storm follows you") and didn't finish the rest. She never needed to: after the Reckoning broke her bind (S21), she sealed the bond wordlessly instead — struck [[forge-shard|the Forge Shard]] with her own lightning and burned her name into it (S22), then departed. Willing anchor, not a leash — she will not be controlled again, by anyone, including him. He tested that once already: tried praying through the shard to redirect her storm off the horizon, and she didn't move for him. See [[forge-shard]] for the mechanic (Ariel's Mark).

**Retroactive canon.** Coriac's storm visions were not Thor alone. Ariel has been reaching him on the wings of storms for some time — Thor's domain functioned as a bridge, not a wall. The lightning-strike vision confirms this retroactively.

**Coriac's old crew — RESOLVED.** He knew [[dessa]] and the group she's sheltering with years ago; that reunion played out at Group A's camp on the island. She's since left with [[elara-venn]] aboard the White Gull (S22) — that chapter is closed, not warmly.

**The Underdark.** During the Frostveil arc, Shah Doh was charmed by a war-mage's control and turned on Coriac before breaking free. That moment sits in the party's history.

**Current position.** Ravencrest, aboard [[the-intimidator]] — the new ship he now captains.
