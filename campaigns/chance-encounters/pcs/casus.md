Fell into adventuring unexpectedly — not a typical career path. Curious, tactically minded, increasingly entangled in ancient magical legacies he didn't go looking for.

## Plot threads

**The Liriel connection.** Casus is somehow tied to [[liriel-baenre]] — by blood, destiny, or both. His origin quest centers on the Ring of Arcane Insight, an item linked to her. That thread hasn't resolved.

**Zal'goroth and the Frostveil artifacts.** During the Underdark arc, Casus encountered information about [[zal-goroth]], soulbinding rituals, and artifacts connected to the Frostveil. How deeply that knowledge binds him is still open.

**The Obsidian Cypher.** Casus carries the [[obsidian-cypher]] — a small black cube from an exiled dwarven archmage named Thlgrin Deepforge. Six rune faces. Six imprisoned immortal entities whose life force powers its effects. A pocket dimension inside with Thlgrin in stasis, treasures, monsters, and lore. Whether Casus understands what he's holding is an open question.

**Ovaltine's potion.** Casus carries an experimental latent-magic potion made by [[ovaltine-jenkins]]. See [[ovaltine-potion]] for full mechanics. The island is saturated with Ariel's presence — using it here could have significant and personalized effects. Held for now.

**Tactical role.** Arcane tactician. In the current fight, he cast Invisibility on himself and Gareth simultaneously — a tactical choice that creates a creative-pain problem: staying hidden preserves their advantage but leaves the others exposed.
