Pragmatic, coordinating, motivated by money. Travels with [[gareth]] — ally, co-conspirator, or both. Former associate of the Circle — an organization that operates thieves' guilds under legitimate trading fronts as cover for artifact collection and worse. Artcoth's connection to the Circle is old and complicated; they tested him again in Ravencrest, and he chose differently than they expected.

## Plot threads

**The captain's letter.** Artcoth returned a stolen letter to the captain. That act built the party's most honest relationship with him — which makes the captain's current disappearance sit specifically on Artcoth.

**The gloves.** Found a pair of magic gloves in the beach wreckage during the landfall chaos. See [[artcoths-gloves]].

**Venn and Artcoth.** Artcoth had friction with [[elara-venn]] earlier in the arc, then earned credit with a clutch save involving her. She's watching.

**Coordinator.** Instinct under pressure: call out positions, make tactical sense of chaos. First thing he did when the ship hit the shallows was start directing.

**The Circle split.** The Circle (via [[jimmy-quickhands]]) hired the party to steal a ruby ring — one of Liriel's infused artifacts — from [[lady-grimhook]]. The party worked the job, then sided with Grimhook instead. Jimmy came to collect or kill; Artcoth gave the order to attack and put the kill shot at 140 feet. An encrypted note on Jimmy's body points to Coal (real name: Nicholas), the Circle operative who runs their tier. The note needs intelligence work to decrypt fully.

**Coal's arc.** The Circle's test of Artcoth wasn't just about the ring. A mid-level operative — an elf in loud clothes, deliberately conspicuous — was sent to watch the party's work and evaluate whether Artcoth could be recruited for larger things. That path is now closed. What comes instead is unknown.
