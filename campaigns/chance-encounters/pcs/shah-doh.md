Half-Orc paladin from the village of Drog, raised in the tradition of Gruumsh, carrying a name he never fully understood. Shah's mother has always been cagey about his father — she knows that if Shah learns the truth, he won't stay on the half-orc path her family built. On his 30th nameday, Shah had a vivid dream: a figure told him his faith didn't belong to Gruumsh and that he needed to leave Drog to find where it did. He left.

That figure, and the divine thread, connect to [[baldr]] — god of light and purity. Shah's father served Baldr and, separated from his son, willed Baldr's protection over him from a distance. Shah doesn't know any of this yet. He carries [[baldrs-gleaming-chain]] — a physical manifestation of that protection, the origin of which he doesn't know. He also carries [[amelias-doll]], given to him by [[amelia]], the ghost of Bellwether House.

## Plot threads

**The Baldr thread.** The divine connection is established but unexplored. Who was the man in the vision? His father? Who does his father serve — and why Baldr? Does his mother know where he went? These questions are live and unanswered.

**The charm incident.** During the Frostveil arc, Shah was charmed by a war-mage and turned on [[coriac]] before breaking free. He broke it himself. That moment — the control, the rupture, the recovery — lives in the party's shared history.

**Living ladder.** Pattern of physical rescue: catches himself on rigging, uses rope to haul Grimvald back from overboard during the landfall grounding. Lit a torch as a secondary beacon in the fog. Quiet heroism, no announcement. That's Shah Doh.
