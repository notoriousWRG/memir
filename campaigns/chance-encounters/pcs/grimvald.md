A performer who chased greatness and found something he didn't expect at the bottom of a rune-bound barrel at the Echo of Mead. The voices started that night — smooth, coaxing, promising he was born for greatness. He thought it was madness or ego. Then the duergar took him.

The party cut the ritual short and pulled him out alive. That's how he got here.

## Plot threads

**The patron.** The voices from the barrel are the source of his warlock abilities. Their nature and agenda are unclear to Grimvald; the table should treat this as an open mystery.

**Suggestion on the captain.** Grimvald cast Suggestion on the captain — and the captain pressed on against his own stated condition as a result. This has not been acknowledged at the table. If the captain surfaces on the island and pieces it together, the fallout will be severe. Grimvald is sitting on a powder keg.

**Emotional register.** Perceptive, attuned to what's unspoken. Took a hit from Draugr C in Round 1 and starts the next fight behind. The interesting question for Grimvald is not whether he fights well but what he does when cornered.
