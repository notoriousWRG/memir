A brood-spawn of Jormungandr, bound into a black amphora with a serpent-and-world stopper (the Circle's mark) and poured out onto the front green at the S24 gala. Seawater pours from the broken jar far past what it could hold, and the thing rises out of it: heads on long necks, scales the color of a winter harbor.

**Reskinned from:** Hydra (MM), five heads. AC 15, HP 172 (15d12+75), Speed 30 ft, swim 30 ft. Bite +8, reach 10 ft, 1d10+5 piercing, one per head. Extra reaction per head beyond the first (opportunity attacks only).

**Regrowth — homebrew tweak:** heads regrow as normal, but not if the creature took **fire or radiant** damage since its last turn. Gives radiant a job.

If it is too hard for five level-7 PCs, start at four heads.

Tactic: spread the heads across as many targets as possible, and go for guests when nobody is engaging it.
