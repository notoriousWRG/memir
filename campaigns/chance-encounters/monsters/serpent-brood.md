A brood-spawn of Jormungandr, bound into a black amphora with a serpent-and-world stopper (the Circle's mark) and poured out onto the front green at the S24 gala. Seawater pours from the broken jar far past what it could hold, and the thing rises out of it: heads on long necks, scales the color of a winter harbor.

**Reskinned from:** Hydra (MM), five heads. AC 15, HP 172 (15d12+75), Speed 30 ft, swim 30 ft. Bite +8, reach 10 ft, 1d10+5 piercing, one per head. Extra reaction per head beyond the first (opportunity attacks only).

**Regrowth — homebrew tweak:** heads regrow as normal, but not if the creature took **fire or radiant** damage since its last turn. Gives radiant a job.

**Tide-spawn (the flood fights too).** Not a humanoid, not even a shape: animated seawater with a primitive will of its own, woken by its long time sealed in the amphora alongside Loki's brood. It doesn't speak or plan. It surges, pools, pulls, and follows warmth and fear, bulging up into a vaguely serpentine crest and sinking back. Run it as a flood with appetites, not a creature with a face. One **Water Elemental** (MM, reskinned: AC 14, HP 114, two Slams +7 for 2d8+4, Whelm DC 15 Str recharge 4-6) rises out of the pool at the start as the brood's tide. It hits softer than the hydra but soaks a lot of damage, and Whelm can drag a guest under, which gives the party one more person to save.

**Budget (6 x level 7, 2024 rules):** Hydra 3,900 + 1 Water Elemental 1,800 = **5,700 XP, Low**. Planned this way on purpose: the fight is meant to run long (regrowth, the hydra's extra reactions, ~290 total HP) while the party is pulled between the serpent, the guests and the Search. Real danger is the clock and the guests, not the party dying.

**Hydra dials (play-time call, DM's discretion):**
- **HP:** base 172 (15d12+75). Bump to ~207 (18d12+90) for a modest lift, ~230 (20d12+100) for a real one.
- **Head-loss threshold:** base 25 damage in a single turn. Six level-7 PCs clear 25 almost every round, so at 25 the hydra loses a head per round. At **35** it takes a focused round, and at **45+** severing a head is a deliberate team effort. Higher threshold means more bites for longer and fewer regrowth moments, so the fire/radiant rule matters less.
- Raising either one doesn't move the calculator's CR. Treat it as extra length, not extra XP.

**Encounter dials:** if it's too trivial, a **second Water Elemental** rises from the pool (round 2-3 reads best) for 7,500 XP, Moderate. If it's too hard, start the hydra at four heads.

Tactic: the hydra spreads its heads across as many targets as possible and goes for guests when nobody is engaging it. The tide-spawn targets the most-wounded guest or the PC closest to the pool.

## Green actions (bank)

On **initiative count 20 (losing ties)**, one effect per round. Pick by feel, don't repeat back to back. These are for dynamism, not length: the party will likely all run at the fight, so the terrain should make them move and choose. Saves are DC 13 unless noted.

**Water and terrain**
- **The flood spreads.** The pool grows 10 ft and becomes difficult terrain. A prone creature in it makes a Str save or is dragged 10 ft toward the center. *(Default opener.)*
- **Undertow.** The water pulls one creature, a PC or a guest, 15 ft toward the pool's middle (Str save resists). Sets up the elemental's Whelm.
- **Sea-cold.** Until the next count 20, a creature that ends its turn in the water takes 1d6 cold damage and loses 10 ft of speed until its next turn. A fire effect that hits the pool hisses into steam that blocks sight for a round.
- **Surge.** A wave crests out of the pool in a 15-ft line, Str save or knocked prone and pushed 10 ft. It carries lanterns, tables and fleeing guests with it, so it hits the crowd as well as the PCs.

**Crowd and set dressing**
- **Stampede.** A panicked cluster of guests runs through a section of the green. Dex save or shoved 10 ft and prone. Guests can also be run into the hydra's reach.
- **Falling lanterns.** The strung lanterns come down onto the grass in a 10-ft area, burning oil on the water's edge. It's fire damage, so it **suppresses regrowth** if it hits the hydra. A gift to the party, and it sets guests' silks alight, which gives them a rescue to make. *Decide beforehand if you want to hand them that.*
- **Dark.** Lanterns fall and the light drops. One zone goes dim, then dark, and the serpent's winter-harbor scales are hard to see against the water. Punishes a PC who relies on one position.

**Story-tied**
- **Casus slipping.** Unmoored shows on the green: a frozen patch of air, rain falling upward around him, a guest who sees him in two places. Environmental, no save, but it lets the table feel the clock.
- **Amelia's frost.** Frost spreads across the water at the pool's edge, a small patch where the cold meets the sea. Safe footing for a round, no difficult terrain. Only if the party has asked Amelia for help.
- **The sword.** The [[sword-of-shadow-and-flame]] hums when it's within reach of the serpent. Ties to Gareth's corruption-track hook (Wisdom save DC 13 on first blood).
