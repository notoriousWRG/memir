The crew of Group B who **took** — spoils prised out of the wreckage, living wood cut from the forest — and the island took them back. They are not draugr; they did not die in a fight at the waterline. They were stopped mid-reach. Each one holds the exact posture of the moment the island closed its hand: an arm extended for something, fingers half-curled around a thing they never got to keep.

They stand in the cold back of the Hollowed Hull, looping that instant — reaching, almost grasping, never closing. The air there is colder than the rest of the island, and quieter; sound thins out near them. [[onund]] and [[tjorvi]] keep a hall full of bad singing between themselves and this room and will not look at it.

This is the dread half of the same thread as the clowns. Same crew, same captain ([[edrin-vael]]), same temptation — the survivors at the front stopped taking in time. These didn't.

## They are not a fight you want

For a 7th-level party the Stilled are **mechanically minor and morally heavy**. The point is not the damage; it's that these are [[coriac]]'s old crewmates, [[ollo]]'s shipmates, the people Önund and Tjorvi are drinking to forget. Run them as horror and grief, not as an encounter to win.

## What breaks them — homebrew

**The Stilled are passive.** They loop. They do not attack, notice, or pursue — *unless someone takes something from the back room.* Lifting any object from among them, cutting from them, or looting the dead repeats the crew's sin, and the island answers the same way it always does:

> **Trigger — Taking:** the instant a creature removes something from the back room (or from a Stilled), every Stilled present completes its reach this round — toward the taker. They become hostile until the taken thing is set down or returned.

- **Stat line:** as **Specter** (CR 1) — AC 12, ~22 hp, incorporeal, *Life Drain* (3d6 necrotic, max HP reduced until long rest), Sunlight Sensitivity, Incorporeal Movement. Run 3–4. Scale by adding bodies, not numbers — a *crowd* of grasping crewmates is the image.
- **To pass safely:** take nothing. Set down anything already taken. Or **witness them** — name who they were, aloud and true (Ollo or Coriac can; the clowns can't bear to). A Stilled that is genuinely seen and named goes still for good — its reach finally finishing on *being known* instead of on a thing. This is the grace, and the ledger beat.
