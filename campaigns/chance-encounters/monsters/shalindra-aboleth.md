Ancient aboleth. Self-titled "the King-slayer" — she defeated the ancient dwarven monarch of the [[duergar-city]] and claimed the dark lake beneath the Iron Spine as her domain. Holds the dwarven signet ring as a trophy, kept on her fang.

Communicates entirely through telepathy. Voice described as "a deep resonance that sent shivers down their spines." Holds deep contempt for the duergar who occupy the city above: *"vermin who dared to skirt the edge of my dominion, too frightened to submit to my influence."*

## The pact with the party

During the Frostveil arc, the party was stranded on a stone island in the dark lake. Shalindra emerged and offered terms: safe passage into the duergar city in exchange for clearing the infestation. [[coriac]] negotiated with flattery; Shalindra granted the deal as a condescending mercy.

She gave the party the dwarven signet ring as a key to the underwater passage, demonstrated her leverage by commanding a thrall to slit his own throat, and sent them forward with: *"Do not disappoint me."*

She kept a thrall — a gaunt duergar with hollow eyes — as the guide. The pact was entered. Whether it was fulfilled to her satisfaction, and what consequences follow, is unresolved.

## Standard aboleth capabilities

Legendary actions, lair actions in her lake. Enslave, Mucous Cloud, Probing Telepathy. Her lake is her lair — she is significantly weaker away from it.
