An instance of the canon [[hollow-warden]] — a construct built to guard a threshold that long ago lost the thing it guarded, and so invented its own answer. [[edrin-vael]] set it on the line between the Spar Field and the deeper island, back when control still felt like care. It no longer remembers why. It only remembers that it *weighs*.

Norse skin: a figure assembled from wreck — ship-timber ribs, whalebone arms, a salvaged figurehead for a face, barnacle and verdigris where joints should be. It stands among the singing spars so still that the party will mistake it for one until it turns its head.

## The challenge — the Unbalanced Scale

The Warden was once the toll-keeper of a wreck-port that the sea has long since taken. It still holds the office in its body: its two whalebone arms hang as the arms of a great **balance**, a pan slung from each. One pan sits heaped and low with the "tolls" it has collected from a hundred years of travelers — coins, teeth, buckles, bone. The other hangs empty and high.

It will not lower its arms and open the path until **the scale is balanced.** It will not explain how. It cannot be talked into simply standing aside — the office is the only thing it has left.

*Set the stage; do not solve it (see `dm-philosophy.md` → "Challenges — Stage Them, Don't Solve Them").* Hold only the two triggers below; let the players find the path between them.

- **Success trigger — the pans level.** However they do it, the moment the balance sits even, the Warden raises its head, lowers its arms, steps off the path, and goes still — its long task, at last, complete. (A grace note: it looks *relieved*.)
- **Fail / force trigger — taking, or barging.** *Removing* weight from the loaded pan without replacing it reads as theft of the toll — and on this island, taking is the sin that animates things (see [[the-stilled]]). Attacking it, or trying to shove past the line, also wakes it to defense.

Open middle, for your confidence only — players may find these or others: add weight to the empty pan (their own goods, gathered stone, a willing body, a *mage hand* press, a held spell); find that one specific item in the heap is *theirs* / a crewmate's and that reclaiming-and-returning shifts the count; freeze, jam, or counterweight the mechanism; convince it the toll is paid; physically force the balance and hold it (Strength contest). Any of these that leaves the pans level satisfies the success trigger. None of them is "the" answer.

## If it comes to a fight

It would rather you solved the scale. **Procedural, not cruel** — nonlethal until struck, and it does not pursue past its threshold.

- **Stat line:** as **Hollow Warden** (CR 5 construct) — AC 17, ~75 hp, slam +6 (2d8+3 bludgeoning), Multiattack 2. Trait *Procedural*: at the start of its turn, if no creature is attacking it and none has taken from the scale, it stops and re-poses the challenge instead of attacking. It is trying to do its job, not kill you.
