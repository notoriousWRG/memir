HOUSEWARMING
## Bellwether House Throws a Party. Something Else RSVPs.

**What's going on:** A week after deciding to throw a gala, the party threw a gala. Bellwether House (the formerly haunted mansion they now own, ghost included) was strung with Ovaltine's floating lanterns, stocked with food in every room, and open to basically all of Ravencrest. The hosts took up stations: Artcoth and Shah on the front door, Grimvald at the piano, Coriac in the music room, and Gareth in the shadows, taste-testing everything before it left the kitchen. (Quality control. Allegedly.) Amelia, the house's resident child ghost, knocked over glasses and wrote "welcome" across the front windows. The one guest everybody was watching for, Lady Grimhook, the party's patron who stopped checking in weeks ago, never came.

**The stakes:** It went great right up until it didn't. Late in the night something shattered outside, someone screamed, and the fountain on the front green started pouring out far more water than a fountain holds, with shards of black pottery swirling in it. Then heads came up. Several. On long necks. The crowd stampeded. Shah kept people inside, Gareth used a floating magic hand to herd guests toward the back door, and Coriac told everyone to run, but not too far, because they'd want to see the aftermath. That's where we stopped: the thing is up and roaring and nobody has rolled initiative. The company is literally called Chance Encounters, so: on brand.

## The News in 6

🗞 The Intimidator, the party's new ship, finished its first job: 450 gold, with Grull taking point and Tjorvi helping. Passive income, but make it nautical.

🗞 Bramwell, the talking brass door-knocker, decided to announce every guest, mostly to Artcoth and mostly wrong: "ARTCOTH, a WOOL MERCHANT." "ARTCOTH, his WIFE, who is WEARING HIS CLOAK." Artcoth's review: "I'm taking these doors off the hinges, and you'll be sitting out back." Bramwell went quiet. Mostly.

🗞 Gary, who runs the Anchor and Tap down by the docks, turned up with a crumpled invitation that had blown out of Artcoth's study and in through his pub window. He was admitted on probation: "If anything happens here tonight, you're the first person I'm coming looking for." Gary, he means it.

🗞 Lord Thorne, a very rich, very stiff halfling, took Artcoth and Shah upstairs to say his ward Tansy has been gone two weeks. The window latch was opened from the inside, she spent her evenings chatting with his talking dragon skull (in Draconic, which nobody else in the house speaks), and her good boots are gone but her purse isn't. Artcoth could tell he was holding something back, pushed, and Thorne left offended. The party is interviewing his staff anyway.

🗞 Brother Sölvi and Dame Hervor came from Baldr's temple to meet Shah. His chainmail, the one that glimmers when he touches it, was left at their temple by one of their order's highest knights, who said only that "one day someone would need it." Sölvi was delighted. Hervor demanded to know what Shah wouldn't do even for his friends, and Sölvi had to physically hold her back.

🗞 Casus (played by Chris) spent the night slipping through time: the house's past, with Amelia alive at a dinner table; the forest where Artcoth shot the Circle's fixer Jimmy; and himself knee-deep in water on the green, a scaled tail sliding past. (An hour early, it turns out.) Ovaltine's diagnosis on his experimental potion: "It doesn't give anything. It provokes what's there." Casus is, apparently, "a man who looks at time sideways." He was escorted to bed.

NEVERMORE
## Grimvald Books a Gig

**What's going on:** Two guests nobody announced (Bramwell didn't see them; Shah and Coriac barely caught the hems of their feathered cloaks) planted themselves by the piano until Grimvald stopped playing. "We book performers," said the tall one. "For a hall in the far north," said the small one. They do that. Grimvald sang them a verse of the party's own adventures. "It is him," said the smaller one. Coriac caught the tall one reaching into his cloak and put a hand on his chest, which accomplished nothing: a black feather was already in Grimvald's hand, etched with a place in the far north and a date about a month away. "Everyone sings for him eventually."

**Vibe check:** On the way out they stopped dead in front of Gareth, looking not at him but at his sword. "That's not ours." "It was, once." Then two ravens flew off and two plain feathers drifted down. A religion check later, the table put it together: Huginn and Muninn, Odin's own ravens. Grimvald has a gig. Nobody knows who "him" is. Gareth has thoughts about his sword that he is choosing not to have.

CORPORATE OUTREACH
## Mr. Coal Would Like a Word

**What's going on:** Last guest of the night: a calm, graying man who sat down across from Artcoth and Gareth like the chair had been saved for him. Artcoth's gut said this was the man behind the original job, the one who hired them through a fixer named Jimmy to steal a ring from Lady Grimhook. (They switched sides. Jimmy didn't survive it. Artcoth took the shot.) Instead of shaking hands, he put a card in Artcoth's palm: the name's Coal. His opener: "Jimmy always told me you had no vision." Artcoth: "Seeing how Jimmy isn't with us anymore, you may be right." Gareth spotted a tattoo at his wrist, a coiled serpent with barbs behind its head, and Coal happily rolled up his sleeve to show the whole thing. "Grimhook had this mark once as well. Did you know that?"

**Vibe check:** Uncle Craig did not take the meeting. "It is a night for making friends. It's not a night for business." Make an appointment or knock on the door, any other day. When the fountain went, Artcoth told him that if he was behind it he'd regret it. Coal: "You have no idea." We genuinely can't tell if that was a threat or a confession, and we're not sure which is worse.

## Family Watch

**Artcoth (Uncle Craig):** Ran the front door, the Thorne interview, and the Coal standoff, and dragged a flickering Casus to Ovaltine by the collar. He's now holding a crime boss's business card and an open invitation for that crime boss to knock. Normal week.

**Gareth (Zack):** Officially "walking around in the shadows, keeping an eye on people." Unofficially eating the food before it went out. He clocked Coal's tattoo, got stared down by Odin's ravens, and when everything went sideways he used Mage Hand to point most of the town at the back door.

**Coriac (Josh):** Welcomed the clerics, caught Casus when he collapsed ("What the hell happened to you? Had too much to drink, huh? The party just started."), grabbed a raven by the wrist, and rolled the natural 20 that spotted the pottery shards in the fountain. For a guy with no plans tonight, he was everywhere.

## Behind the Screen 🤫

*Things the table doesn't know. Please don't tell your uncle.*

**The scream was a setup.** That shatter on the green was a Circle plant (a deliberately loud elf in deliberately loud clothes) smashing a black amphora and screaming on purpose, to empty the house. Coal's entire job was keeping Artcoth at the table until it happened. While everyone's on the lawn, the Circle's people are heading inside, upstairs, after the house ledger's pages on Lady Grimhook. The party left Casus and Ovaltine upstairs. You see the problem.

**About those heads.** The thing in the fountain is a brood-spawn of Jormungandr, the World Serpent, hauled up out of the sea in that jar. At the table it'll fight like a hydra. Plan accordingly.

**Casus is leaving.** Ovaltine's potion didn't just make Casus sick, it knocked him loose in time. Every slip tonight was a tick on a clock, and by the end of this gala he'll be gone. Not dead: somewhere, somewhen else. Chris knows and is working on a new character. Which makes Uncle Craig telling him "Why don't you just go upstairs and go to bed? No one's going to miss you … People aren't going to miss one person" land a little differently.

**Why the ravens came.** Grimvald's warlock voices started the night he drank from a rune-bound barrel at the Echo of Mead. What was in it: the mead of poetry, Odin's stolen draught. The ravens weren't reviewing the song. They were listening for the mead.

**"That's not ours. It was, once."** Gareth believes Odin is his patron. It isn't. It's Feanoro, a seer who once served Odin and fell to Nidhogg, the dragon gnawing at the roots of the world tree, and who's been posing as Odin's messenger since Gareth was a kid. The sword came from Feanoro. Odin's own eyes just looked right at it. Zack has no idea.

**Prep vs. reality.** Coriac had no visitor planned at all; the last arc leaned hard on him, so he was getting the night off. (See: Family Watch.) Casus had a parting power, Out of Step, that let him nudge his friends' dice at the cost of slipping away faster. Never used. And Coal had a tell: glancing at the window, ordering drinks to stall. If Artcoth had called him a distraction to his face, Coal would have raised his glass and said, "Of course I am. Go on, then."
