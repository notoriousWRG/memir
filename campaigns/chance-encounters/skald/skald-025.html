THE GREEN

Hydra Hunters

What's going on: We left off with a many-headed thing rising out of the fountain, and Session 25 started with initiative. Grimvald opened with Dissonant Whispers, which drove it thirty feet backward through the fountain, a fountain Uncle Craig's character had just paid for. Coriac followed with Call Lightning and a Channel Divinity for maximum damage, and two heads burst. The hydra grew them back, and a few more besides: seven heads. Shah Misty Stepped across the flooded green and put a Divine Smite into it, and three severed stumps withered for good. Dame Hervor, who had run across the water leaving white footprints, followed up with a glowing blade, and Shah took the last head.

Why it matters: Coriac learned what the thing was the way a cleric learns anything: it looked at him. It was a child of Jörmungandr, the World Serpent and the old enemy of his god, and it knew whose servant he was. (That's one way to be noticed at a party.) Brother Sölvi got dragged under by something in the pool, and Coriac parted the water to keep him breathing. The thing in the pool let go only when the hydra died. Something is still down there.

Related: The gala. Remember the shards in the fountain? They matched a seal.

THE STAIRS

Casus Steps Out

What's going on: Casus was still glitching like a worn videotape when an alarm went off in Gareth's head. Casus cast Blink, then grabbed every vial from Ovaltine's desk and Misty Stepped down the staircase, falling a half-second through a green field into the stairs. Artcoth came up in time to stab one of the intruders. They ran, and so did the halfling scout. Artcoth worked out that the commotion on the green was a distraction to get them inside, and her seal matched the amphora.

The goodbye: Casus rounded the corner, and something snapped. He held out the vials to Ovaltine. "Is there anything that would help keep me here?" Ovaltine had no answer. Casus emptied his pockets at their feet: a black cube, a vial of bear blood, and a letter sealed in black wax. Then he was gone, with no flicker, no afterimage. Ovaltine's face said the rest.

Behind the Screen 🤫

Things the table doesn't know. Please don't tell your uncle.

The scream was a setup. Last issue's shatter on the green was a Circle plant: a deliberately loud elf in deliberately loud clothes, smashing a black amphora to empty the house. Coal's job was keeping Artcoth at the table. It nearly worked. Hellen, the scout, got into the Ledger Room, and Ovaltine and Artcoth ran her off before she copied a page. The loud elf never showed up at all. We still don't know his name. (Neither does he, probably.)

Coal had a way out. A one-use rook on a chain, a chess piece that teleports him the moment he flips it. If Artcoth had grabbed him, the rook would have gone face-down on the table and Coal would have been gone before the sentence ended. He never needed it. Artcoth didn't push.

About that letter. The sealed letter isn't just a letter. Casus got it from a stranger he met underground, who promised to come to his aid if the seal is ever broken. Chris has a new character in mind that grows out of it. Casus isn't dead, and he isn't stuck: he's loose in time, and the way back, if there is one, is his to find. Uncle Craig has picked up everything Casus left behind, for now.
