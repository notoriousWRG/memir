Meet the Party

Artcoth · played by Uncle Craig · Rogue (professional sneak, retired from the Circle, mostly)
The one who starts giving orders the second anything goes wrong. Money-motivated, pragmatic, and the owner of the most consequential arrow in the campaign. He's currently bound to their employer's contract by a magical sigil, which is a very literal way to read "terms and conditions."
🤫 The Circle's original job was partly a test of whether Artcoth could be recruited for bigger things. He chose differently than they expected. That door is closed now.

Gareth · played by Zack · Warlock (believes Odin is his patron)
Quiet, lurks in shadows, carries a Sword of Shadow and Flame that grows stronger with every kill. In the underdark, the party found a temple statue that looked exactly like him. Nobody has explained that yet.
🤫 His patron isn't Odin. It's Feanoro, a seer who once served Odin and fell. Feanoro appears over the bodies of Gareth's kills, pleased.

Coriac · played by Josh · Cleric of Thor (spent his first century at sea)
A sailor first, a priest second, and now a ship's captain. He carries a hammer blessed by Thor, stepped into a lightning bolt to save a ship, and is the only mortal who has ever invited a storm goddess to speak.
🤫 She'd been reaching him through storms for years before that. Thor's domain wasn't a wall between them; it was a bridge.

Casus · played by Chris · Wizard
Joined mid-dungeon by a route nobody has ever accounted for. Carries the Obsidian Cypher, a six-sided rune cube that does extraordinary things when you press the right faces and alarming things when you don't.
🤫 He's connected to Liriel, the name engraved inside the ruby ring that started this whole campaign.

Grimvald · played by Jesse · Bard / Warlock
A performer who drank from a rune-bound barrel at a tavern called the Echo of Mead and started hearing voices promising him greatness. The party rescued him from a duergar ritual. He has since sung the party's legend in every tavern in town and talked a sea captain into a voyage he didn't want to take.
🤫 The barrel held the mead of poetry: Odin's stolen draught.

Shah Doh · played by Corey · Paladin (half-orc, still working out which god he serves)
Raised to follow Gruumsh, until a figure in a dream told him his faith belonged somewhere else and he walked out of his village to find it. Quiet heroics, no announcements. He wears a chain shirt that glimmers when he touches it.
🤫 His father serves Baldr, god of light, and willed that protection over him from a distance. His mother knows.

Deeply Important Information

🌍 The world is Norse-flavored fantasy. Odin, Thor, and Baldr are real and occasionally RSVP.

⚓ Home base is Ravencrest, a port town where everyone has a cousin on the docks.

💼 The party is a registered business: Chance Encounters LLC. Adventuring, retrieval, and, recently, event hosting.

🏚️ Headquarters is Bellwether House: haunted, renovated, still haunted. Live-in alchemist Ovaltine Jenkins, child ghost Amelia, and Bramwell, a brass door-knocker that talks.

🧝 The employer is Lady Grimhook, a noble enchanter with very good taste and very unclear motives. The enemy is the Circle, which runs thieves' guilds behind respectable trading companies.

PREVIOUSLY, ON A DIFFERENT ISLAND

Starring Calendula (played by You)

What's going on: Before any of this, there was Stormwreck Island and a cloister, and Calendula was in the party. You know how that one went better than we do. Session 1 opens with the goodbye: the island arc closed, each of them got a pearl for the road, Artcoth handed over a dragon bone to have a custom bow made, and Gareth left a tooth with a craftsman for reasons he has never explained.

How it ended: The raccoon armor commissioned for Calendula had, in her absence, been adopted by a stray cat, and had to be retrieved. We'd like the record to show it was in good hands. The seat at the table is still technically open. No pressure.

HEIST SEASON

The Ring Job Goes Sideways

What's going on: Back on the mainland, the party took a job through a fixer in Edook (passcode riddle and all): steal a ruby ring from a wealthy woman in Ravencrest named Lady Grimhook. The ring is inscribed inside with a name, Liriel, "song of the Night." The client went by Coal. Artcoth's old Circle contact, Jimmy Quickhands, laid out the options, and the party picked the classiest one: crash a gala at Lord Thorne's estate. Thorne opened the evening by using a Rod of Rulership to make Grimhook kneel in front of his guests and recite compliments she didn't mean. The party went for the rod. The animated armor in the gallery went for Gareth.

How it ended: Grimhook didn't need them to win, only to be busy. While they tied up the gallery, her own agent teleported in and took the rod. She snapped its tip on the floor, told the room the whole humiliation had been staged theater announcing a business partnership, made Thorne confirm it with the rod's last gasp, and tousled his hair on her way out. Then she hired the party. Directly, eyes open. The Circle took that personally. Jimmy caught up with them on the Ironspine road with backup ("Gettin' in bed with a mark? Even for you, this is a bad look.") and did not get the ring back.

GOING UNDER

The Underdark Has a Landlord

What's going on: Grimhook's first commission sent them into the Ironspine Mountains after the bracers of an exiled shaman named Zal'goroth, which meant going under the mountains. The tunnels led to a lightless lake under an old dwarven capital, now held by duergar (grim, grey underground dwarves). Coriac picked up his hammer here, from a dead paladin of Thor whose death the party avenged. Grimvald joined the hard way: the party cut short the duergar ritual he was strapped into.

How it ended: An aboleth named Shalindra (think ancient psychic fish with a crown on its tooth) offered safe passage in exchange for clearing out an infestation she disliked. They took the deal. Casus turned up in the city's library. At the Soul Forge, Zal'goroth fell, Ovaltine helped get them out, and the bracers went to Grimhook. Whether Shalindra considers the deal done is a question nobody has asked her.

REAL ESTATE

Chance Encounters Buys a Haunted House

What's going on: Back in Ravencrest for some downtime, the party went house hunting. The two listings were Hobsworth House, a shop where the rooms don't connect the same way twice, and Bellwether House, which was properly haunted. They chose Bellwether. Inside, they found what was left of a family: a mother, Eleanor, trapped by her husband's soul ritual, and their daughter Amelia, a ghost who couldn't bear to be left alone in the dark again. The party reversed the ritual, freed Eleanor, and promised Amelia they'd stay.

How it ended: They moved in. Amelia still writes messages in frost on the windows. Meanwhile Grimhook formalized their next job, the Pendant of Stars, sealing the contract with a soulbinding sigil on Artcoth, and a message in a bottle washed up addressed to Coriac: an SOS from Tjorvi, record-keeper of a ship called the White Gull.

SMALL CRAFT ADVISORY

The Storm Had Opinions

What's going on: Grimhook's contract came with a ship, the White Gull, and a captain who agreed to the voyage on one condition: "If the sea resists, I yield." The sea resisted. Grimvald cast Suggestion on him anyway, and he pressed on without knowing why. The storm got worse in deliberate stages, like it was steering. In the worst of it, Coriac stepped into a lightning bolt to protect the ship and was pulled into a vision of Ariel, a storm goddess with lightning in her eyes. He held out his hand and invited her to speak. She was stunned, and she started to answer. The vision cut out mid-sentence.

How it ended: The Gull ran aground on a black-sand beach. The captain was gone. Undead sailors came out of the surf to fight, and when they started losing they did something undead don't do: they coordinated a retreat, one of them staying behind to cover the rest. The party won and felt terrible about it. Inland, a handful of shipwreck survivors were living behind a fence of rope and bone.

ISLAND LIFE

The Captain Walks Into the Storm

What's going on: The island tested them. A field of standing ship spars hummed a chord that measured their intentions (Shah pulled one out before anyone understood that). A warden with a whalebone scale wouldn't let them pass until Gareth worked out the puzzle and balanced it. In a rotting ship-hull called the Hollowed Hull they found Tjorvi, singing badly and drunk, and Önund, who had declared himself king of a court of junk. Casus won over the court by conjuring a firebird out of the fire and calling it "Tradition." Önund: "My wizard. You have blessed us." When Coriac handed Tjorvi back his own bottle, Tjorvi sat down on the floor: "You came." Artcoth: "Of course we did."

How it ended: In a clearing with Ariel bound in a stone frame, the captain was waiting. He asked Grimvald one question: did you know? Grimvald told the truth. The captain took it like an accounting, not a fight, then walked into Ariel's storm and was gone. Then Coriac's old captain, Edrin Vael, stepped out of the trees. He'd bound Ariel, and he turned into a werebear to keep her that way. When he fell, Ariel was free. She branded her name into Coriac's Forge Shard and left with the storm. The party salvaged a wreck, took on the survivors as crew, and named their new ship the Intimidator. Then they sailed home to Bellwether House, where a brand-new talking door-knocker would only speak to Artcoth.

The Greatest Hits

🗞 140 feet. On the Ironspine road, Artcoth gave the signal and put an arrow through Jimmy Quickhands in the tree line at 140 feet. A note on the body named Coal: "Take care of them." The Circle has been unhappy with Uncle Craig ever since.

🗞 Shah breaks free. A war mage on an underdark bridge charmed Shah and turned him on Coriac. Shah broke the spell himself. Coriac has never once brought it up, which is how you know it was bad.

🗞 Hostile negotiations. Shalindra opened talks by making a duergar slit his own throat, just to show she could. Then she handed over a king's signet ring as a key. The party signed.

🗞 No contest. Between adventures, at a dockside pub, the party challenged the Salt Wreck Four to a drinking contest while Grimvald sang about the underdark. They won so decisively that half of Ravencrest knew the name "Chance Encounters" before the party owned a house, a ship, or a business license. The losers still owe them a favor.

🗞 Asked politely. Casus asked the island's warden nicely to let them pass, then tried to walk around it. The warden threw him across the field. He teleported behind it to investigate. It was immune to charm. He stood down.

🗞 Witness Mjölnir's fury. Against the werebear, Coriac charged in calling on Thor ("Let the thunder break your bonds") and hit for 29 points of damage, leaving the beast one hit from death. Ariel finished it herself.

🗞 Captain Crunch. Rather than tell the White Gull's new captain, Elara, that her old captain was dead, the party disguised Grimvald as him. She saw through it immediately. A follow-up spell to make her stay and listen failed. She sailed off, and the party will not be getting a Christmas card.

🗞 Roasted. Bramwell, the house's new talking door-knocker, roasted Gareth so hard it left a lasting psychic wound. Literally: it took the Obsidian Cypher's healing sequence to close it.

Behind the Screen 🤫

This stays between us. Please do not tell your uncle.

Grimhook was Circle once. The serpent tattooed around her ring finger is the Circle's mark. She left because they found her too distracted and she found them too single-minded. They still want her Liriel artifacts, which is why the Circle hired your family to rob her in the first place.

What she actually wants. Grimhook is collecting Liriel's artifacts to channel Niflheim, the realm of ice and the dead, and to perfect soulbinding: binding souls with surgical precision rather than emotion. Her ring, which detects anyone who intends to betray her, was taken from a seer named Eamon. Keep that in mind next time someone at your table says "she seems nice."

Bellwether House has loose ends. Dr. Bellwether, whose ritual destroyed his family, was never found. The ritual consumed him and nobody located the remains. The party sealed the ritual chamber but kept a stash of soul-jar fragments "for research." And Ovaltine, prying up the old ritual floor, has found the start of a teleportation circle buried underneath it. Somebody built a door in the basement. We don't know where it goes.

Where Things Stand

The party is home at Bellwether House with a crewed ship, a talking door-knocker, and an enchanted quill that writes their marketing. They're planning a gala to put Chance Encounters on the map and have invited half the town, including Grimhook and Lord Thorne. Grimhook stopped checking in a while ago, and the party has decided not to think about that. Thorne wrote asking for help with a missing ward. A letter arrived for Artcoth signed with a flourished "C," the same signature as the original ring job. Casus drank Ovaltine's experimental potion, failed to handle it, and then dropped gravity out of a room with the wrong combination on his cube. He's not doing great. The gala is next. It goes about how you'd expect, and the Session 24 issue has the whole thing.
