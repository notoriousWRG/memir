An ancient observatory built by the Astral Seekers — a society that believed starlight held pure, primordial magic capable of revealing the secrets of fate, time, and reality. Their downfall came when they attempted to harness a falling star. The catastrophic release of unstable energy created a permanent anomaly around the site.

The anomaly distorts time, space, and memory. The remnants of the Astral Seekers' magic still protect it through arcane constructs and illusionary wards.

[[lady-grimhook]] contracted the party to retrieve the [[pendant-of-stars]] from this location. The party has not yet been there.
