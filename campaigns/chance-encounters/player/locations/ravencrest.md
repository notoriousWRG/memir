Port city. Where the party assembled, worked their first job for [[lady-grimhook]], and departed on the White Gull. A working port that doesn't ask questions — commerce, salt, and the particular indifference of a city that's seen everything.

[[bellwether-house]] sits at the edge of the Old Quarter — the party's headquarters. [[ovaltine-jenkins]] runs his shop out of it now and minds the place while the party is away. [[amelia]] keeps it warm.

The party spent downtime here between arcs — establishing contacts, spending coin, and preparing for the voyage. They departed from Ravencrest's docks aboard the White Gull.
