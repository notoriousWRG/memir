Not natural land — this island formed where [[ariel]] was forced to remain still, shaped by intention rather than erosion. The fog doesn't behave like weather. It moves between the trees at a consistent height, like water at low tide, and sound drops away behind you faster than it should.

The island responds to how you approach it. Those who try to control or extract find it resistant. Those who observe and yield find it cooperative. The presence woven into the ground is not neutral — it is aware, and it is watching.

## Key locations

**The beach** — Black sand, wrecked ships, fog. The draugr come from the water; the island absorbs them when they dissolve. The surviving draugr watch from offshore.

**The survivor's camp** — Inland of the wrecks, behind a crude perimeter of rope and bone. Home to [[dessa]], [[marek]], and [[ollo]]. A fire, organized scarcity, people who've been here long enough to stop being scared.

**The Spar Field** — Just past the treeline. Masts and spars from multiple wrecks at different angles; moss grows toward the island's center. Wind through the standing spars produces low, sustained tones — not music, not voices, but the threshold between them.

**The Hollowed Hull** — Mid-island. A ship's skeleton half-swallowed by the forest, old enough that wood and root have become the same thing. Home to [[tjorvi]] and [[onund]]. The cold back room holds four former crewmates frozen mid-reach, the island's answer to what they did.

**The interior** — Deeper than the Hollowed Hull. The fog doesn't enter the inland clearing. The trees at its edge lean away. The air coming out is warmer than it should be and smells of ozone.
