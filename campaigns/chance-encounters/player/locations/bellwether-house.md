The party's headquarters — an alchemist's estate at the edge of [[ravencrest]], abandoned and feared after Dr. Bellwether's soul-ritual destroyed his family from the inside. The party recovered the estate deed for the city guilds; it was signed over free and clear.

They didn't just claim it. They cleansed it — gathered the shattered soul of [[eleanor-bellwether]] and set her free. The house remembers that.

The walls no longer knock. The candlelight that burned in dark windows is quiet and ordinary. What remains is a home that is *almost* at peace: a lullaby down the stairwell, chairs tidied by no one, the smell of lilac through a closed room, the word *thank you* forming in frost on a cold morning. That's [[amelia]], the ghost-child who stayed.

[[ovaltine-jenkins]] has moved Ovaltine's Oddities into the house. He minds the place while the party is away and operates his shop out of it. He only relaxed once he met Amelia.

Resting here is different from resting anywhere else.
