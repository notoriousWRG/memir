An ancient dwarven city now occupied by duergar, built above a vast underground dark lake — [[shalindra-aboleth]]'s domain. The dwarven kings who built it were defeated by Shalindra; [[zal-goroth]] later seized it, poisoned the surrounding land, and bent the minds of those nearby.

The party entered via a passage beneath the lake using a signet ring retrieved from Shalindra's fang. They navigated the city through its ventilation shafts, discovered the treasure room, and solved the throne room statue puzzle. [[casus]] was found in the abandoned library, researching Zal'goroth's history.

The arc resolved at the Soul Forge. Zal'goroth collapsed the mines in a final act of defiance. The party surfaced.

## Notable features

**The dark lake** — Shalindra's domain. A vast underground lake; the aboleth's eyes follow movement beneath the surface.

**The library** — Abandoned. Scattered with historical records of Zal'goroth's conquest. Where [[casus]] was found.

**The throne room** — Ancient dwarven kings carved in the walls. The statue puzzle here opened an escape route.

**The temple** — Active duergar religious site, focused on Zal'goroth worship.
