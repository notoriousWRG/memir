## Where the Party Stands

The party is home at Bellwether House in Ravencrest, planning a success-tour gala about two weeks out. Town is already talking: Grimvald's ballads are in the dockside taverns, and Ovaltine's flyers are out. Thorne has written and the party has replied, so a meeting with him is next. Casus is carrying two levels of exhaustion from forcing Ovaltine's potion, and the Intimidator sits at a full crew of ten with a Bold escort job (frost amber, through the same draugr-haunted waters) waiting for a yes. Lady Grimhook's daily check-ins stopped about a week ago, and nobody has decided what to do about it.
