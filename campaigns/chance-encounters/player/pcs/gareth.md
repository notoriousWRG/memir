Human warlock. He believes his patron is Odin. He travels with [[artcoth]] — an alliance with history behind it.

**Carries:** [[sword-of-shadow-and-flame|Sword of Shadow and Flame]]

## The Patron

Gareth came to his patron in a moment of desperation — separated from his parents, surrounded by cloaked men in Odin's temple. He believes his pact is with Odin.

In the duergar city's underdark temple, the party encountered a statue that bore a resemblance to Gareth. That moment seeded a question neither he nor the party has fully answered.

## The Sword

[[sword-of-shadow-and-flame]] was given to him by his patron as encouragement. It accumulates charges from kills.

## The Spar Field

At the Threshold Warden, Casus took the aggressive approach and the Warden threw him. Gareth read the puzzle — the scale was unbalanced, one pan heaped and one empty. He collected discarded weapons and gear from the ground around the Spar Field and loaded the empty pan. Casus placed a cursed sword from an earlier arc on the scales to bring it level. The Warden stepped aside.

## The Anchor

In the party's running dynamic, Gareth was responsible for the anchor. The anchor is gone. The White Gull is grounded. That equation doesn't resolve yet.

## The Hollowed Hull

Made invisible by Casus before the draugr fight on the beach; carrying a lit sword, currently seen. Went through the sailcloth partition with Artcoth and Casus when Tjorvi said not to.
