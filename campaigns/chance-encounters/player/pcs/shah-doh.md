Half-orc paladin. From the village of Drog, raised in the tradition of Gruumsh. On his 30th nameday, a figure appeared in a dream and told him his faith didn't belong to Gruumsh and that he needed to leave to find where it did. He left.

**Carries:** [[baldrs-gleaming-chain|Baldr's Gleaming Chain]], [[amelias-doll|Amelia's Doll]]

## The Charm Incident

During the Frostveil arc, a war-mage in the underdark charmed Shah Doh and turned him on [[coriac]]. He broke it himself. That moment — the control, the rupture, the recovery — lives in the party's shared history.

## The Dream

The figure who appeared in Shah Doh's dream on his nameday has not been explained. Who that was, and what the dream was pointing him toward, is an open thread.

## Quiet Heroism

Shah Doh doesn't announce what he does under pressure — he just does it. He caught himself on rigging. He hauled Grimvald back from overboard during the grounding. He lit a torch as a secondary beacon in the fog so people could find each other. The list is longer than the people around him realize.
