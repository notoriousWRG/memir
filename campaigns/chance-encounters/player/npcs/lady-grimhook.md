A master enchanter of noble lineage and the party's employer. Calm, precise, transactional — every word is a calculation. A serpent tattoo coils around her ring finger. *"Old loyalties, like old tattoos, fade — but never disappear."*

The party first saw her at Lord Thorne's gala, the night Thorne used a Rod of Rulership to humiliate her in front of his guests. They moved to grab the rod and failed — but in failing, they cleared the gallery long enough for her own agent to get in and out. She emerged from the crowd holding the rod, broke its tip against the floor, recast the whole thing as theatre announcing a new business partnership, and used the rod's last influence to make Thorne confirm the lie. Then she tousled his hair on the way out.

She hired them afterward, eyes open — precisely because they'd proven useful by being *observed* rather than effective. First the bracers from Zal'goroth's exile in the Ironspine. Then the real commission: the Pendant of Stars, hidden in the Starlight Watchtower. That contract is enforced with a soulbinding sigil on [[artcoth]]. A dangerous ally, and a treacherous enemy.
