Twenty at most, still carrying the look of someone who hasn't fully accepted he isn't going home soon. Smart, observant, quietly desperate. He attached himself to [[dessa]] because she doesn't treat uncertainty like a personal failure, and he glances at her before answering anything that matters, even when she isn't in the room.

He has been logging patterns the whole time he's been stranded — wreck positions, who washed up and when, when the draugr come and when they don't (always from the water, never from inland). His crude map has a circle drawn around one stretch of the island marked *"don't go here yet."* He doesn't know what's there. He just knows it feels different.

When the party decided to push inland, Ollo asked to come along — to chart the island and finally get answers to the questions he'd been too afraid to ask. They agreed. He travels with them now, useful for his logs and his map and dangerously naive about what the interior costs. Since the back room of the Hollowed Hull, he hasn't picked up his pen.
