Coriac's old captain — the same crew now wrecked and stranded across this island. Coriac served under him. They were each other's closest conversational and spiritual peers before their paths diverged. He believed storms could be reasoned with, not merely endured.

He chose to work alongside a nature mage whose methods grew dangerous. Their combined work began endangering people. The party intervened, and both were imprisoned. The mage died in custody. Edrin escaped during the aftermath. He and Coriac have not spoken since.

The survivors at the beach camp reported seeing him at the treeline — a silhouette that holds eye contact and then isn't there. Not movement. More like it was never solid. The camp can't agree whether what they see is real. What they agree on: he hasn't come to them.

He is somewhere in the interior. He is watching.
