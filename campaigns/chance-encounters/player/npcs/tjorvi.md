The White Gull's record-keeper, and the one who sent the bottle. The SOS that found [[coriac]] in Ravencrest with his name on it — Tjorvi wrote it, enchanted it, and put it out into the ocean not knowing if it would reach anyone.

The party found him in the Hollowed Hull, deep in the island's interior: gaunt, one eye swollen and infected, far into his cups and singing badly and gloriously. Coriac recognized the song and the voice and sang along from outside. When the bottle came back to him, the drink fell away for a moment and there was just a man who'd thrown a message into the sea. *"You came."* Coriac healed his eye. He needed a minute.

He plays herald to [[onund]]'s court — laughing a half-beat too long at every decree — because as long as the performance keeps going, neither of them has to be where they actually are. He was there when the others took things from the wreck and the forest, and there when the island took them back. He didn't take anything himself.

He told the party what he knew: the captain is inland, deeper. The ones who went into the cold back room looking for things to take didn't come back. *"Don't go in there."* When [[artcoth]] showed him a knotted cord from the hoard, he confirmed it was from their ship, then handed it back. *"Just to be safe — this is a gift."*
