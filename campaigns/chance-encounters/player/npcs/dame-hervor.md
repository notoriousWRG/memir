Came to the gala fully armed and never more than a couple of feet from [[brother-solvi|Brother Sölvi]], with a face that gave nothing away. When Coriac asked about the weapons, she stared at him blankly. Shah recognized her at once as a cleric of the same order as his armor.

She was hostile to Shah from the start. She asked him who he was and why he thought he deserved to wear it, since he didn't even know what it was, then asked in a rote voice, as if she'd said it before: *"What would you not do, even for your friends?"* Shah answered that he wouldn't betray the brethren. She wasn't satisfied, but Sölvi pulled her back, and she followed him, grudgingly, for the rest of the night.
