He sits on a dais of stacked crates inside the Hollowed Hull — the skeleton of a ship half-swallowed by the forest — dressed in layered clothes worn like regalia, spreading his hands for arriving petitioners. He was a cooper on [[coriac]]'s old crew once, barrels and bracing, not banners. When the crew that walked inland started coming apart, Önund declared himself king of the island and dared it to argue. It hasn't, so he's decided that proves the crown.

Bombastic, generous, ridiculous, and one wrong question from coming apart. His "treasury" is a heap of junk — broken buckles, bird bone, sea glass. There's a reason it's only junk. The crewmates who took the *good* things, spoils out of the wreck and living wood off the forest, are in the cold back room now, and they aren't crew anymore. Önund learned the island's price and won't say it aloud: he hoards only what the island doesn't want back, and calls it wealth.

The court works if you play along instead of breaking it. [[casus]] knelt, called the proceedings *tradition*, and raised a firebird from the fire — and Önund's whole posture changed, the weight of someone who'd been waiting a long time for something worth looking at. *"My wizard. You have blessed us. You may ask what you came to ask."* He gave the party what he could: the court has no knowledge of the interior, only a catalogue of what remains. He will not go to the back of the hull, and he will not stop performing — because the performance is the only thing holding.
