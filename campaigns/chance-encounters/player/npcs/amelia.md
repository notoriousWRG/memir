The daughter of the house. A small ghost in a faded dress, eyes a touch too wide and a smile a touch too quick. She died in the slow unraveling of [[bellwether-house]] and simply stayed — not as a frightening haunting, but as a child who was never told it was time to go, and decided she didn't want to be told.

When the party reversed what her father had done and gathered [[eleanor-bellwether|her mother]] whole again, Eleanor ascended. Amelia stayed. *"Mama's resting now."* She keeps the house warm: humming, tidying, fogging the mirrors, watching over the people who come through the door.

The cold of the house followed [[shah-doh]] from the first, and she liked him for it. She pressed [[amelias-doll]] into his hands with both of hers, gravely, and didn't take it back. The party didn't drive her out — they freed her mother and let her keep her home. She loves them for it, in the uncomplicated, slightly alarming way of a lonely child who has decided you are hers now.

- *"Houses need laughter to stay warm."*
- *"If you die here… you can stay in my room."*
- *"Don't worry if the chairs move — that's just me tidying."*
- *"I'll keep watch. Shadows tell me stories."*
