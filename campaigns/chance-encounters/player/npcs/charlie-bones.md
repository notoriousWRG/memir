A tiny undead rat. **Currently with [[coriac]].**

Coriac took him from a necromantic sorcerer who was part of a waylaying party that ambushed the group as they came out of the underdark. Charlie Bones has been with him since.

*HP 1. AC 12. Speed 30 ft., climb 20 ft. Darkvision 60 ft. Resistant to necrotic damage. Immune to the poisoned condition and exhaustion. Understands Common and Undercommon but cannot speak.*

**Undying Skitter.** If reduced to 0 HP, Charlie collapses into a pile of bones and reassembles after 1 minute — unless his bones are scattered or destroyed.

**Undead Scent (1/day).** Senses undeath within 30 feet. Becomes agitated — chittering, spinning in place — when such energy is nearby.

**Pocket Size.** Hides easily in a cloak, pouch, or satchel. Makes no noise unless commanded.

**Creepy Familiarity.** When perched on a shoulder, grants +1 to Intimidation checks against creatures that fear undead, duergar, or necromancy.
