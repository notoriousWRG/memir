A spry halfling wizard, just under three feet tall, with unruly chestnut hair and coppery darting eyes. His patchwork robes are stitched all over with pockets, loops, and dangling charms, sleeves smudged with chalk and the faint sparks of constant tinkering. He smells of singed parchment and tea. Theatrical and fast-talking — he'll shift from sass to lecture mid-sentence — and he sees himself as a mage on the brink of greatness, not *just* a shopkeep. The comic exterior hides real talent.

The party met him the hard way: they tried to steal from him, and his alarm ward caught them. The deal that followed — one scroll, in exchange for right of first refusal on anything interesting or unique they bring him to sell — has held since. His shop, Ovaltine's Oddities, is a small cozy clutter of potions, scrolls, books, and curiosities, guarded by whimsical-but-effective wards and animated objects.

He lowballs his first offer and negotiates up from there, and he has strong opinions about dangerous or soul-touched items. During the party's downtime in Ravencrest, his shop produced a small rift — not sustained, but a threshold crossed, with the party standing right there when it happened.

Since then, he has been enlisted as a partner. He is now at the party's headquarters at the Bellwether estate, setting up shop and working on custom modifications to the house as requested by [[artcoth]].
