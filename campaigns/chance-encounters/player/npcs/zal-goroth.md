Dark shaman, master of blood magic and soul manipulation. Banished from the surface after attempting to usurp a king, he returned beneath the Iron Spine, seized the [[duergar-city]], poisoned the surrounding land, and bent the minds of those nearby. He served as the final obstacle at the end of the Frostveil arc.

The party fought him at the Soul Forge. He collapsed the mines in a final act of defiance. **No body was recovered.** Some believe his spirit still dwells in the ruins beneath the Iron Spine. Others say his mind was bound into a soul jar before the end. His name is still invoked in the deep places.

[[casus]] arrived in the duergar city following Zal'goroth's trail, for reasons of his own.
