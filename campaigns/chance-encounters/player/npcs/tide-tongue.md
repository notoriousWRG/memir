One of the Salt Wreck Four, and the one who reaches for the old stories first. When the storm built around the White Gull, Tide Tongue was the first to name it for what it was: not weather, something intentional. Omens, old stories, and what the sea is saying are the way in.

Now aboard the *Intimidator*, running Weather work. The omens come through best when they're real, and are weakest when everything's calm.
