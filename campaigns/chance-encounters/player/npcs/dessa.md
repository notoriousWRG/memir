Tall, deliberate, sun-weathered. She keeps her hands busy — tying knots, unwinding them, retying. It isn't nerves; it's how she thinks. When she's about to change the subject, she finishes the knot she's working first.

She leads the small group of survivors camped inland of the beach — people who have been on the island long enough to stop being scared and start being organized. They watched the draugr fight from the treeline and did not step in. Her reasoning: *"We've learned not to spend ourselves on the beach. The beach takes everyone eventually. What matters is who's left standing when it's done."*

She knew [[coriac]] years ago — not as a peer, but as a constant, the reliable one who was always there. She was warm with him in the way of someone who has thought about a reunion and decided not to perform it.

When the party pressed her for answers, she held the line — gave them the patterns of the place but not its truth — then finished her knot and went to bed. On her way out she confirmed two things: some of the crew walked inland weeks ago and never came back, and their old captain has been sighted from the treeline, though the camp doesn't agree on whether anyone's really seeing him. Whatever she's still holding back, she hasn't said it yet.
