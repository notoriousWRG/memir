An ancient aboleth who rules the lightless lake beneath the Iron Spine. She calls herself the King-slayer: she defeated the old dwarven monarch and has held the ruins ever since, wearing his signet ring on her fang like a trophy. She speaks directly into the mind — resonant, ancient, contemptuous of hesitation — and she holds the duergar of the city above in open contempt, vermin too frightened to submit to her.

The party met her stranded on the lake island during the Frostveil arc. She surfaced and offered terms: safe passage into the duergar city in exchange for clearing out an infestation she wanted gone. Before they could negotiate, she demonstrated her leverage — a thrall slit his own throat at her command. [[coriac]] talked her around with flattery, which she framed as condescending mercy. She handed over the dwarven signet ring as the key to the lake passage and sent them on with a warning: *"Do not disappoint me."*

Whether the party fulfilled the bargain to her satisfaction was never asked. She has not been heard from since — which is not the same as gone.
