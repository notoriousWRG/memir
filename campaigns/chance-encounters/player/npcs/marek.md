The oldest of the survivors at [[dessa]]'s camp. Quiet in a way that reads as either wisdom or grief depending on the day. He doesn't volunteer information, but he doesn't dodge it either. Watch for the moments he goes still mid-conversation — that's listening, not discomfort. It usually means something true just got said.

He offers food before anything else. Always. When the party walked up out of the surf, Marek fed them stew before anyone explained why they'd come — no calculation in it, just stew. *"You can be angry and hungry at the same time."*

He asks about ships. Not the party's ship in particular — ships in general: who built her, how old she is, what kind of wood. He has some theory he's testing against every wreck that washes up, and he hasn't said it out loud yet.
