The Circle's coordinator, named on the note found on Jimmy's body and behind the letter signed with a flourished "C". The party met him face to face for the first time at the gala. A human in late middle age with graying hair, calm and soft-spoken, he sat down across from Artcoth and Gareth as if the chair had been saved for him and put his card in Artcoth's hand: *"Jimmy always told me you had no vision. I've never agreed with him less."*

He remarked that Lady Grimhook was missing and asked whether it was because they hadn't invited her. When Gareth leaned in on his sleeve, a coiled serpent with barbs behind its head showed at his wrist, and Coal rolled the sleeve up to display it, the same mark as the letter's watermark and the ambushers' tattoos: *"Grimhook had this mark once as well. Did you know that?"* He asked if they knew her plans. Artcoth told him not tonight: make an appointment or knock on the door.

When the crash came from the green he said, *"Sounds like someone may have had a bit too much."* Artcoth warned him he'd regret it if he had anything to do with this. Coal's answer: *"You have no idea."*
