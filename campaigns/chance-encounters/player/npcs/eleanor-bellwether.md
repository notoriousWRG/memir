The lady of [[bellwether-house]] and mother of [[amelia]]. Wife to Dr. Bellwether, the alchemist whose obsession unmade the family. He sought to bind souls to matter, and he used Eleanor as the anchor for his ritual. It failed. Her soul shattered — scattered into fragments through the house — leaving only a wrathful remnant behind, bound to a fractured soul jar beneath the library.

For years she was the haunting: lashing out in psychic fury, a woman's weeping face that could not remember why she screamed. Not evil — desperate, incomplete, and in unending pain.

The party reversed the ritual and returned what had been torn apart, making her whole. [[amelia]] carried the final fragment — a tiny silver flame her mother had hidden with her — and brought it forward. Gathered and at peace, Eleanor ascended.

Her last words to the party: *"Where he sought to divide, you have made whole. Let this house breathe again."* To Amelia: *"You carried my light… my brave girl."*

Her gratitude is woven into Bellwether House itself. The house rests easy now, and protects those who shelter under its roof. Dr. Bellwether's fate remains unaccounted for.
