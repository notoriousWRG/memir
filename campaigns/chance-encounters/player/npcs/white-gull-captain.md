Haunted by a past decision — pressed forward in a storm once, lost a crew member. His governing principle since: *"If the sea resists, I yield."* His condition for this voyage was explicit — if the wind turned them back, they'd turn back.

On the island, in the Circled Zone, he confronted the party about Grimvald's Suggestion — and took the truth without anger, an accounting rather than an explosion. He confirmed Elara was alive and said he was glad the Gull would have a captain. Then he walked into the stone frame at the clearing's center and let the storm take him. Casus tried to stop him and couldn't reach him in time.
