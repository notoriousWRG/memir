Halfling noble. Vain, ambitious, and consistently outmaneuvered. Considered himself a serious collector of power — the Rod of Rulership was his proudest acquisition.

At the gala at his estate, he used the rod to humiliate [[lady-grimhook]] in front of his assembled guests — forced her to kneel, made her speak words of admiration she didn't mean. The crowd laughed. While the party's heist attempt drew Thorne's attention away from the gallery, Grimhook's own agent retrieved the rod. When Grimhook publicly framed the whole night as a staged bit of comedy announcing a new business partnership, Thorne — still under the rod's influence — confirmed the story to his own guests.

He lost the rod, the auction, and his standing in one speech. He has not forgotten any of it.
