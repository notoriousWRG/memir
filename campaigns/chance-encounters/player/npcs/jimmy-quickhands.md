A small-time operator and low-level member of the Circle, and someone [[artcoth]] had known for years — easy familiarity, professional history, mutual wariness. Jimmy was the point of contact for the Ruby Ring job: the Circle hired the party to steal a ring from [[lady-grimhook]], and Jimmy was the one who laid out the table rules.

> *"Man, good to see you. You look... well, older."*
> *"You never did have much vision, Arty."*

He knew the ring carried Liriel's power and warned the party plainly not to put it on. He operated at the level of following orders, not understanding them — reporting up a chain that ran through a conspicuous elf in flamboyant clothes to a higher figure named Coal.

When the party sided with Grimhook instead of delivering the ring, Jimmy brought backup to the road camp at night — a half-orc thug and a halfling archer — and made his ask: the ring, or a hand. [[artcoth]] gave the signal. Jimmy put [[gareth]] down before Coriac revived him, then tried to run. Artcoth put an arrow through him at 140 feet. An encrypted note on his body, once decoded, pointed back to Coal: *"Enough excuses. I've been patient enough. Take care of them."*
