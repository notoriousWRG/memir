An older priest, warm and unhurried, with a kindly smile and a nod for everyone. He introduced himself at the gala as a cleric of Thor, "one of Thor's guardians," and recognized the marks on Coriac's armor and hammer, thanking him for what he does for the community. He came for Shah: Brother Ogle, a priest at Baldr's temple, had told him an unknown member of their order had come by.

He touched the hem of Shah's chain mail and it glimmered for him the way it does for Shah. [[baldrs-gleaming-chain|The armor]] was left at the temple, he said, by one of the Dawnbound's highest knights, who said only that someday someone would need it. He didn't say who, and wouldn't. When [[dame-hervor|Hervor]] pressed Shah, he held her back: the armor wasn't stolen, it was Shah's by right. After that he went off to enjoy the wine and to hear Grimvald play.
