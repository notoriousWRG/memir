First Mate of the White Gull. Professional and watchful — her trust is hard-won and her judgment is delivered through posture, not speech.

Early friction with [[artcoth]] over his conduct on the crossing. That shifted when he made a clutch save involving her.

After the White Gull grounded on black sand, she returned to the ship with [[wrenn]] to assess hull damage and keep the remaining crew together while the party moved inland.
