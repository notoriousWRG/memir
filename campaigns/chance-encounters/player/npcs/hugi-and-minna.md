type: npc

---

A tall man and a small woman in feathered black cloaks, with black irises that held a strange brightness. They barely blinked and never together, and one would start a sentence and the other finish it. Bramwell never announced them and no one saw them arrive: only Shah and Coriac caught the hem of the cloaks slipping through the door, and Shah's dagger did not stir. Coriac sensed that a human form wasn't their natural one.

*"We book performers,"* he said, *"for a hall in the far north,"* she finished. They asked Grimvald for a verse, and he sang a piece of the party's adventures. They weren't listening to the words. They were listening beneath them. *"It is him,"* the woman said quietly. The man dropped a black feather into Grimvald's hand, and as they left: *"Everyone sings for him eventually."*

On their way out they stopped at Gareth's sword. *"That's not ours."* *"It was, once."* Outside, two ravens flew off, and two plain feathers fell.

**The party's theory:** Odin's ravens, Huginn and Muninn (Thought and Memory), who fly the world and report back to the All-Father everything they see. Unconfirmed.
