The wreck the party claimed at Ariel's island, hull tarred black end to end. [[coriac]] captains her, [[onund|Önund]] serves as first mate and bosun, [[tjorvi]] as ship's bard, [[ollo]] navigating, and [[marek]] running the galley — the post he'd been quietly hoping for since the survivors' camp. Still known dockside as the Black Gull to some, a nod to her predecessor.

## Ship Specs

Roughly a 70-ft. gargantuan hull, two masts, ~50 tons of cargo space.

- **Full crew:** 10
- **Minimum to sail:** 4

Sailing lean (down to 6 hands) costs nothing but flavor. Down to 4–5, she's stretched thin — every roll takes a hit, and the bigger, riskier missions are off the table until she's crewed back up. Below 4, she doesn't sail at all.

## Mission Mechanic

Between sessions, the Intimidator can be sent out on a mission instead of sailing with the party — a trade run, an escort job, a smuggling errand, a salvage dive, a storm-chasing haul.

**Pick the kind of job:** Trade, Escort, Intel, Salvage, or Weather.

**Pick how much to risk:**

| Tier | Risk | Payout |
|---|---|---|
| Milk Run | ~50 gp | ~100–150 gp |
| Standard | ~125 gp | ~250–400 gp |
| Bold | ~250 gp | ~500–800 gp, sometimes a rare good or a lead |
| Reckless | however much it takes | could be anything |

**Put someone on point** — whichever crew member's specialty matches the job. Their bonus is what's riding on the roll, and if it goes bad, it's their name on the complication. Support crew like Tjorvi can ride along for free, no matter the job.

**Roll to see how it lands:**

- **Bust** — the stake's gone, and a complication follows the party into a future session
- **Break-even** — the stake comes back, nothing gained, nothing lost
- **Weak Success** — the stake comes back plus a smaller payout, or the ship's tied up a while longer finishing the job
- **Success** — the stake back plus the full payout
- **Strong Success** — full payout plus something extra — bonus coin, a favor, a lead

The better the specialist's bonus fits the job, and the more crew aboard, the better the odds. Sending her out short-handed or into a job nobody's suited for is how a milk run turns into a problem.
