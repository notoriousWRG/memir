All six of the party call it home now, each with a room of their own — not a base they visit between jobs, but where they actually live. [[ovaltine-jenkins]] lives there too, running Ovaltine's Oddities out of the house, and [[amelia]] and [[bellwether-door-knocker|Bramwell]] round out the household.

**The deal with Ovaltine:** workshop space and caretaker's rights, in exchange for fronting the party's gear — and in return, the party gets a standing 10% discount at Ovaltine's Oddities, plus first look at whatever "edge" magic he's tinkering with before it ever reaches the shop floor.

## Resting here

A long rest at Bellwether House leaves you steadier for it — the cleansing that freed [[eleanor-bellwether|Eleanor]] left something behind, and the renovation added more on top. At the end of a long rest here, you get both:

**Always on:**
- **True Rest** — regain one additional spell slot of 3rd level or lower, once per long rest.
- **Warm Walls** — immune to fear effects while resting here; advantage on the first Insight/Perception check to notice something wrong within the walls.
- **Steeped in Soulwork** — +1 to Arcana checks made inside the house, especially anything to do with soulbinding or undeath.

**Choose one, added with the renovation:**
- **Anchored Mind** — advantage on one Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma save before your next long rest. Especially good against charm, fear, confusion, possession, memory alteration, or illusion.
- **Prepared Action** — advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw of your choice before your next long rest. Declare before rolling.
- **Steeled Resolve** — temporary hit points equal to proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier (minimum 1), lasting until depleted or your next long rest.
- **Focused Insight** — advantage on the first check made with one skill or tool proficiency of your choice, before your next long rest.

Word travels fast in a house with staff — servants, locals, and contacts already know your names.
