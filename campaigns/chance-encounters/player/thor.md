God of storms and thunder, and [[coriac]]'s patron since long before Chance Encounters began. Coriac spent his first century at sea devoted to him — and carries [[mjolnirs-fury]], a plain warhammer that has held Thor's blessing quietly for years.

That blessing crystallized during the storm crossing, when Coriac planted himself in the path of a lightning strike to protect the ship. Thor gave him a second gift too: the [[forge-shard]], worked by Coriac's own hand at a sacred forge — charged with potential, but left unfinished. *Thor gave him potential. He hasn't finished earning it yet.*
