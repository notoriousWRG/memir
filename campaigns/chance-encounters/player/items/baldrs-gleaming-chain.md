Radiant chain mail woven from links that shimmer with soft, sunlit warmth. Worn only by those who have taken the Oath of the Dawnbound, servants of [[baldr]] — the Lightborn, the Gentle Heart. **Currently carried by [[shah-doh]].**

This armor symbolizes clarity, compassion, and the gentle strength of Baldr's light.

## Properties

**Radiant Pulse (1/long rest).** As a bonus action, shed bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. Choose up to three creatures within the bright light; each chosen creature gains advantage on its next saving throw made within the next minute.

**Baldr's Endurance.** Once per combat, when you are reduced to half your hit point maximum or lower, you gain 5 temporary hit points.

**Light of the Dawnborn.** While wearing this armor, any bright or dim light you create cannot be magically suppressed by spells or effects of 2nd level or lower.
