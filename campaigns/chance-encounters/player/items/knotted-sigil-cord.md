A short length of cord worked into a knotted sigil — a sailor's ward-knot, the kind tied against drowning, foul luck, and worse things the sea keeps. **Currently carried by [[artcoth]].** [[artcoth]] pulled it from [[onund]]'s hoard inside the Hollowed Hull: junk all around it, but this one had held up better than everything else. [[tjorvi]] recognized it — *"This is from our ship"* — held it a moment, then handed it back. *"Just to be safe — this is a gift."*

That it survived intact when everything else in the hoard rotted is the tell. The ward held.

## Properties

**Sailor's Luck (once per day).** While the cord is carried, when the bearer rolls a 1 on an ability check or a saving throw, they can reroll it and must use the new result. Recharges at dawn.

**Break the Knot (one use, then inert).** As a reaction, when the bearer or a creature within 5 feet drops to 0 HP — or fails a save against being grappled, restrained, paralyzed, or petrified — they can break the cord. The triggering effect fails: damage that would drop them leaves them at 1 HP instead, or the condition doesn't take hold, and they may immediately move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Then the knot unravels into frayed string and nothing more. It works exactly once.
