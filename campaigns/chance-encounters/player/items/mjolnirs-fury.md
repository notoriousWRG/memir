A warhammer with a worn grip and simple ironwork. **Currently carried by [[coriac]].**

Coriac found it in the underdark on the body of a dead paladin — a servant of [[thor]] who had been cut down before the party arrived. They avenged the paladin's death, and with that, Thor's blessing passed to Coriac along with the weapon. After the lightning strike on the crossing to Ariel's island, a rune of protection seared itself into the handle where he held it. It looked the same after the strike as before. That's the point.

During the storm crossing, Coriac planted himself in the path of a direct strike to protect the ship and crew. The hammer absorbed it. Thor's latent blessing, already present in the weapon, crystallized in that moment — not as a gift, but as recognition.

*The storm did not break him. It recognized him.*

## Properties

**+1 Warhammer.** +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

**Storm's Judgment (1/long rest).** Call down a bolt of divine lightning on a target you can see within 60 feet. The target makes a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 lightning damage on a failed save, or half on a success.

**Wrath of the Stormforged (1/short rest).** When a creature within 5 feet hits Coriac with a melee attack, he can use his reaction to unleash retaliatory lightning. The attacker takes 1d6 lightning damage, and Coriac briefly glows with divine sparks until the start of his next turn.

**Stormrod (1/long rest).** When Coriac deliberately places himself in the path of a lightning effect aimed at himself or an ally, he can use his reaction to absorb the strike entirely, taking no damage. Until the start of his next turn, his next weapon attack deals an extra 2d6 lightning damage and he sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius.

The power only triggers on a voluntary, declared choice to take the hit — never on a strike that simply happens to land. It is a mechanic that only works through sacrifice.
