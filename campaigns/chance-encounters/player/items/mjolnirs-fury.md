A warhammer with a worn grip and simple ironwork — no runes, no visible enchantment. [[coriac]] has carried it since his early devotion to [[thor]], and it looked exactly the same after the lightning strike as it did before. That's the point.

During the storm crossing to Ariel's island, Coriac planted himself in the path of a direct strike to protect the ship and crew. The hammer absorbed it. Thor's latent blessing, already present in the weapon, crystallized in that moment — not as a gift, but as recognition.

*The storm did not break him. It recognized him.*

## Properties

**+1 Warhammer.**

**Stormrod (once per long rest).** When Coriac deliberately places himself in the path of a lightning effect aimed at himself or an ally, he can use his reaction to absorb the strike entirely, taking no damage. Until the start of his next turn, his next weapon attack deals an extra 2d6 lightning damage and he sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius.

The power only triggers on a voluntary, declared choice to take the hit — never on a strike that simply happens to land. It is a mechanic that only works through sacrifice.
