A glass vial of faintly luminous liquid threaded with silver filaments. The light pulses in response to unseen rhythms rather than glowing steadily. Brewed by [[ovaltine-jenkins]], who would privately describe it as a field test.

**Drunk by [[casus]] in S23. The dose is gone and can't be taken back.**

Ovaltine's note on the label: *"This does not grant power. It provokes what is already present."*

## What it did

A rising hum through his chest and limbs, half-formed runes at the edge of his vision. He failed the Constitution save and blacked out for half a minute, then failed the follow-up Arcana check by two. The label's fine print turned out different from what he feared: no hard collapse waiting at three failures. Each failure costs a level of exhaustion, and it all clears once he gets through. He's at two levels and hasn't had a success yet.
