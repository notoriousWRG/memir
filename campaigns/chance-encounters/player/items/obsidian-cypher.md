A small black cube, palm-sized, carved with archaic dwarven runes on each face. Heavy for its size, cold to the touch even in a warm room. Six faces, six runes — and pressing them in the right sequences does things a box this small has no business doing. **Currently carried by [[casus]].**

## What the party knows

The cube is believed to have belonged to Thalgrin Deepforge, an exiled dwarven archmage. Beyond that, its origins are uncertain — a trail of half-truths and embellished legend. Casus uncovered three records in the library near where the Cypher was originally found in the underdark.

---

*From a record titled "The Black Relic of the Deep":*

> "Discovered in the lower tunnels of the South Hollow Caverns, the relic's unearthly design unsettled even the bravest miners. Those who touched it whispered of voices — cold and cryptic — gnawing at their minds. One poor soul, Durm Steelvein, was found ranting madly about 'shackles in the dark' before hurling himself into the cavern chasm. The artifact was retrieved carefully and sealed in the citadel, passed to the learned scholars of the Arcanum Forge. It is believed to bear the mark of the exiled Archmage Thalgrin Deepforge, though none can claim certainty as to its origin or purpose."

---

*From the Arcanum archive:*

> "Heart, Shield, Fist... An alignment of light and steel... curious, yet fleeting. The resulting magic healed my wounded apprentice's hand, but at what cost? The cube — no, the Cipher — sang as it drew power. I dare not try further."

---

*From a history of Thalgrin Deepforge — accounts conflicting, embellished, unreliable:*

> "Thalgrin Deepforge, the Betrayed Hoarder, was once the brightest runesmith of Svartalfheim. Some claim he was a genius, others a thief who hoarded artifacts for himself."

> "Banished for his arrogance, it is said Thalgrin retreated into the darkest reaches of the earth. Rumors speak of a vault — a pocket realm forged by his hand, guarded by riddles and traps only he could solve."

> "Legends claim he defied death itself, sealing his soul within his greatest creation. Whether it is truth or mere dwarven boasts, none can say. Yet Thalgrin's name remains whispered where the air grows cold, and the dark presses close."

> "His runes are said to be the key — to what, only fools and the doomed still search."
