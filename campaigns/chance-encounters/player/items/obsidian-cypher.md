A small black cube, palm-sized, carved with archaic dwarven runes on each face. Heavy for its size, cold to the touch even in a warm room. Six faces, six runes — and pressing them in the right sequences does things a box this small has no business doing. [[casus]] carries it.

## Origin

Thlgrin Deepforge was a dwarven archmage of Svartalfheim — renowned, then paranoid, then exiled. He spent decades filling this cube with his most dangerous possessions: treasures, monsters, weapons, forbidden knowledge. To power it, he captured six immortal beings and bound one to each rune face, drawing on their essence. Cornered at last, he triggered the cube's stasis function, cast it into an underground chasm, and disappeared inside it. Centuries later dwarven miners cut it out of the rock, and it passed through many hands before reaching the party.

## What it holds

Each of the six runes — heart, shield, book, lightning, fist, eye — is bound to one of the imprisoned: a deceived dryad, a tricked celestial dwarf, the soul of a mage who sought immortality and got a cage, a thunder giant, a frost giant, a shadow fey. The right sequences call on their power for healing, protection, knowledge, lightning, strength, or true sight. Each use costs the bound one something, and their cries are briefly audible when a sequence opens.

The longest sequence opens a portal into a pocket dimension — Thlgrin's hidden vault of hoard, lore, and monsters, with the archmage himself waiting inside in stasis. It is a ward Casus carries, and one he may not yet fully understand.
