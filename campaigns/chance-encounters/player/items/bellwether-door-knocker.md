A brass knocker in the shape of a wide, whiskered face — [[ovaltine-jenkins|Ovaltine]]'s work, and he's proud of it. Its eyes are cut glass, and they blink. It doesn't knock so much as announce.

**Bramwell**, as it introduced itself, over Ovaltine's objections that it hadn't earned a name yet. Vain about its own craftsmanship, chatty with anyone who'll listen, and quietly convinced it's the most important fixture in the house. It only speaks to [[artcoth]] — everyone else it sizes up in silence, eyes narrowing or widening, before deciding whether to let the latch give.

## What it does

If someone at the door is wearing a magical disguise, an illusion, or isn't who they appear to be, Bramwell sees straight through it and won't open — instead announcing, loudly and unhelpfully specific, exactly what it sees underneath. It has no opinion about lies that aren't magical.

It gossips about who's been in and out, whether asked or not, and is genuinely offended by rudeness to the doorstep — not wiping your feet, not waiting to be announced. It will not stop talking about Ovaltine's craftsmanship, even in front of Ovaltine.
