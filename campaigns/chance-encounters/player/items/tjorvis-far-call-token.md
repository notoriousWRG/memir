Two halves of one bone chip, threaded on the same knot-pattern cord [[tjorvi]] ties into everything that matters to him. He split it clean down a natural crack — the kind sailors call luck, or a sign the thing was already meant to be two. Given to [[coriac]] at the parting on the beach, the same gesture as the [[knotted-sigil-cord]], aimed forward this time instead of back.

## Properties

**Far-Call (1/day).** Speak a short phrase and Tjorvi hears it, a beat later, wherever he is — as long as he's somewhere in the same coastal region. It doesn't reach across open ocean or planes. No ritual, no casting. Just two halves that remember being one piece.
