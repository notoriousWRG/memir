A slim, water-stained book bound in bark-tanned leather, singed at the corners. The pages mix precise arcane notation crossed out and rewritten in a looser, older hand — roots and thorn-shapes in the margins, like a man arguing with himself in two languages. Dark-touched, druidic-leaning magic: the kind [[edrin-vael|Edrin]] turned to once he stopped trusting anything that could be reasoned with. It doesn't open easily. Whatever's in it wants to be earned, not skimmed.

## What's been decoded so far

[[casus]] has been the one working through it. He's copied *Bramblebind* — a cousin to *Spike Growth* — into his own spellbook, and picked up Edrin's own notes on how a magical binding is built and held. The rest is still locked.
