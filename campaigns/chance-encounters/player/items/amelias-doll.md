A small rag doll, hand-stitched by a child's hands — careful, crooked seams and two mismatched glass button eyes, one amber and one cloudy blue. It smells of lilac and cold hearth-ash. When something dead or ill-meaning draws near, the blue eye catches light that isn't there and glimmers faintly, and the stitching across its chest draws taut, as if the doll is holding its breath.

[[amelia]] made it herself, back when there was still someone to make things for. She gave it to [[shah-doh]] the way a child surrenders her most-precious thing: gravely, both hands, and no taking it back. *"You can hold her when you're scared. She doesn't mind. She's used to the dark."*

It is a gift, and it is grateful, and it is never *quite* still.

## Properties

**Hush-Now Lullaby (1/long rest).** As a bonus action, the attuned bearer may clutch the doll close. They hear Amelia's lullaby hummed soft and backwards, just at the edge of hearing, and gain advantage on the next Wisdom saving throw they make within the next 10 minutes.

**Button-Eye Watch.** While attuned, the doll's blue eye warms whenever undead or open hostility is near. The bearer has advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks to sense hostile intent, deception, or coercion directed at them, and cannot be surprised by undead they can see.
