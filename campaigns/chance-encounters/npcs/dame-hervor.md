Served the Oath of the Dawnbound for years and was never given the chain. Wants to know why an unsworn half-orc was. Tests [[shah-doh]] with a question, not a duel: *"What would you not do, even for your friends?"* A real answer earns her respect; a glib one earns a formal challenge later.
