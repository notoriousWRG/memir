Served the Oath of the Dawnbound for years and was never given the chain. Wants to know why an unsworn half-orc was. Tests [[shah-doh]] with a question, not a duel: *"What would you not do, even for your friends?"* A real answer earns her respect; a glib one earns a formal challenge later.

See [[brother-solvi]], [[baldrs-gleaming-chain]].

## Stat block (S25 — the green)

Reskinned from **Knight** (MM, CR 3). Medium humanoid, lawful good. **AC** 18 (Dawnbound plate, gold-edged, polished to a mirror). **HP** 52. **Speed** 30 ft. STR 16 · DEX 11 · CON 14 · INT 11 · WIS 11 · CHA 15. Senses: passive Perception 10. Condition immunity: frightened (while her aura is up).

- **Multiattack.** Two greatsword attacks (+5, 2d6+3 slashing).
- **Leadership (recharge after a short or long rest).** For 1 minute she can utter a command; any ally within 30 ft who hears her adds 1d4 to attacks and saves.
- **Parry.** Reaction: +2 AC against one melee attack she can see.
- **Dawnlit Blade** *(homebrew)*. Her greatsword sheds dim light. **Three times per fight**, a hit deals an extra **1d8 radiant**, warm gold-white light, not wrath. Radiant damage from her **stops the serpent's head regrowth** like Shah's.
- **The Gentle Heart** *(homebrew)*. A 10-ft aura of soft sunlight. Allies inside can't be frightened. Undead and fiends in it have disadvantage on attacks against her.
- **Lightborn Step** *(homebrew; bonus action, once per fight)*. She crosses up to 30 ft in a line of dawn-light to a space adjacent to an ally who is below half HP or in grave danger. This is how she drops back to Sölvi.

**Her test, in action.** She watches Shah. If he steps between the serpent and a guest, or takes a head meant for a friend, that is an answer to her question and earns her respect. If he abandons a straggler to chase the kill, she says nothing and notes it.

**Tactics.** She holds the line on the stragglers, not on the monster. She engages the serpent only to cover a guest or Sölvi. She trusts Shah to be the hammer and takes the shield's job.

## S25 — played

Ran across the water leaving white footprints, hit with a natural 20, and her blade took on the same light as Shah's smite and withered a head. Used Leadership on Coriac. She and Shah traded smirks mid-fight. She serves Baldr, like Sölvi and Shah.
