Prodigious drow sorceress and artificer, descended from Hel through Gromph Baenre, archmage of the most powerful house of Svartalfheim. Originally trained as a priestess of Skadi; secretly pursued arcane experimentation, blending divine essence and soulbinding into powerful artifacts.

Founder and ruler of Nimbral, a magically dominated surface kingdom. Mastermind behind the Frostveil, a set of six soul-infused artifacts collectively known as "Liriel's Loom." Disappeared after a mysterious wanderer sought an audience in Nimbral — no confirmed sightings since.

[[casus]]'s origin quest centers on the Ring of Arcane Insight.

## Relevance to the party

Casus is somehow connected to Liriel's legacy — by blood, destiny, or both. Zal'goroth was her advisor and co-developer of the artifacts. Lady Grimhook considers Liriel's methods crude and seeks to perfect them.
