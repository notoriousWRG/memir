Recognizes [[baldrs-gleaming-chain]] as a Dawnbound relic and came to meet whoever it went to. The chain was not given out the usual way: it was requested, years ago, by a Dawnbound knight, for someone who would need it. He does not give the knight's name. How much he knows is the DM's call.

Travels with [[dame-hervor]].

See [[session-024-prep]], [[shah-doh]], [[baldr]].

## S25 — the green

Reskinned from **Priest** (MM, CR 2): **AC** 13, **HP** 27, WIS 13 (+1 spell mod; DC 11). Not a fighter. He spends the first rounds blessing, and then he is in trouble. Dagger +2 (1d4) if cornered.

**Sun-disc blessings** *(homebrew)*. Three uses total, as an action, range 30 ft, **one willing creature per use, up to three PCs over the fight** (his pick, based on who looks hurt or reckless). He touches his sun-disc and says the name of the one he blesses. Each blessing lasts until the end of that creature's next turn:
- **Warding Light.** 10 temporary HP and advantage on the next save against being grappled, restrained, or dragged (the tide-spawn's Whelm).
- **Sunlit Strike.** The creature's next weapon or spell hit also deals +1d8 radiant damage. **This stops the serpent's regrowth**, so it opens the radiant gate to the whole party. *Dial: strike it if the fight feels too easy.*
- **Mercy.** End one condition on the target (charmed, frightened, prone-and-pinned, poisoned) or revive them from 0 HP with 1 HP.

**The danger.** After his third blessing (or at round 3, whichever comes first), the tide-spawn's undertow or a stray head goes for him. He is old and slow, and he doesn't run. He just stops, touches the disc, and waits. This pulls **Dame Hervor to him** (Lightborn Step) and out of the front line, which is the reason she drops back. If the party saves him, he is grateful, warm, and a little more honest about the knight.

## S25 — played

Cast Bless on Hervor, Gareth, and Shah, then used Sunlit Strike on the brood itself. Failed two Strength saves against the tide-spawn, which restrained and dragged him under. Coriac parted the water with Control Water so he could breathe; the elemental released him when the brood died. He owes Coriac. He serves Baldr, like Hervor and Shah.
