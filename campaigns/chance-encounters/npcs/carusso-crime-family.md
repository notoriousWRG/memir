A powerful and ruthless criminal organization operating in Ravencrest and surrounding cities. Involved in gambling, drug trafficking, racketeering, and contract violence. They are not the Circle, but they operate in the same ecosystem of money and silence.

## Leadership

**Angela and Giuseppe Carusso** — siblings, known as "The Angel and The Butcher." Angela runs the business; Giuseppe handles the enforcement.

**Vincenzo (Boss)** — loyal uncle, acts as underboss and adviser.

**Salvatore (Big Sal)** — cousin, lawyer, consigliere.

## Operations

**Marco "The Hammer" Moretti** — capo overseeing gambling operations.

**Gina "The Widow" Moretti** — capo overseeing trafficking.

**Tony "The Rat"** — enforcer under Gina.

**Leon "Tunder" Lightfoot** — enforcer under Marco.

## Known presence

The party encountered a Carusso-connected operative ("Scarface") watching Aimon Young at the Lucky Coin. The organization was keeping tabs on the guard with the gambling problem — either running him or watching for leverage.

The Carusso family's interest in the party is not established. Their interest in Aimon, and their proximity to the same circles as the Circle, makes them relevant background pressure in Ravencrest.
