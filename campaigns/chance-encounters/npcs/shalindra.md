Ancient aboleth who claimed dominion over the dark lake beneath the Iron Spine. Self-titled "the King-slayer" — she defeated the ancient dwarven monarch and has held the ruins ever since, collecting the signet ring as a trophy. Holds deep contempt for the duergar who occupy the city above, calling them vermin too frightened to submit to her influence.

## The pact

When the party was stranded on the lake island during the Frostveil arc, Shalindra emerged and offered a deal: safe passage into the duergar city in exchange for clearing out the infestation. [[coriac]] negotiated — flattery worked, which Shalindra framed as condescending mercy.

She gave the party the dwarven signet ring as a key to the lake passage, demonstrated her leverage by commanding a thrall to slit his own throat, and sent them forward with the warning: *"Do not disappoint me."*

## Current status

The pact was entered. Whether it was fulfilled, and what Shalindra considers the outcome, is part of the campaign's unresolved ledger.
