Tiny undead rat. HP 1. Reassembles itself one minute after being reduced to 0 HP — unless its bones are scattered or destroyed. Senses undead within 30 feet and becomes agitated (chittering, spinning in place) when such energy is nearby. Makes no noise unless commanded. Can hide easily in a cloak, pouch, or satchel.

When perched on a shoulder, grants +1 to Intimidation checks against creatures that fear undead, duergar, or necromancy.

Belongs to [[ovaltine-jenkins]].
