Tiny undead rat. Belongs to [[coriac]]. Taken from a necromantic sorcerer who was part of a waylaying party that ambushed the group on their way out of the underdark. Coriac claimed him after that fight.

## Stat Block

| | |
|---|---|

| **Speed** | 30 ft., climb 20 ft. |
| **Resistances** | Necrotic |
| **Condition Immunities** | Poisoned, Exhaustion |
| **Senses** | Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10 |
| **Languages** | Understands Common and Undercommon; cannot speak |

## Special Traits

**Undying Skitter.** If reduced to 0 HP, Charlie collapses dramatically into a pile of bones — but reassembles himself after 1 minute unless his bones are scattered or destroyed.

**Undead Scent (1/day).** Can sense the presence of undeath within 30 feet. Becomes agitated — chittering, spinning in place — when such energy is nearby.

**Pocket Size.** Can hide easily in a cloak, pouch, or satchel. Makes no noise unless intentionally commanded.

**Creepy Familiarity.** When perched on a shoulder, grants +1 to Intimidation checks against creatures that fear undead, duergar, or necromancy.
