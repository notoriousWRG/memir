Master enchanter from a noble lineage of magical scholars. A prodigy who surpassed her peers and turned to manipulation and darker paths in pursuit of power. Loyal and influential member of the Order of the Black Star. Dangerous ally; treacherous enemy.

%%
## Motivation

Grimhook is attempting to harness the [[liriel-baenre]] Frostveil artifacts as conduits to Niflheim's essence. She considers Liriel's use of soulbinding magic crude and chaotic — she wants to perfect it, binding souls with surgical precision instead of emotional fervor. Her ideal future mirrors Niflheim: lawful control, static order, no room for chaos.

The Order of the Black Star does not share her ambitions; they view the Frostveil artifacts as fuel or toys, not world-reshaping instruments. Their contempt for her leads them to send agents after her or the party more to annoy and punish than to win anything strategic.
%%

## Connection to the party

Contracted [[artcoth]] (primary contact) to retrieve the Pendant of Stars from the Starlight Watchtower. Contract is enforced with a soulbinding sigil on Artcoth. Reward negotiated on completion.

## Current move — as of S21

She has not been idle while the party was on the island, and she is not waiting anymore. The Order's Circle is pressing her directly for the Pendant — their patience with her personal timeline is gone, and when the party ignored her summons, that decided it: she is going after it herself rather than sending an agent.

She's left the manor arranged to look occupied. She'd already quietly dismissed all non-magical staff, so the place reads the same amount of deserted as always — but now it's layered with wards and small enchantments (a light in the right window at the right hour, a warmth in the study, the sense of someone just having stepped out) built to make it seem like she's still there, working. Anyone who actually gets inside will find it empty.

**Why this matters for the party:** she is now in motion on the same artifact they may still be entangled with (Artcoth's sigil), off-screen and ahead of them, not a patient patron waiting on their schedule anymore.

## Secrets

Possesses the Cloak of Starry Veil (Frostveil artifact). The Ring of Arcane Insight she wields was taken from Eamon the Seer. Her connection to the Watchtower is not purely strategic — there is grief in it.

**She was Circle once.** The serpent tattoo on her ring finger is the Circle's mark. She left because they found her too distracted, and she found them too single-minded. The Circle still wants her Liriel items. Established S24 prep (DM call); no one at the table knows yet.

## Hooks — base set for next arc

- **The Pendant chase.** She's already moving on the Starlight Watchtower herself. The party could race her there, catch up after the fact (success or failure — decide when it's relevant, not now), or find out what it cost her to go alone.
- **The soulbinding sigil on Artcoth.** Still live, still hers to pull. If the party stalls, crosses her, or gets in her way, this is the pressure point — not violence, just a reminder of what she can do with it.
- **The manor mystery.** See below — a self-contained investigation hook if the party goes looking for her instead of waiting to be found.
- **The Guest-Right.** If the party takes any of the six gifts from the manor, she has a quiet line into whoever's carrying it — surveillance, or in a couple of cases something costlier tucked into the same generosity. Not revealed unless they go looking, or unless she chooses to cash it in later. See [[the-guest-right]].
- **The Order's contempt.** The Order of the Black Star views her ambitions as beneath them and might approach the party directly to work against her — a wedge, playing both sides, testing where the party's loyalty actually sits.
- **Liriel's ring.** Still hers, still wanted by Cole (Nicholas)/the Circle. An old debt that never closed — could resurface as leverage from either direction.

## If the party visits the manor

No combat wards, no alarms meant to bar or hurt anyone who walks in — she isn't defending the place against people who actually get inside. But it isn't undefended information-wise either: she left something sharper than the occupancy illusions, tuned specifically to *them*. See below.

**What the occupancy wards do:** a light in the right window at the right hour, warmth in the study, the sense of someone having just stepped out — small, low-key enchantments meant for anyone watching from the street, not for people who actually walk in. They hold up until someone's actually inside and paying attention.

**The Hostess's Eye.** A scrying ward keyed into the front hall threshold, tied to a paired sensor she carries. She isn't watching live, minute to minute — she's at the Watchtower, working the Pendant — but the instant living creatures cross fully into the hall, the ward completes, and she knows, whenever she next has a spare thought to notice it. **Trigger:** a medallion set into the hall floor, DC 14 Perception/Investigation to clock as more than decoration before stepping past it; Detect Magic anywhere in the house picks it up immediately. It isn't hidden to catch anyone off guard — it's just a fact about the house, noticed or not.

**What fires automatically — the Guest-Right.** A side room that reads as a locked linen closet unseals: a table inside, six items wrapped and labeled in her small, precise hand — one name each, Artcoth, Casus, Coriac, Gareth, Grimvald, Shah Doh. A standing glass above the table clouds and clears to a still image of her — a message prepared before she left, not a live conversation:

> "If you're reading this, you let yourselves in, which tells me something I already suspected. I don't begrudge it — I'd have done the same. Call these a peace offering, or professional courtesy, whichever sits easier. Take them or don't. I'd rather you owed me a kindness than a grudge, when we next speak."

Full contents, effects, and the charm hidden in each gift: [[the-guest-right]].

**What's actually there, beyond the parlor:** an empty house, clean and undisturbed, the non-magical staff long since dismissed. Travel gear is missing from what would otherwise be a fully appointed wardrobe — she packed for a real journey, not a quick errand.

**What can be found, if searched:**
- A locked study (a simple mechanical lock or a mundane Arcana-adjacent ward, DM's call on DC) — inside, a travel itinerary sketched toward the Starlight Watchtower, confirming where she's gone without saying why.
- Something that lands the grief thread physically — a portrait, a letter, or a keepsake tied to the Watchtower predating her current ambitions. Not an explanation, just weight. Pairs with the existing Insight/Arcana DCs on her Ring of Arcane Insight (see Secrets, below) if the party ever presses her on it directly later.
- No sign of the Pendant, the Cloak of Starry Veil, or anything actively dangerous — she took what mattered and left the rest.

**Consequence, not danger.** The moment the ward fires, she knows they've been inside — and, given time, which gifts they kept. That's a live fact for later scenes, not resolved here; she may reference it, use it, or say nothing and let it sit. This is still a discovery scene, not a combat or trap room — the tension is entirely in whether the party trusts a gift this well-aimed, not in anything that can hurt them here.
