Was a cooper on [[coriac]]'s old crew under captain [[edrin-vael]] — barrels and bracing, not banners. When the crew that walked inland (Group B) started coming apart, Önund did the only thing that kept the fear at bay: he declared himself king of the island and dared it to argue. It hasn't, so he's decided that proves the crown.

Bombastic, generous, ridiculous, and one wrong question away from coming apart. He is genuinely warm — he wants subjects the way a lonely man wants company, and he'll "knight" a PC, grant them land that isn't his, and declare an alliance before they've agreed to anything.

## The hoard, and the rule he won't say out loud

His "treasury" is a heap of junk — broken buckles, bird bone, sea glass. There's a reason it's junk. The crewmates who took the *good* things — spoils out of the wreckage, living wood off the forest — are in the back of the hull now, and they aren't crew anymore. Önund learned the island's price the hard way and never named it: **take nothing that matters, and it lets you keep your mind.** So he hoards only what the island doesn't want back, and calls it wealth.

## What he wants tonight

An audience. Recognition. To be a king in front of someone who'll play along. Underneath: for someone with actual power to deal with the "usurper" inland (he means Edrin, his own old captain — though he won't connect those dots aloud) so he can stop pretending he's in charge.

## What he won't do

Go to the back of the hull. Look directly at what happened to the others. Stop performing — because the performance is the only thing holding.

## Buried in the bluster

Real intel, if the party plays the court instead of breaking it: the warm dead air inland, the stone frame where "the usurper" keeps his power, and the captain who watches from the trees.

## Sobered — S22

Once the "usurper" is actually gone, the crown has nothing left to hold up. By the time the party reaches him, he and [[tjorvi]] have already buried the Stilled — Önund sits apart in silence through the funeral, no decrees, no crown. Sober, chastened, and for the first time all arc genuinely wants a real start: not an audience, actual responsibility. A live candidate for first mate on the new ship, if offered — and if it's offered sincerely rather than indulged, he takes it dead serious.
