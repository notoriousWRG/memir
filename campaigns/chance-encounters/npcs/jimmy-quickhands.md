Small-time operator. Low-level member of the Circle. Known [[artcoth]] for years — easy familiarity, professional history, mutual wariness. Jimmy was the point of contact for the Ruby Ring job: the Circle hired the party to steal a ring from [[lady-grimhook]], and Jimmy was the one with the table rules.

> *"I don't leave this pub for less than 10k."*
> *"Man, good to see you. You look... well, older."*
> *"You never did have much vision, Arty."*

## What he knew

Jimmy understood the Ruby Ring was infused with Liriel's power. He warned the party explicitly: don't put it on. He knew the Circle's stake in collecting Liriel's items but operated at a level where he followed orders rather than understood them.

He reported to a mid-level Circle operative — an elf in flamboyant clothes, conspicuous, not subtle — who in turn reported to Coal (Nicholas), a higher-level figure in the organization.

## Death

When the party sided with Grimhook instead of delivering the ring, Jimmy brought backup: a half-orc thug named Gern and a halfling archer named Hellen. He came to the road camp at night and made his ask — the ring, or a hand.

[[artcoth]] gave the signal. Jimmy took down [[gareth]] before Coriac revived him. He tried to run. Artcoth put an arrow through him at 140 feet.

An encrypted note found on his body reads, in decoded form: *"Enough excuses. I've been patient enough. Take care of them."* The sender is Coal. Full decryption requires intelligence work over several days.
