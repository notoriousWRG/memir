Halfling archer who came with [[jimmy-quickhands]] and Gern to the Ironspine road camp. Gern died there and Jimmy did too; Hellen got away. She knows Artcoth's face.

At the S24 gala she is the one who goes through Bellwether House while the party is out on the green. Leaves windows exactly as she found them. Afraid of Amelia, because she doesn't know about Amelia.

Stats if needed: Scout (MM). She runs rather than fights.

## S25

Came through an upstairs window with two thugs, entered the Ledger Room, and was interrupted by Ovaltine and Artcoth. She got nothing and escaped when Artcoth failed to catch her (13 against a DC 15). Her seal matches the amphora shards. She may have dropped something while fleeing (undecided).
