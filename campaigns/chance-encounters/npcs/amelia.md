The daughter of the house. A small ghost in a faded dress, clutching the doll she made, eyes a touch too wide and a smile a touch too quick. She died in the slow unraveling of Bellwether House — her mother [[eleanor-bellwether|Eleanor]] shattered by Dr. Bellwether's failed soul-ritual, the family undone — and Amelia simply... stayed. Not as a haunting in the frightening sense, but as a child who was never told it was time to go, and decided she didn't want to be told.

After the party cleansed [[bellwether-house]] and gathered Eleanor's soul whole again, Eleanor ascended. Amelia stayed. *"Mama's resting now."* She keeps the house warm the only ways she knows: humming, tidying, fogging the mirrors, and watching over the people who come through the door.

What she's like: grateful, playful, and ever-creepy — all at once, with no sense that those don't go together. She hums lullabies during rests. She moves the chairs back. A small cold hand will slip into yours and be gone before you look down.

## Relationship with the party

The party didn't drive her out — they freed her mother and let her keep her home. She loves them for it, in the uncomplicated, slightly alarming way of a lonely child who has decided you are hers now.

The cold of the house followed [[shah-doh]] from the first, and Amelia liked him for it. She gave him a doll she'd made — [[amelias-doll]] — pressed into his hands with both of hers, gravely, as a promise that he wouldn't be alone in the dark.

## What she wants

For the house to have laughter in it again. For the people she's decided to protect to keep coming back through the door. To not be left behind a second time.

## Quotes

- "If you die here… you can stay in my room."

- "I'll keep watch. Shadows tell me stories."
