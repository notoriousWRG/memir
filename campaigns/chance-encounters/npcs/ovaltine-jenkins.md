Spry halfling wizard, just under three feet tall. Unruly chestnut hair, coppery darting eyes. Patchwork robes stitched with pockets, loops, and dangling charms; sleeves smudged with chalk and faint sparks from constant tinkering. Smells of singed parchment and tea.

Level 9 wizard (School of Conjuration). Equal parts eccentric merchant and ambitious mage — boasts loudly about his wares, mutters about magical mishaps under his breath. Sees himself as on the brink of greatness, more than "just a shopkeep." Beneath the comic exterior: very talented, potentially dangerous if crossed.

## History with the party

The party tried to steal from him. He caught them with an alarm ward. They made a deal: one scroll in exchange for right of first refusal on any interesting or unique items they bring to sell.

## Ovaltine's Oddities

Small but cozy shop filled with magic items, potions, scrolls, books, and curiosities. Security is whimsical but effective — animated objects, wards, and the alarm system that caught the party.

## Current status

Ovaltine has been enlisted as a partner and is setting up shop at the party's headquarters in the Bellwether estate. He is there now, working on custom modifications to the house as requested by [[artcoth]].

## Approach to the party

Lowballs purchases first (40–60% of value), then negotiates upward. Has strong opinions about dangerous or soul-touched items. For Frostveil-adjacent artifacts, will redirect to "someone he knows" rather than buy outright — which foreshadows connections to [[lady-grimhook]] or rivals.
