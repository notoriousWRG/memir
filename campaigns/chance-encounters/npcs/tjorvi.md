Ship's boy turned royal herald, by Önund's appointment and his own desperate gratitude. Where [[onund]] performs the crown to keep the fear out, Tjorvi performs *belief in the crown* for the same reason. As long as the bit is going, neither of them has to be where they actually are.

He was there when the others took from the wreckage and the forest, and he was there when the island took them back. He didn't take anything. He's never been sure whether that makes him lucky or a coward, and he can't ask anyone, because the only person left to ask is pretending to be a king.

## What he wants tonight

For the performance to continue. For no one to go to the back of the hull. For someone to tell him he did the right thing by not reaching for the good stuff — without him having to admit that's the question.

## The tell

Tjorvi cracks before Önund does. Push the court too hard, mock it, or step toward the cold back room, and his laugh stops mid-sound. He's the one who will say the true thing first — *"don't take anything, please, don't take anything"* — if the party gets close to repeating the crew's mistake.

## What he knows

The clearest account of what actually happened to Group B: who took what, in what order, and how the forest answered. He's been carrying it alone.
