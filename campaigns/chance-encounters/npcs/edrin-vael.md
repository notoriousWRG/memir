**Captain of [[coriac]]'s old crew** — the same crew now wrecked and stranded across this island as the survivors the party is meeting (Group A — [[dessa]], [[marek]], [[ollo]] — and Group B inland). Coriac served under him, and they were also each other's closest conversational and spiritual peers before the break. Believed storms could be reasoned with, not merely endured.

That makes the survivors on this island Coriac's old crewmates, and Edrin their lost captain. The reunion is not with a stranger.

## The break

Chose to work with a dark, nature-based mage despite warnings. Their combined rituals began endangering innocent people in a nearby town. The party intervened and captured both Edrin and the mage. Both were imprisoned together. The mage was later found dead in the cell. Official narrative was unclear; suspicion lingered. Edrin escaped during the aftermath.

**DM truth:** The mage attempted a dangerous binding ritual in the cell. It catastrophically failed; the mage died to magical backlash. Edrin survived due to partial protection from [[ariel]], a storm-entity already aware of him.

## Now

Replaced experimentation with control. Binds Ariel out of fear of ungoverned power — sees himself as responsible, not cruel. Believes restraint caused the tragedy, not ambition.

He does not yet know that Ariel reached [[coriac]] voluntarily. When he learns this, it will destabilize his entire justification.

He assumes Coriac will understand the necessity of control. He does not regret choosing the mage's certainty over Coriac's faith.

## The treeline sightings

The "old captain" the survivors glimpse at the treeline is Edrin — but not in the flesh. It is a **scry-shadow**, his attention carried on the island's air and [[ariel]]'s bound domain: a perceivable presence, a silhouette that holds eye contact and then isn't there. This is why the camp can't agree he's real. His body is inland at the Circled Zone; what watches from the treeline is the reach of a man who binds a storm-spirit and uses her senses as his own.

Approached directly, the image withdraws (he isn't ready). Watched and left alone, it watches back — and he can speak through it, briefly, if he chooses.

## How it ended — S21

He took something off the mage's body before he escaped: a knotted totem of bone and bark, dark nature magic he never fully understood — he kept it because it was the mage's, not because he could use it. At the edge of dropping in the Reckoning, he broke it and transformed — a corrupted flavor of wild shape, fur going wrong colors, joints bending slightly off, claws too long. Reskinned Werebear (MM p.211).

He didn't control it cleanly. The party fought the bear down, and Ariel — her bonds falling away as he died — killed him herself with her own lightning. No last word. The totem burned out with him.

His spellbook is still on the battlefield, unclaimed.
