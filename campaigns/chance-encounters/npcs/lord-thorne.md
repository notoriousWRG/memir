Halfling noble. Neutral. Vain, ambitious, and consistently outmaneuvered. Aligned with no known faction but moves in circles of money and influence. His "talking dragon skull" sits in his study; he learned Draconic from it. He considers himself a serious collector of power — the Rod of Rulership was his proudest acquisition, and losing it publicly was the worst night of his life.

## History with the party

During the gala at his estate, Thorne used the Rod of Rulership to humiliate [[lady-grimhook]] in front of his assembled guests — forced her to kneel and recite words of admiration. The crowd laughed. Grimhook walked out steady and came back holding the rod.

While the party's failed heist attempt disrupted the gallery and drew Thorne's attention, Grimhook's actual agent retrieved the rod. When Grimhook publicly "revealed" it had all been staged comedy for their new partnership, Thorne — still under the rod's influence — confirmed the story to his own guests. He lost the rod, the auction, and his standing in one speech.

## Stats (for reference)

Medium humanoid (halfling), neutral. AC 15. HP 36. Speed 25 ft. CHA 18 (+4). Proficient in Deception and Persuasion. Lucky (reroll 1s). Magic Resistance.

Carries a ring of protection, a cloak of elvenkind, and a cube of force. Fights with dagger or cane, prefers not to fight at all.

## Motivation

Wants to recover the rod, or failing that, ensure Grimhook pays some cost for the humiliation. He may become relevant again if the party's relationship with Grimhook creates openings.
