Huginn and Muninn in human skins, visiting Bellwether House at the S24 gala. They book Grimvald "for a hall in the north" and listen for the mead of poetry he drank at the Echo of Mead. On the way out they stop at Gareth's sword: *"That's not ours."* *"It was, once."* Both lines are true, since Feanoro served Odin before he fell.

Each leaves a black feather. Grimvald's carries a date and no place. What the All-Father wants is not decided.

Eat nothing. Take every shiny thing.
