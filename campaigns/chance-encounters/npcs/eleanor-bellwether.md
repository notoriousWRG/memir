The lady of Bellwether House and mother of [[amelia]]. Wife to Dr. Bellwether, the alchemist whose obsession unmade the family. He sought to bind souls to matter — to *divide grief from flesh* — and he used Eleanor as the anchor for the ritual. It failed. Her soul shattered: scattered into fragments through the house, the gifts of creation pulled apart — **Anda** (breath), **Hugr** (will), **Likamr** (form) — leaving only a wrathful remnant behind, bound to a fractured soul jar in the chamber beneath the library.

For years she was the haunting: a spectral remnant lashing out in psychic fury, a woman's weeping face that could not remember why she screamed. Not evil — desperate, incomplete, and in unending pain.

## The cleansing

The party reversed the ritual circle and returned her three gifts under fire, reforging what her husband had torn apart. Amelia carried the final fragment — a tiny silver flame her mother had hidden with her and forgotten — and brought it forward to make Eleanor whole. Gathered and at peace, Eleanor ascended.

Her last words, to the party: *"Where he sought to divide, you have made whole. Let this house breathe again."* To Amelia: *"You carried my light… my brave girl."*

## Legacy

Eleanor is gone — released, not lingering. But her gratitude is woven into [[bellwether-house]] itself: the house rests easy now, and protects those who shelter under its roof. [[amelia]] stayed behind to keep it warm. Dr. Bellwether's fate is its own loose thread — the ritual consumed him, and what remained of him is not accounted for.
