Haunted by a past decision: pressed forward in a storm, lost a crew member. That event shaped his governing principle: *"If the sea resists, I yield."* His condition for the voyage to Ariel's island was explicit — if the wind turns them away, they turn back. Responds to honesty; shuts down to manipulation.

[[artcoth]] returned a stolen letter to him. That act built the party's most honest relationship with him. The captain suspects who took it but never confirmed.

## Grimvald's Suggestion — RESOLVED S20

[[grimvald]] confessed at the Circled Zone. The captain heard it without rage — accounting, not explosion. *"You knew what I said. 'If the sea resists, I yield.' You heard me say it."* He closed the chapter himself. The cost lives with the table; he did not forgive, but he did not need to. He had already decided what he was going to do.

## Arc — CLOSED S20

Walked to the Circled Zone on his own terms. Confirmed with [[artcoth]] that [[elara-venn]] was alive; said he was glad the Gull would have a captain. Called for the storm. [[ariel]] received him — not wrath, not compulsion. A tide coming in. He looked back once. The wind took him.

[[casus]] tried to stop him with Misty Step. Failed the Athletics check. The captain slipped through.

Tom Rell (young deckhand) is known to ask "Is this like before?" when a storm builds — a line that hit the captain hard. Rell does not know what happened on the island.
