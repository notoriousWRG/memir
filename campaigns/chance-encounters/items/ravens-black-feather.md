A black feather left behind by [[hugi-and-minna]], Odin's ravens in human form, whose names mean Thought and Memory. They leave one whenever they take wing, and each holds a trace of the All-Father's eyes. [[grimvald]] was handed one at the gala (session 24): his is etched down the spine with a far-north place and a date about a month out. [[coriac]] picked up two plain ones from where the ravens flew; the effect is the same, only the etching differs.

**Consumable, one use.** Burn it and choose which raven answers:

- **Thought.** Ask one question about something that happened in the realms in the last day. The DM answers with one true sentence, or the ravens didn't look.
- **Memory.** Relive one moment you were present for, in full sensory detail, including what you missed at the time.

Either way the All-Father hears: the question, or the memory, reaches him too.
