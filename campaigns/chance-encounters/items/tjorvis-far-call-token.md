Two halves of one bone chip, threaded on the same knot-pattern cord [[tjorvi]] ties into everything that matters to him. He split it clean down a natural crack in the bone — the kind sailors used to call luck, or a sign the thing was already meant to be two. Not magic in the way the party's other finds are magic. Just craft, care, and however much of this island's listening still clings to him.

## Properties

**Far-Call (1/day per holder).** Whoever holds a half can, once per day, speak a short phrase — 25 words or fewer — and the other half's holder hears it, a beat later, in their own mind. No meaningful range limit within the same coastal region; it doesn't reach across open ocean crossings or planes. Functionally equivalent to the *message* cantrip. No spell slot, no casting — just the two halves remembering they used to be one piece.
