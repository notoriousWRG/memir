A glass vial containing faintly luminous liquid threaded with silver filaments. The light pulses in response to unseen rhythms rather than glowing steadily. Brewed by [[ovaltine-jenkins]], who would privately call it a "Pre-Ignition Lattice — Field Test."

Currently held by [[casus]]. One dose. Irreversible once consumed.

This potion does not grant power. It provokes what is already present.

## Resonance Checks

When consumed, the drinker enters Magical Resonance for up to 1 hour. During that time, Resonance Checks occur when:
- The drinker experiences stress
- Makes a meaningful choice
- Is exposed to strong magic
- At DM discretion during heightened moments

Each check alternates between Constitution saving throw and Arcana check (DC 14, increasing by 1 after each success).

**3 successes before 3 failures → Controlled Awakening**

**3 failures before 3 successes → Resonance Collapse**

## Failure Track — Resonance Collapse

For 1d4 days, the drinker loses access to any class features tied to predictive or fate-based magic.

For Casus specifically: he loses Portent rolls for 1d4 days.

Describe it as: *"The threads go quiet."* Do not tell him it's temporary.

## Success Track — Controlled Awakening (Casus-Specific)

### Feature: Thread Intercession

Once per long rest, when a creature within 30 feet makes a d20 roll, Casus may:
- Add 1d4 to an ally's roll, **OR**
- Subtract 1d4 from an enemy's roll

After doing so: Casus has disadvantage on his next saving throw.

*He pulls on a thread not meant to be pulled.*
