A brass knocker in the shape of a wide, whiskered face — [[ovaltine-jenkins|Ovaltine]]'s work, and he's proud of it. Its eyes are cut glass, and they blink. It doesn't knock so much as announce.

**Bramwell** — that's the name it picked for itself, over Ovaltine's objections that it hadn't earned one yet. Vain about its craftsmanship, chatty with anyone who'll listen, and quietly convinced it's the most important fixture in the house. It considers itself the first and last word on who's welcome. It only speaks to [[artcoth]] — everyone else it sizes up in silence, eyes narrowing or widening, before it decides whether to let the latch give.

## Ward — Honest Face

**Homebrew, one clean rule:** if a creature trying to enter is under an illusion, disguise, shapechange, or impersonation effect concealing its true identity, Bramwell sees straight through it — no check required — and will not open. Instead it announces, loudly and unhelpfully specific, exactly what it sees under the mask. It has no opinion about lies that aren't magical — it's a ward against disguise, not a lie detector.

## Personality notes

- Gossips about who's been in and out, whether asked or not.
- Genuinely offended by rudeness to the doorstep itself — wiping feet, waiting to be announced, that sort of thing.
- Warms slowly to new faces Artcoth vouches for; instantly to anyone who compliments the brasswork.
- Will not shut up about Ovaltine's craftsmanship, even in front of Ovaltine.
