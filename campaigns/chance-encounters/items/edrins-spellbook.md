A slim, water-stained book bound in bark-tanned leather, found singed at the corners where the totem burned out beside it. The pages are a strange mix — precise arcane notation crossed through and rewritten in a looser, older hand, roots and thorn-shapes sketched in the margins like a man arguing with himself in two different languages. This is dark-touched, druidic-leaning magic: the kind [[edrin-vael|Edrin]] turned to once he stopped trusting anything that could be reasoned with.

It doesn't open easily. Whatever's in it wants to be earned, not skimmed.

## Decoding — homebrew

Deciphering a passage costs a scene or a stretch of downtime — however long is spent actually sitting with it. Roll Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Nature), DC 14. Success reveals one entry below (DM's choice, or roll 1d6). A natural 1 on the check triggers that entry's effect even on failure — the book pushes back.

1. **Bramble Bind** (spell) — a reflavor of *Spike Growth*: roots and thorns rise to grip and hold. [[casus]] can copy it into his spellbook if he succeeds. Thematically this is Edrin's whole arc in one spell.
2. **Binding theory** — Edrin's own annotated notes on how a bind is built and held. Grants advantage on any future check made to identify or counter a binding effect. (Quiet setup for later — the soulbinding sigil on [[artcoth]], if it comes up.)
3. **A pressed leaf, sigil-marked** — traces back to the dark mage's origin or teacher. A Frostveil-adjacent lore hook for Casus — don't resolve it, just seed it.
4. **Cursed — the ink still remembers reaching.** DC 13 Wisdom save or Frightened until the end of the reader's next turn; they hear one word, in a voice that isn't Edrin's.
5. **Cursed — a half-drawn sigil.** DC 13 Constitution save or 2d6 necrotic damage, and a faint binding-mark appears on the reader's skin, fading after 24 hours. Cosmetic and ominous — worth noting if [[ariel]] is nearby when it happens.
6. **A page that isn't blank to her.** Not decoded by a check — shown to [[ariel]] directly, she reveals one true fact about Edrin's descent, DM's choice. This can happen as early as the night it's found, if the party brings it to her at the Circled Zone.

Not meant to be fully cracked in one sitting — future material. If a player wants to try an entry at the table, allow it; otherwise this is a downtime hook for later sessions.
