A warhammer with a worn grip and simple ironwork — no runes, no visible enchantment. [[coriac]] has carried it since his early devotion to [[thor]]. It looked the same after the lightning strike as before. That's the point.

During the storm crossing to [[ariels-island]], Coriac planted himself in the path of a direct strike to protect the ship and crew. The hammer absorbed it. Thor's latent blessing, already present in the weapon, crystallized in that moment — not as a gift but as recognition.

*The storm did not break him. It recognized him.*

## Properties

**+1 Warhammer.**

**STORMROD (1/long rest):** When Coriac deliberately places himself in the path of a lightning effect targeting himself or an ally, he may use his reaction to absorb the strike entirely. He takes no damage. Until the start of his next turn, his next weapon attack deals an additional 2d6 lightning damage, and he sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius.

The ability only activates on a voluntary, deliberate absorption — not a lightning effect that happens to hit him. The choice must be declared before the damage is rolled.

## DM Note

The power is a description of his character, not a reward for a good roll. The condition — that it only works when he chooses to take the hit — should stay visible at the table. It's a mechanic that only functions through sacrifice.
