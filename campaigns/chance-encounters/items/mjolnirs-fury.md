A warhammer with a worn grip and simple ironwork. [[coriac]] found it on a dead paladin's body in the underdark — a servant of [[thor]] who had been cut down before the party arrived. They avenged the paladin's death, and with that, Thor's blessing passed to Coriac along with the weapon. It looked the same after the lightning strike as before. That's the point.

During the storm crossing to [[ariels-island]], Coriac planted himself in the path of a direct strike to protect the ship and crew. The hammer absorbed it. Thor's latent blessing, already present in the weapon, crystallized in that moment — not as a gift but as recognition. After the strike, a rune of protection seared itself into the handle where Coriac held it.

*The storm did not break him. It recognized him.*

## Properties

**+1 Warhammer.** +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

**Storm's Judgment (1/long rest).** Call down a bolt of divine lightning on a target you can see within 60 feet. The target makes a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 lightning damage on a failed save, or half on a success.

**Wrath of the Stormforged (1/short rest).** When a creature within 5 feet hits Coriac with a melee attack, he can use his reaction to unleash retaliatory lightning. The attacker takes 1d6 lightning damage, and Coriac briefly glows with divine sparks until the start of his next turn.

**STORMROD (1/long rest).** When Coriac deliberately places himself in the path of a lightning effect targeting himself or an ally, he may use his reaction to absorb the strike entirely. He takes no damage. Until the start of his next turn, his next weapon attack deals an additional 2d6 lightning damage, and he sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius.

The ability only activates on a voluntary, deliberate absorption — not a lightning effect that happens to hit him. The choice must be declared before the damage is rolled.
