A short length of cord worked into a knotted sigil — a sailor's ward-knot, the kind tied against drowning, foul luck, and worse things the sea keeps. [[artcoth]] pulled it from [[onund]]'s hoard inside the Hollowed Hull: junk all around it, but this one had held up better than everything else (session 19). [[tjorvi]] recognized it: *"This is from our ship."* He held it a moment, then handed it back. *"Just to be safe — this is a gift."*

That it survived intact when everything else in the hoard rotted is the tell. The ward held.

## Properties

**Sailor's Luck (1/day).** While carried, when the bearer fails an ability check or a saving throw, they can choose to reroll it and must use the new result. Footing on a pitching deck, breath held a beat longer, the sea a touch less hungry. Recharges at dawn.

**Break the Knot (one use, then inert).** As a reaction, when the bearer or a creature within 5 feet is reduced to 0 HP, or fails a saving throw against being grappled, restrained, paralyzed, or petrified, they may break the cord. The triggering effect fails — damage that would drop them to 0 leaves them at 1 HP instead, or the condition doesn't take hold — and they may immediately move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks. The knot unravels; the cord becomes frayed string and nothing more.

## DM Note

Tjorvi gave it *"just to be safe."* He knows what the interior took from the ones who went in reaching. The cord is the one piece of his crew's protection that still works — and it works exactly once.
