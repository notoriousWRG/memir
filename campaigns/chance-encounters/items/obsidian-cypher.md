A small black cube, palm-sized. Dwarven runes carved into each face — archaic, from an earlier age of craftsmanship. Heavy for its size. Cold to the touch even in warm rooms. Six faces, six runes; pressing them in specific sequences does things that have no business being done by a box this small.

Currently held by [[casus]].

## Origin

Thlgrin Deepforge was a dwarven archmage of Svartalfheim — renowned, then paranoid, then exiled. He spent decades filling this cube with his most dangerous possessions: treasures, monsters, weapons, forbidden knowledge. He captured six immortal beings and bound one to each rune face, siphoning their essence to power the Cipher's functions.

Cornered by his enemies, Thlgrin activated the stasis function, cast the cube into an underground chasm, and disappeared inside it. Centuries later, dwarven miners found it embedded in rock. It passed through many hands before arriving in the party's possession.

## What Casus found (library research)

Three records uncovered in the library near where the Cypher was originally found:

**"The Black Relic of the Deep"** — a miner's account describing the cube's discovery in the South Hollow Caverns. Mentions Durm Steelvein ranting about "shackles in the dark" before throwing himself into a chasm. Notes it was sealed in the citadel and passed to the Arcanum Forge scholars. States it is "believed to bear the mark of the exiled Archmage Thalgrin Deepforge, though none can claim certainty as to its origin or purpose."

**Arcanum archive fragment** — a scholar's note after experimenting: "Heart, Shield, Fist... An alignment of light and steel... curious, yet fleeting. The resulting magic healed my wounded apprentice's hand, but at what cost? The cube—no, the Cipher—sang as it drew power. I dare not try further." This is the first in-world confirmation of the rune sequences.

**History of Thalgrin Deepforge** — fragmented legends, clearly embellished. Key claims: "once the brightest runesmith of Svartalfheim"; "Banished for his arrogance, retreated into the darkest reaches of the earth"; "Rumors speak of a vault—a pocket realm forged by his hand, guarded by riddles and traps only he could solve"; "Legends claim he defied death itself, sealing his soul within his greatest creation." Party now knows the pocket-dimension opening sequence is real.

## The Runes

Six faces, each bound to an imprisoned entity whose life force powers the effect:

| Rune | Symbol | Imprisoned Being |
|---|---|---|
| Healing | Heart | A radiant dryad from Alfheim — deceived into thinking she was protecting her forest |
| Protection | Shield | A celestial dwarf, servant of Brokkr's forge — tricked into believing his sacrifice would protect dwarvenkind |
| Wisdom | Book | The soul of a human mage who sought immortality and received imprisonment instead |
| Power | Lightning Bolt | A thunder giant from Jotunheim — subdued through cunning |
| Strength | Fist | A frost giant from Niflheim — captured during a raid |
| Secrets | Eye | A shadow fey from Alfheim — the circumstances of capture are deliberately obscured |

Each use costs the entity something. Their cries are briefly audible when the door sequence is activated.

## Rune Sequences and Effects

**Healing (Heart → Shield → Fist):** Radiant burst heals nearby allies significantly; grants temporary damage resistance for one encounter.

**Protection (Shield → Heart → Lightning Bolt):** Shimmering barrier on one creature for 10 minutes. Immune to physical damage; magical damage halved. Absorbs 15 HP before breaking.

**Wisdom (Eye → Book → Shield):** One yes-or-no question about the current quest. DM determines truth.

**Power (Lightning Bolt → Fist → Shield):** 30-foot cone of lightning, DC 15 Dex save, 4d8 lightning damage (half on success). May ignite flammable objects.

**Strength (Fist → Lightning Bolt → Shield):** +2 Strength and Constitution, advantage on all Strength checks and saves, for 10 minutes.

**Secrets (Eye → Book → Fist):** True sight to 60 feet for 1 minute. Sees through illusions, magical darkness, invisible creatures. Hidden glyphs and arcane traps become visible.

**Opening the Pocket Dimension (Book → Book → Shield → Heart → Lightning Bolt → Fist → Fist → Fist):** A portal opens into the pocket dimension where Thlgrin stored everything — treasures, monsters, lore, and Thlgrin himself, preserved in stasis. All six entities are drained simultaneously. Their voices echo briefly as the doorway forms.

**Self-Destruct:** Press any single rune 7 or more times in a row. The Cipher fractures, releasing everything inside into the material plane simultaneously. The imprisoned entities are freed — and likely furious.

## Incorrect Sequences

Roll d20 on failed sequences. Effects range from arcane backlash (2d6 force to nearby creatures) and dimensional leaks (gravity shifts in 15-foot radius) to harmless polymorph and one case involving multicolored glitter. The scale tips toward unpleasant.

## What's Inside

- Thalgrin's personal hoard: magical artifacts, weapons, scrolls
- Lore about Thlgrin's creations and the Arcane Age
- Monsters stored for experimentation or as guards
- Thlgrin Deepforge himself — in stasis, waiting. Ally, rival, or something in between, depending on circumstances.

## DM Note

The Cipher is a ward Casus carries and may or may not fully understand yet. The imprisoned entities are canon — the dryad fading, the celestial dwarf despairing, the mage speaking in riddles. If the party opens the pocket dimension, what they find inside should be treated as a full encounter location, not a treasure room.
