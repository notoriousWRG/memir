A sliver of glass formed where [[ariel]]'s lightning came down during the storm crossing to [[ariels-island]] — sand and seawater fused in the instant of the strike. It came to [[gareth]] out of the wreckage (session 17). Smooth, dark, faintly translucent, with a hairline of brighter glass running through it like a captured bolt. It hums when storm energy is near.

It is a fragment of her presence. The island recognizes it; it warms and brightens the closer the party moves toward Ariel.
