A pendant shaped like a star, crafted from rare metals and enchanted with Ymir's Breath. Glows faintly, like starlight. One of the six [[liriel-baenre]] Frostveil artifacts — designed to "listen," distilling clarity from fate, time, and possibility. Combat is secondary to perception and self-knowledge.

Hidden in the [[starlight-watchtower]] observatory in the Iron Peaks. [[lady-grimhook]] contracted the party to retrieve it.

## Powers (when attuned)

- See normally in magical darkness
- **Limited Precognition** — once per day: reroll any failed saving throw, or force an enemy to reroll an attack roll
- Cast *Augury* or *Divination* once per long rest

## Corruption

- **Stage 1:** Bearer sees brief, horrifying visions of death in mundane scenes
- **Stage 2:** Struggles to distinguish past, present, and future
- **Stage 3:** Acts based on a future that hasn't occurred yet — creating self-fulfilling tragedies
