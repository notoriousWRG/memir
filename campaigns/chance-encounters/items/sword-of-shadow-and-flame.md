Given by [[feanaro]] — the corrupted seer who masquerades as a servant of Odin — as encouragement for [[gareth]] to pursue power. The gift is a claim: this is what you are now. Whether Gareth agrees is the ongoing question.

A longsword that carries darkness in its blade even in bright light. When the command word is spoken, fire erupts from the edge and runs the length of it.

## Properties

**Command Word (bonus action):** Speak *Feanaro* (elvish: "spirit of fire") to ignite the blade. While ablaze:
- Sheds bright light in a 30-foot radius, dim for an additional 30 feet.
- Deals an extra 2d6 fire damage on hit.
- Flames last until you use a bonus action to speak the word again or until the sword is dropped or sheathed.

**Soul Charges:** Gained through kills. (Specific spell or effect TBD at table.)

## Corruption Risk

When Gareth uses soul charges, make a Wisdom saving throw. Failure advances the corruption track:

1. *Aggressive* — when attacked, always retaliate.
2. *Always go for the kill.*
3. *Attack the nearest creature, regardless of alignment.*

## Feanaro's Agenda

The sword is a leash dressed as a gift. Every kill charges it; every charge pulls Gareth deeper. Feanaro has appeared over the bodies of Gareth's kills — not threatening, watching. The sword is working.

## Reforge Path (Pending)

Feanaro's original design for the sword was refinement through corruption. That story has shifted: the reforge path now runs through Odin, not through Feanaro's approval. What that costs and what the sword becomes is unresolved. When it happens, the command word changes. The sword becomes something Gareth owns rather than something that owns him.
