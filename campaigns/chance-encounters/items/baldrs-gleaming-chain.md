Radiant chain mail woven from links that shimmer with soft, sunlit warmth. Worn only by those who have taken the Oath of the Dawnbound — servants of [[baldr]], the Lightborn, the Gentle Heart. [[shah-doh]] carries it.

This armor arrived without explanation. Its presence on Shah is consistent with the Baldr thread — his father's distant intercession, the divine protection willed toward a son who doesn't yet know where it came from.

## Properties

**Radiant Pulse (1/long rest).** As a bonus action, Shah sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. He may choose up to three creatures within the bright light; each chosen creature gains advantage on its next saving throw made within the next minute.

**Baldr's Endurance.** Once per combat, when Shah is reduced to half his hit point maximum or lower, he gains 5 temporary hit points.

**Light of the Dawnborn.** While wearing this armor, bright or dim light Shah creates cannot be magically suppressed by spells or effects of 2nd level or lower.

## DM Note

The armor is a physical manifestation of Shah's father's prayer over him. Shah doesn't know his father served Baldr or that this protection is deliberate. When the thread resolves — if it resolves — the armor will be the first evidence that someone was watching over him all along.
