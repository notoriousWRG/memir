Six wrapped gifts, set out on a table in a room that wasn't there until the Hostess's Eye ward fires — see [[lady-grimhook#If the party visits the manor|the manor]]. One per party member, labeled by name in [[lady-grimhook|Lady Grimhook]]'s hand. Old custom, sharpened into a calculation: offer a guest something genuinely valuable, and the debt runs both ways whether they know it or not.

Prepared before she left for the Starlight Watchtower — insurance, not malice. She fully expects some or all of these to go unclaimed, and isn't attached to the outcome either way.

## How they work

Each gift is real: a genuinely useful magic item, no strings visible on casual inspection. Detect Magic confirms each is enchanted, same as any magic item would read. The surface function works exactly as described below, no catch, no delay — that's what makes them worth taking.

The catch is woven into the surface enchantment, not separate from it, which is why it doesn't jump out under a simple Identify. A DC 15 Arcana or Investigation check — after a short rest spent studying the item, or after casting Identify — surfaces that *something else* is bound in, a thread running back to someone. DC 18 is needed to work out what that thread actually does. Treat this as discoverable, not automatic: the party has to think to look.

## The six

### Artcoth — The Fair Count
A ledger bound in dark leather, silver clasp, blank pages. **Surface:** writes and tallies on its own; advantage on Insight checks made to catch a lie during a negotiation or deal. **Thread:** every entry it records is mirrored, faintly, into a twin ledger in Grimhook's keeping — she reads what he tracks. Pure surveillance; no cost to Artcoth's own performance.

### Casus — The Clear Lens
A monocle cut like a snowflake, in a fitted case. **Surface:** advantage on Arcana checks made to identify a magic item or an active magical effect. **Thread:** significant readings echo faintly back to her — she catches an impression of what he examines. It's also tuned to her own kind of precision magic: turned on something wild or chaotic in nature (Ariel, the storm, [[coriac]]'s Mark), the reading comes back subtly incomplete — disadvantage on Arcana specifically in that case, her bias built into the glass.

### Coriac — The Fair-Weather Glass
A hand-sized weatherglass with a tiny captured storm turning inside it. **Surface:** once per day, calm rough seas and wind in a small radius around the ship for up to an hour, or read the next 24 hours of weather with total accuracy — genuinely valuable for a new captain. **Thread:** built to dampen wild or divine intensity nearby — while it's aboard, [[forge-shard|Ariel's Mark]] runs a shade duller: the first time in a day its passive resistance or the 1/long rest Ask would trigger, it doesn't. A quiet test of whether the storm she covets can be leashed at one remove, through the person closest to it.

### Gareth — The Steady Ward
A plain iron ring, unadorned, cool to the touch. **Surface:** advantage on saving throws against being charmed, possessed, or magically influenced — precisely, painfully, what Gareth doesn't know he needs against Feanoro. **Thread:** it dulls his own read on influence already inside him, not just what's incoming — while worn, disadvantage on the Wisdom saves tied to [[sword-of-shadow-and-flame]]'s corruption track. The ring keeps new charms out and lets the old one sit unnoticed. The cruelest of the six, and the one she'd deny knowing anything about.

### Grimvald — The Listening Coin
An old, worn coin, blank on both faces until turned in the light, when a face flickers into view. **Surface:** once per day, compel a truthful answer to one question from a willing or unwilling listener. **Thread:** it listens both ways — what's said near it eventually reaches her. And it's tuned against its own bearer: while Grimvald carries it, disadvantage on Deception checks made to lie in direct response to a question. An item that extracts truth from others makes his own harder to hide.

### Shah Doh — The Warm Lantern
A small oil lamp that needs no fuel, flame steady gold-white. **Surface:** sheds warm light that grants advantage on saving throws against fear within its radius — and the flame burns visibly brighter and warmer near anything connected to [[baldr]], a deliberate dangle toward his unresolved father thread. **Thread:** the gentlest of the six — no debuff, just a quiet line reporting his location and wellbeing back to her. Whether that's respect or simple economy — he's the least likely of the six to suspect it — is the DM's call.

## DM note

Nobody has to take any of these, and nothing forces the question at the table — they're just sitting there, real and useful, with her name on the note above them. If a gift is kept, decide *when*, not *whether*, the thread becomes relevant again: a future scene where she references something she shouldn't know, a moment where a debuff bites at the worst time, or nothing at all if the party never gives her reason to cash it in. Once a gift is identified as compromised, that's a live choice for the player — keep it anyway, look for a way to break the thread, or discard it. Don't resolve that choice here.
