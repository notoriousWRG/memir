title: "Campaign Journal"
description: Narrative through-line of Chance Encounters.
---

Arc 1 began underground. The party descended into the dark tunnels beneath the surface and came to a flooded lake they couldn't cross — walls too distant, water too black. [[shalindra-aboleth]] surfaced. She called herself the King-slayer and held a dwarven monarch's signet ring on her fang as a trophy. The terms she offered were not negotiated; they were demonstrated. The party entered the pact and went into [[duergar-city]] through her route.

What followed was several sessions in the dark: the dwarven city repurposed and lowered, the infestation the duergar wouldn't face themselves, a temple with a statue that looked like Gareth's patron, Zal'goroth at the Soul Forge. The party cleared it. [[casus]] joined them somewhere in the library section — the exact moment is unconfirmed. The escape out involved [[ovaltine-jenkins]] and whatever experimental leverage he could offer. They surfaced. Whether Shalindra considered the pact fulfilled was never asked.

Back in [[ravencrest]], [[lady-grimhook]] formalized the next commission. The [[pendant-of-stars]] — a job that would require a ship. Downtime happened. [[gareth]] joined. A contract was signed with specific stakes. [[ovaltine-jenkins]]' shop opened a rift in the process of going wrong, which crossed a threshold that shouldn't have been crossable.

Arc 2 moved outward. The White Gull was acquired. Its captain came with the ship — not a simple contractor: a man with a relationship to the sea and to the particular voyage the party needed from him. [[grimvald]] cast Suggestion, and the captain pressed on against his own stated condition without knowing why. Then the storm arrived.

[[coriac]] stepped into a lightning strike to protect the ship. [[ariel]] pulled him into a vision. He reached out his hand and invited her to speak. She was moving toward speech when the vision ended. The White Gull grounded on black sand. The captain is missing — no body. Five Storm Draugr emerged from the water. Round 1 is over. Round 2 is next.
