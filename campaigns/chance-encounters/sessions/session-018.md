*Source: Post-Session Recap. Covers the grounding, the Storm Draugr fight to its conclusion, and first contact with the island's survivors.*

The White Gull grounds on the black sand shore of [[ariels-island]]. The party orients on the beach: storm wreckage, dark sand, fog. [[artcoth]] pulls gloves from the debris — found in the initial survey of the shore (see [[artcoths-gloves]]).

[[ariel]]'s presence saturates the island. She began a sentence to [[coriac]] that she did not finish.

Five [[storm-draugr]] emerge from the fog and the surf.

## The fight

The draugr fought like the sailors they had been — footing, leverage, the waterline as territory. It was not a clean fight to win and it was not a clean fight to feel good about.

Toward the end, with their numbers thinning, they did something undead aren't supposed to do: they **coordinated a retreat**. One gestured to another to fall back. The one signalled broke for the water and got away. The one who gave the signal — who stayed a beat longer to cover the escape — was struck down where he stood.

The party won the beach. Nobody felt like they'd won anything. This wasn't a hack-and-slash against monsters; it was watching something that remembered being a crew die in front of them.

The draugr that escaped did not go far. They surfaced offshore and **watched** — trailing the party from the water, not approaching, not leaving. Just present.

## The first crew's camp

Past the beach, the party found a fire and stepped over a **crude alarm spell** to reach it — a perimeter of rope and bone strung at ankle height. The camp belongs to the island's survivors (Group A), and three of them were there:

- **[[marek]]** (cook) was making stew. He shared it without thinking about it — food before anything else.
- **[[ollo]]** (navigator's apprentice) was eager to talk: notes, theories, the patterns he's been logging. He wanted to be useful and to finally have someone to ask.
- **[[dessa]]** (former bosun, their de facto leader) was more careful. Her answers were measured and gave little away. The party pushed; she gave them the patterns and not the truth, and when the pushing continued she finished her knot and retired to her bed. The party was left frustrated.

## What they learned

- Some of the survivors' crew **left a few weeks ago** and have not come back.
- There have been **sightings of the survivors' old captain from the treeline** — though the camp debates whether anyone's actually seeing him or just seeing things.

*DM truth: the survivors' former captain may be alive inland — watching, keeping eyes on everyone, while he works through whatever he's working through. Or the island is playing tricks. Left open. (Open question: is this the figure already on the island — [[edrin-vael]] — or someone distinct?)*

## The decision: inland

After a long rest at the camp, the party decided to push **inland** in the morning rather than stay on the beach. **[[ollo]] asked to come** — eager to chart the island and solve its mysteries, and very naive about what that costs. The party agreed; he travels with them now.

The session ends with the party resting inside the magical dome [[casus]] conjures (Tiny Hut), preparing for the move inland at first light.
