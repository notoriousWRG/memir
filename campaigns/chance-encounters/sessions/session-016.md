*Source: "Setting Sail?" Notion page.*

The White Gull is acquired. The Grimhook contract facilitated this — the ship is the means by which the Pendant commission gets done, or at least the means by which they get close enough to try.

The captain's psychology is established as a factor. He is not simply a contractor: his relationship to the sea, to the crew, and to the particular voyage the party needs him to take has texture. The party will need to understand him to manage him.

[[grimvald]] has a moment here — a "one more mile" beat, pushing past exhaustion or doubt to hold something together that was on the edge of failing. The crew responds.

The Storm That Remembers begins. Phases 1 and 2 are a slow intensification: weather that starts as bad and becomes something with intention. By the end of this session the storm is not something to sail around — it's a direction they're being pushed toward, or into.
