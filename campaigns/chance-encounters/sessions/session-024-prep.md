# Session 24 — The Gala at Bellwether

---

## 1 — The Charge

Two weeks on. Bellwether House is lit from cellar to attic, the front green is strung with lanterns, and half of Ravencrest is coming to see what Chance Encounters LLC looks like up close — Grimvald's ballads did their work. The gala was Casus's idea. He won't see the end of it. Ovaltine's potion pried something loose in him, and time has stopped holding him properly. Meanwhile, not everyone on the guest list came for the canapés: Lord Thorne needs a favor he can't ask for in daylight, two strangers in black want to hear Grimvald sing, Baldr's temple wants a look at Shah's chain — and the Circle has sent Coal himself, to keep Artcoth talking while his people go through the house looking for Lady Grimhook. Grimhook, of course, doesn't come.

*If they do nothing:* Every visitor gets what they came for without the party steering it. Coal plants his doubt and walks out clean. The Circle leaves with whatever the Ledger Room's logbook knows about Grimhook. The serpent on the green hurts guests, and the LLC's big night becomes the night people got bitten. Casus fades at his own party with nobody watching.

## 5 — Scenes

### Scene 1 — Doors Open
[[bellwether-house]] — the front hall, the green, every room lit

**Read-aloud:** Lanterns strung across the front green, a fiddler on the steps, and Bramwell bellowing names at Artcoth as each guest crosses the threshold — *"ARTCOTH, a WOOL MERCHANT. ARTCOTH, his WIFE, who is WEARING HIS CLOAK."* Frost on every window, in a child's hand: *welcome welcome welcome.* Across the room, Casus lifts a glass for a toast he hasn't started giving yet.

**Loaded:** The party is hosting. People want their attention and they can't be everywhere. Casus starts at Stage 1.

**Homebrew — Unmoored.** *Brief Chris before the session:* "Your two failures didn't just exhaust you — they cracked something open. You're a diviner, and time has stopped holding you properly. You'll slip through the night, and by its end you'll be gone. Not dead. Somewhere else, somewhen else."

| Stage | What the table sees |
|---|---|
| 1 — Flicker | Answers a beat early. Reaches for things before they're offered. |
| 2 — Echo | Describes *this same place* at another time, aloud, without meaning to (vision bank below). |
| 3 — Slip | Gone for a round, back a few feet off. An afterimage where he stood. Amelia can still see him. |
| 4 — Gone | He's not in the room. |

**Out of Step** *(Casus's parting power).* Once per stage, as a reaction, Casus adds or subtracts 1d4 from any d20 roll made within 30 ft — he saw it coming. **Using it advances Unmoored immediately.** Every time he helps, he loses time with them. Chris's call, every time. His 2 levels of Exhaustion stay until he goes.

**Grimhook — hint ladder.** She doesn't come. If the table misses one hint, the next is louder:
1. Her RSVP arrived early, gracious, in her precise hand. The courier mentions it was left with him *two weeks ago*, with instructions to deliver "on the day."
2. Her seat at the head table stays empty. Guests notice before the party does: *"Her lamps go on at the same hour every night. Every night. Clockwork."*
3. Thorne, pleased and then uneasy: *"She's never missed a chance to be looked at. Where is she?"*
4. Coal asks it outright (Scene 3).
5. The Circle turning the house over for her is the loudest hint of all. They can't find her either.

**Triggers:**
- *If Casus gives the toast* → Unmoored ticks mid-toast. He finishes a sentence nobody heard him start.
- *Default* → guests pull the party in different directions. Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The Visits
*Run in any order. One spotlight each, then they fade into the crowd. Casus can flicker through any of them.*

#### 2a — Lord Thorne's Ask
**Read-aloud:** He finds whoever signed the letter, alone, near the drinks. He doesn't take a glass. *"I accepted your invitation because it was the only way to speak to you without being seen to."*

**Loaded:** Tansy has been gone about three weeks now. He's spent two of them trying alone. He reads back one word from their letter, *"Daughter,"* and corrects it: *"My WARD."* Offended, theatrical. There's nothing hidden behind it. The slip was just a slip (DM call).
- **Gives:** Tansy, halfling, ~15, last seen at his estate. He's told no one, not even the Watch. He wants it quiet. Pay: TBD.
- **Withholds:** Why he has a ward at all.
- **Clue seeds, each reading at least two ways (decide meaning at the table):**
  - Her window latch was opened from the inside.
  - She spent most evenings with his talking dragon skull. Nobody else in the house speaks Draconic.
  - Her good boots are gone. Her coin purse isn't.

**Triggers:** An apology softens him one degree. A joke gets business only, no goodwill. Mentioning Grimhook: *"If she learns of this, I will know where she learned it."* Refusing sends him out the door and advances Tansy's Trail.

#### 2b — Grimvald's Callers
**Read-aloud:** Two guests in black who arrived without being announced. Bramwell didn't see them come in, and says so, loudly. A tall man and a small woman, both with very dark, very bright eyes. They don't blink at the same time. *"We book performers,"* he says. *"For a hall in the north,"* she finishes.

**Loaded:** Odin's ravens, Thought and Memory, in human skins. They want one verse, sung here, now. They're listening for the mead of poetry: the stolen draught that Grimvald drank from a rune-bound barrel at the Echo of Mead. They don't say any of this.
- **Dig in:** DC 15 Insight: they aren't listening to the words, they're listening *under* them. DC 18 Religion or Arcana: two ravens have been sitting on the gutter all night, and now they aren't. They eat nothing. One pockets a silver spoon without looking.
- **If he performs:** one of them says quietly, *"It's him."* They leave a booking token: a black feather, a date, no place. The All-Father's eye is on him now. Don't decide what that means yet.
- **If he refuses:** *"Everyone sings for him eventually."* They leave the feather anyway.
- **The patron question:** the voices from the barrel stay an open mystery. The ravens react to the mead, not to the patron. Don't tie the two together tonight.

#### 2c — Gareth and the Ravens
*Runs right after 2b, as the ravens leave. No new NPC.*

**Read-aloud:** On their way to the door, the two in black pass Gareth, and both stop at the same time. They aren't looking at him. They're looking at his sword.

**Loaded:** Gareth believes Odin is his patron. It's actually Feanoro, a seer who once served Odin and fell to Nidhogg. Odin's real eyes are standing in front of him. The ravens know that sword, and they know who it doesn't belong to.
- **What they say:** she, flatly: *"That's not ours."* He, after a beat: *"It was, once."* Then they leave. They answer no questions. *(Both lines are true: Feanoro served Odin before he fell.)*
- **Feanoro answers:** the next time Gareth draws or ignites the sword tonight, Feanoro is in the flame: calm, warm, reassuring. *"Messengers are tested too. Do not let crows tell you who you serve."* He'll use any doubt to tighten his hold, as his file says, so he doesn't let the moment settle.
- **Don't decide:** whether Gareth takes this as a warning, a test, or nothing. That's up to him. This beat plants the reforge path; it doesn't open it.

**Triggers:**
- *If Gareth follows them out* → two ravens lift off the gutter. One drops a single black feather at his feet. He now has one too, with no date on it.
- *If Gareth tells the party* → Grimvald is holding a black feather too. Let them compare.
- *Later, on the green* → the serpent is a brood-child of Jormungandr, one of Loki's children. The sword *wants* this kill. First blood against the serpent: Gareth makes a Wisdom save (DC 13) or the corruption track advances one step. *(Reuses the existing corruption track; see [[sword-of-shadow-and-flame]].)*

#### 2d — Baldr's Temple
**Read-aloud:** An old priest in a sun-gold stole, leaning on the arm of a woman in polished mail who looks like she's never once leaned on anything. He smiles at Shah's armor before he looks at Shah's face.

**Loaded:** Brother Sölvi recognizes the Gleaming Chain, a Dawnbound relic, and wants to meet whoever it went to. Dame Hervor served the Oath for years and was never given it. She wants to know why an unsworn half-orc was.
- **Sölvi gives, gently:** the chain wasn't handed out by the temple the usual way. It was *requested*, years ago, by a Dawnbound knight, for "someone who would need it." He doesn't say who. *(How much he knows is DM's call; see Decide Before Play.)*
- **Hervor tests:** a question, not a duel. *"What would you not do, even for your friends?"* A real answer earns her respect. A glib one earns a formal challenge at some later date.
- **Triggers:** If Shah uses Radiant Pulse or shows the chain's light, Sölvi touches his sun-disc and goes quiet, and Hervor looks away. If Shah asks about his father directly, Sölvi pauses too long. He knows *something*.

#### 2e — Ovaltine Catches On
**Read-aloud:** Ovaltine is halfway through selling a Bottled Squall to a customs officer when Casus flickers out beside him, gone a full breath and back two feet to the left. Ovaltine sets the bottle down and doesn't pick it back up.

**Loaded:** Ovaltine understands before anyone explains it. His want flips to tracking the drift, timing it, taking notes on napkins, and saving Casus if it can be done. He won't raise his own guilt. If someone else does, he takes it without defending himself.
- *"The potion doesn't give. It provokes what's already there. What was already there was a man who looks at time sideways."*
- He wants Casus near the ritual chamber, where the half-built teleportation circle hums when Casus passes.

**The anchor challenge (stage it, don't solve it):**
- **Stage:** Casus is going. That's fixed. *How* he can come back isn't. "For now" is real either way: if Chris ever wants to bring Casus back, there's a way. The anchor only decides which way. *(Think Number Five in* Umbrella Academy.*)*
- **Success trigger:** the party gives him a fixed point to leave *from*. Any approach works. Doors the table might find: Ovaltine's circle, Coriac's *Ask* through Ariel's Mark, Amelia holding his hand, a Cypher sequence, a promise spoken in the Ledger Room so the quill writes it down.
- **If anchored:** a **tether**. There's a known point in the here and now he can aim back at. If he returns, it's by following the thread home.
- **If not anchored:** no tether. If he returns, it's because he **mastered the jump himself**, on his own, somewhere else, somewhen else. He comes back changed: older maybe, and carrying something he saw.

### Scene 3 — Coal
[[bellwether-house]] — a quiet corner, or wherever Artcoth is

**Read-aloud:** A man takes the empty chair across from Artcoth as if it had been saved for him. Bramwell let him in: he's exactly who he says he is. He's soft-spoken and precise, and he already knows which drink Artcoth ordered. He sets down a card with a flourished *C* on it, the same one from the letter. *"I thought we should finally meet properly. Jimmy always said you had no vision. I've never agreed with him less."*

**Loaded:** Coal's job tonight is to keep Artcoth at this table. He plays it like Moriarty: courteous, patient, faintly amused, as if he worked out every reply three moves ago and is only checking Artcoth's answers against his own. He's also here to plant a real doubt. The lines to land, in whatever order the talk allows:
- *"Is she even here? Has she been, these two weeks?"*
- *"Do you know what her plans are? Not what she told you. What she's doing."*
- *"You're tools to her. I think you're more."*
- **Optional, your call on timing:** *"She wore our mark once, you know. Ask her about her ring finger."* It's true: Grimhook was Circle (see Don't Contradict). This line puts the reveal in play. Hold it back if you'd rather it land later.
- **What he'll say:** he knows Grimhook is gone, and he's letting Artcoth work out that he doesn't know where.
- **What he won't say:** what the Circle wants with her. (It's Liriel's ring, and her Liriel items.)
- **Tell:** he glances at the window like he's checking the weather. When Artcoth pushes, he orders another drink. That's stalling.

**Dig in:** DC 16 Insight: he's waiting for something. DC 18 Perception: a thin line of ink at his wrist, the edge of a tattoo. It's the design from the watermark: a serpent. Anyone who's looked closely at Grimhook's ring finger would recognize it. *"Old loyalties, like old tattoos, fade — but never disappear."*

**Triggers:**
- *If Artcoth leaves the table early* → the green erupts early, and the Search starts with more time on the clock.
- *If Artcoth calls him out as a distraction* → Coal smiles and raises his glass: *"Of course I am. Go on, then."* Then the shatter and the scream from the green.
- *If they try to grab him* → he doesn't fight. He's a guest under their roof, in front of half the town, and he's counting on that. Holding him has a price with the crowd.
- *Default* → mid-sentence, a shatter and a scream from the green, heard from inside. Coal finishes his drink.

**Leads to:** Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — The Green
[[bellwether-house]] — the front green, with the house behind it

**The party doesn't see the setup.** They're inside the gala when it starts.

**Read-aloud (from inside):** Through the open doors comes the flat crack of something large shattering on stone, a beat of silence, then a scream, high and real-sounding. Then a second scream. The fiddler stops mid-note. Half the room is already running for the green.

**What's actually happened:** one Circle member, the loud elf, broke the black amphora on the stones and screamed, to pull the whole house outside at once. Hellen goes in through a back window the moment the room empties. The elf isn't fighting. By the time anyone reaches the green he's already playing a terrified guest, pointing at the wreck, and the crowd is the real danger.

**Read-aloud (on arrival):** The green is a stampede of silk and lantern light. In the middle, a black jar lies in shards, and a flood of seawater pours out of it far past what any jar could hold, spreading across the grass. Something is rising out of the puddle: heads on long necks, scales the color of a winter harbor, five mouths opening at once. Lanterns come down.

**Loaded:** Three things are happening at once, and the party can't be in all three places. That's where the creative pain comes from.
1. **The serpent** is among the guests. Each round it isn't engaged, one head snaps at the crowd. Someone has to intercept, or a guest gets hurt (and the LLC's reputation with them).
2. **The Search** started at the shatter. Hellen is already in the house.
3. **Casus** is still slipping. Out of Step is at its most useful now, and each use costs him time.

**Investigating after:** whatever the party does about the serpent, the wreck stays on the green. The **black amphora**, in pieces, with a spreading pool of seawater where it broke. A pool like that doesn't sink into the grass. It stays cold. Detect Magic: conjuration, sea-salt, and something old (Jormungandr's taint). A clean DC 15 Investigation turns up the stopper carved with a serpent circling a world, which is the Circle's own symbol. Nothing identifies *who* broke it unless someone clocked the elf; Insight (DC 15) on a guest who screamed first will pick him out as acting.

**Inside, as the Search advances:**
- **1:** Ledger Room. Amelia sees Hellen. If anyone has asked Amelia to keep watch, she frosts a warning onto the front windows: *SOMEONE IN THE BOOKS.* If nobody has asked, she hides.
- **2:** Hellen copies the logbook's Grimhook pages: the commission terms, the Pendant of Stars, the Watchtower.
- **3:** She turns over private rooms. Prime targets are Artcoth's room (Coal's letter, anything tied to the soulbinding sigil) and Casus's or Coriac's room. If the Obsidian Cypher is left in a room rather than carried, she finds it, and the Search clock's 4/4 now means the Circle has the Cypher too.
- **4:** She's out the back window and gone.

**If it comes to a fight:** [[serpent-brood]] *(new stub)*: a brood-spawn of Jormungandr, hauled up out of the sea. Reskinned **Hydra** (MM). Full stat line in Section 6. Tactic: it spreads its heads across as many targets as it can and goes for guests when nobody's engaging it. The loud elf slips out the gate in the confusion.
*Tuned for 6 x level 7: the flood also spits out one **Water Elemental** (tide-spawn, animated seawater with a primitive will). Planned as a long fight, a distraction long enough for the Search clock to run. **If it's too trivial, a second elemental rises from the pool** (Low becomes Moderate). Dials and numbers in [[serpent-brood]].*

**Triggers:**
- *If a PC goes back inside* → the Search freezes. Hellen fights only to get away (Scout stats). She knows Artcoth's face from the Ironspine road.
- *If Hellen is caught* → she carries a copy of the ledger pages, a Circle tattoo matching the watermark, and a way out. She'll trade one true answer for her freedom.
- *If the loud elf is caught* → he's delighted to be caught. It's all theatre. He knows less than he acts like he does.
- *If Amelia's been asked to watch the house* → she sends frost-words to the front windows the instant Hellen enters. That's the party's best early warning.
- *If the serpent kills a guest* → ledger entry at wrap. The gala's reputation takes a hit.

**Leads to:** Scene 5.
