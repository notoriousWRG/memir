The short rest was behind them. They moved through the interior — Artcoth took a shot at the scry-shadow on the path and missed. When they reached the Circled Zone the clearing opened: warmer air, ozone, the stone frame at the center with Ariel bound inside it, and the White Gull captain standing at the treeline edge.

They sent a message. He walked over.

He looked at them and asked directly: *"Did you know?"* He meant the Suggestion. Grimvald answered — said he had a mission, didn't know it would come to this. The captain held it without eruption. Accounting, not rage. *"You knew what I said. 'If the sea resists, I yield.' You heard me say it."* He confirmed with Artcoth that Venn was alive. Said he was glad the Gull would have a captain. Closed the chapter and turned toward the frame.

Casus Misty Stepped to stop him. Failed the Athletics check. The captain slipped through his grip into the arch.

The clearing went still. Ariel received him — not wrath, not compulsion. A tide coming in, patient and certain. He looked back once. The wind took him.

Then, before anyone moved, she spoke. To Coriac. *"The storm follows you."* Unprompted. Before any clock work, before Edrin arrived. The first time she had spoken voluntarily since the bind.

Then Edrin stepped out from the trees.

Coriac engaged him. Drew Ariel toward him in the way that matters — present without commanding. The bind activated. Edrin pulled it because control was slipping, not because he wanted to. The frame shuddered. Casus cast Augury on the question of attacking Ariel directly and received Woe. He shared it with the party.

Initiative was rolled. Two players were absent; the table stopped here. The Reckoning continues next session, freedom clock at 1/4.
