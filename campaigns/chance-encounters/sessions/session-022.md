Ariel lingered after Edrin fell, freed and unmanaged for the first time — bolting Coriac twice, more play than threat, and reaching for Artcoth's bow with a blessing he never heard over the wind. She struck the ingot from Thor's temple with her own lightning and burned her name into it: the Forge Shard bond, sealed wordlessly. She did the same to the [[obsidian-cypher]], lighting its lightning-bolt, fist, and shield faces in sequence before it discharged a cone of lightning into the trees. Then she rose into the clouds, and the storm and fog that had sat on the island since the party arrived cleared behind her.

Gareth took the werebear's head. Coriac took the coin and the fox foot from Edrin's body — proof for the crew, and the fox foot his to keep. Casus read Edrin's spellbook cold (a 21): Mark's theories on the binding, insight that'll sharpen any future check against binding or warding magic, and a spell copied clean into his own book — Bramblebind, a cousin to Spike Growth. He bottled werebear blood in an emptied ink vial while he was at it. Then he cast Identify on the stone arch as a ritual and got the full shape of it: a witnessing stone, ancient, built for oaths sworn and held completely — not a portal. What Edrin had used it for was never its purpose. The captain who stepped through it wasn't taken anywhere. He was taken apart, absorbed into Ariel as storm.

They found Önund and Tjorvi at the Hollowed Hull already finished — three graves dug and filled, Tjorvi singing through it, the vigil that had held them on this island finally over. Coriac told Önund the truth: the captain was the werebear, and he's dead. Önund took it with more weight than anger, thanked him, went back to packing. The coin went into the ground with the Stilled. The fox foot didn't. Two canine teeth came out of the bear's skull for each of them before the funeral wrapped, which is its own kind of answer to what this party is now.

Two ships waited at the shore: the White Gull, and a second wreck the tide had worked loose. Coriac felt the Forge Shard warm as Ariel's storm pulled off toward the horizon, and tried praying through it to bend the wind back — nothing. She isn't steered, not even by him. The party rowed to the wreck first: salvageable, cargo spoiled, decent bones. Dessa and Marek were aboard with two more recovered shipmates. When Elara rowed over to ask after the captain, the party's improvised story — Grimvald disguised as a fake "Captain Prime" — fell apart in front of her, and she read the truth off their faces before anyone said it outright: Captain Crunch was dead, and he'd been a monster the whole time. She didn't rage. She went cold, turned to leave. Grimvald threw Suggestion at her back to make her stay and listen — she made the save clean, and with a nat 1 already in the mix elsewhere, the table let it land as a real failure rather than stack anything to save it. Dessa dove off the party's boat to join her. The White Gull sailed without them. Whatever this party had with Elara, it ended on that water, and not well.

The wreck became theirs. Önund and Tjorvi came aboard for good — the two who actually chose to leave the island with the party — alongside Marek and Ollo, already traveling with them from before. Roles fell out fast: Coriac captain, Önund first mate and bosun, Tjorvi the ship's bard, Ollo navigating, Marek in the galley — the place on Coriac's ship he'd been quietly hoping for since the survivors' camp. Coriac's new Mending cantrip shaved a day off repairs on the crossing. Casus tarred the whole hull black. The table argued names for an hour and landed on the Intimidator.

At port, Coriac put the party's name in front of the Salt Wreck Four — Ron, Tide Tongue, Quickhand, and a fourth whose name didn't survive the table clearly — and opened the kind of connection a standing operation needs. Bellwether House had changed while they were gone: Ovaltine's renovations were mostly done, floors mapped out top to bottom, and a new brass door-knocker with blinking eyes that spoke only to Artcoth before letting the six of them in. The session closed soft — everyone settling into a room that was theirs for the first time, making it show. Coriac closed the ledger on the new ship out loud: they're patrons now, and the operation has a name.
