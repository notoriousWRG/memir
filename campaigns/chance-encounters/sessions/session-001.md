*Calendula's player said farewell. The island arc closed. The party reoriented toward Edook and Ravencrest.*

The party departed the Stormwreck Island cloister. [[Runara]] gave each member a pearl worth 100 gp. Tarak distributed healing potions all around. The Cobalt Coin trading company arranged passage back to Edook. Artcoth dropped off the dragon bone for a custom bow; Gareth left a tooth at a craftsman for something unspecified. The raccoon armor commissioned for Calendula had, in the interim, been adopted by a stray cat — and had to be retrieved.

On the crossing, [[coriac]] had a vision at sea: blood bloomed in the water without warning. A massive shark breached and dove. In its wake, a large fish floated to the surface — impaled not by a harpoon but by something bone, jagged, like a weapon grown from something living.

## The job

Back in Edook, contact was made with an intermediary for a retrieval job. The handoff used a riddle as a passcode: *"The more you have of it, the less you see."* The answer — Darkness — identified a member of a thieves' guild operating as a legitimate trading company. The target: a ruby ring held by a wealthy woman in Ravencrest named [[lady-grimhook]]. The client's identity is withheld; they go by Cole. The ring has a name inscribed inside: Liriel.

The party set out toward Ravencrest.
