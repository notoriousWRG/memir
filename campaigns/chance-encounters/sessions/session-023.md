The party woke to Amelia hovering at the foot of a bed, delighted to have them home — she giggled, phased through the wall into Casus's room and down through the floor, and left "Welcome home" written in frost on the window. Shah Doh drew a heart around it and tried breathing his own message back through the condensation. Downstairs, Ovaltine was shirtless and elbow-deep in the old ritual chamber floor, prying up Bellwether's layered rituals one at a time — Grimvald's Arcana check caught the shape of what he's currently uncovering: the start of a teleportation circle, buried under everything else Bellwether stacked on top of it.

Over breakfast, Casus pitched a success-tour gala at the house — invite the town, invite Grimhook, put Chance Encounters on the map — and the table ran with it. Ovaltine unveiled the payoff of the "edge magic" he'd been building all along: an enchanted quill-and-ledger secretary, writing marketing copy (some of it lifted straight from Grimvald's pub songs), sending it out the window to town, and logging incoming mail in a ledger held down with sea glass. The ledger surfaced a Bold escort job — a merchant needs frost amber shipped through the same storm-draugr-haunted waters the party already knows — and two flagged letters: one from Lord Thorne's household, sent by his steward Voss on Thorne's behalf, reaching out despite his open dislike of the party; and one addressed personally to Artcoth, signed with the same flourished "C" from the original Grimhook commission. Artcoth's investigation (26 total) turned up a watermark under that signature matching the tattoo worn by the group that ambushed the party outside the Underdark mountain pass — the first real thread tying Coal to that ambush.

Ovaltine mentioned, almost in passing, that Grimhook's daily check-ins had stopped about a week ago, right around when the party was still on the river out to the island. Artcoth raised whether that was worth looking into; the table's temperature ran noncommittal — no real urgency, a passing lean toward letting her come to them instead — and nothing was decided or acted on before attention moved to the Thorne letter. They replied to Thorne directly rather than through Voss, inviting him to Bellwether — and in drafting the letter, called Tansy his "daughter" rather than his ward. Nobody at the table caught the slip; it's sitting in Thorne's hands now, uncorrected, headed into next session's meeting. The gala itself got scoped out to roughly a fortnight away, giving the party time in town first — and town is already responding: Grimvald's ballads are circulating in the dockside taverns, Ovaltine's flyers are out, and Thorne soliciting them at all is a sign their name's climbing into higher circles too.

Casus spent the afternoon testing Ovaltine's patience and his own restraint. He tried to fake having already used the potion Ovaltine gave him — passed off a random empty vial as the spent dose — and lost the contested check outright; when Ovaltine asked for the real vial back, Casus drank it instead. The resonance hit fast: a rising hum through his chest and limbs, half-formed runes flickering at the edge of his vision. He failed the Constitution save and blacked out for half a minute while Shah Doh fielded Ovaltine's rapid-fire vitals questions and stopped Grimvald mid-pickpocket. Casus came to disoriented, tried to recover by the fire, and failed the follow-up Arcana check by two. Two failures in, no successes yet — the potion's fine print changed on the spot to match what actually happened at the table: no hard collapse waiting at three failures, just Exhaustion piling up as the real cost of forcing it, clearing whole once he finally gets there.

Before handing the Obsidian Cypher off to Coriac for the afternoon, Casus tried a sequence that had never been tried before — Book, Eye, Lightning. Wrong combination: the runes lit, flickered, and shorted out, and the follow-up roll came up a natural twenty. What that meant in practice was gravity giving out in the room — the cube, both chairs, the table, Coriac, and Casus all lifted into the air for the better part of a minute while Gareth held the floor by a foot hooked under the rug. Casus swam through the air to reach the cube and was still holding it when gravity snapped back and dropped him on his side. All of it happened in full view of Ovaltine's counter, and the cube went straight from that landing into his hands for a proper look. He wouldn't touch it directly — tongs and a wedge to turn it, his quill hovering alongside taking notes — and came within one point of a real breakthrough on his Arcana check before reaching for the lightning rune himself. Casus, mid-vision, shouted a warning in time: heart instead. Ovaltine pressed Heart, then Shield, then Fist — the cube's documented Healing sequence, confirmed live as green light closed the psychic wound Gareth had been carrying since Bramwell's earlier roast.

The session closed with the meeting with Lord Thorne locked in as next session's open, and the gap between now and the gala handed off to downtime: Coriac's solo visit to the Cathedral of Thor to show off the Forge Shard, Artcoth mapping Ravencrest properly with the DM, and the mission board's first real job sitting on the ledger, waiting on a decision.
