*Planning the approach. The gala at Lord Thorne's estate becomes the window.*

The party secured invitations to the gala at [[lord-thorne]]'s estate. Their cover: Freethorn Caterham of the Cadarn Forest Kingdom, with one party member paying a messenger handsomely for discretion.

## Grimhook's study — reconnaissance

The approach shifted. Rather than a direct break-in, the party scoped the gala as the entry point. [[lady-grimhook]]'s manor security was mapped: five guards, a sealed study, an Alarm spell on the door, and an animated armor inside. The ring — a simple band with a ruby, inscribed with the name *Liriel, "song of the Night"* — sat in her study alongside scattered notes about "Liriel's Weave" and powers of invisibility. The study also held references to the Circle: symbolism on the desk, letters from members.

Other items in the study noted for reference: a Dagger of Venom under the desk, a Sword of Wounding on the wall, an orbiting Ioun Stone guarded by a Glyph of Warding, a Tome of Leadership and Influence on the bookshelf, and a Deck of Many Things in a locked drawer. The Codex of Chaos hung in a metal cage — trapped with a poisoned needle.

## The Carusso watch

The mob-connected man (Scarface) from session 2 was watching Aimon Young again. The [[carusso-crime-family]] has eyes in this city.
