# Session 23 — The Ledger Room

---

## 1 — The Charge

The house is quiet in the good way. Ovaltine is underfoot with a slate full of half-finished lists, muttering price adjustments to himself between sentences meant for the party. Downstairs, a new writing desk has already collected a small stack of sealed letters — the first fruits of a system nobody's fully tested yet. Out past the harbor gate, Lady Grimhook's townhouse shows a light in the study window at exactly the right hour, and nothing else. For the first time in a long while, nothing is chasing them. That's the charge tonight: not a threat, a held breath.

*If they do nothing:* Ovaltine finishes the renovations without their input — competent, forgettable choices instead of the ones they'd have picked. The shop restocks and sits there. The intake letters wait; Coal's needle goes unanswered, the Cold Cargo client eventually books someone else, and Tansy Thorne stays missing one day longer. And Grimhook's silence keeps ticking whether they notice it or not — she's already at the Watchtower, working the Pendant, with or without an audience.

## 5 — Scenes

### Scene 1 — Home: Ovaltine's Walkthrough
[[bellwether-house]]

**Read-aloud:** The door-knocker's eyes blink open, find Artcoth's face in the lamplight, and the latch gives before anyone touches it. Inside: the smell of chalk dust and tea, Amelia's frost-word *welcome home* fading off a cold windowpane, and Ovaltine already talking before the door's shut behind you.

**Loaded:** Ovaltine wants three things tonight — sign-off on the house changes, a straight answer on the potion, and an opening to make his ask. The house wants to be *lived in* — Amelia's whole tell is proof of concept.

**Dig in:**
- **Three renovations, DM's to adjust or replace live:**
  1. **The Ledger Room** — a converted study, now the LLC's formal intake: a self-transcribing logbook that writes down any spoken contract terms as they're said. (Doubles as Scene 2's location.)
  2. **Amelia's Nook** — a dollhouse-scale alcove off the main hall, built for her specifically. No mechanic — pure gesture, deepens the relationship.
  3. **The Understair Working.** Clearing the sealed ritual chamber enough to reinforce it, Ovaltine's found something under the old scarring that isn't ritual damage — a diagram, too large and too specific to be leftover from Dr. Bellwether's soul-ritual. His trained eye (School of Conjuration) reads it as the bones of a teleportation circle. Whether Bellwether was building one, or it's some byproduct of what he did to his family, is unknown. **He's not waiting on a greenlight — he's already down there.** Play it as an arcane construction site: chalk lines taped off, containment wards half-strung, tools and a half-eaten sandwich on an overturned crate, Ovaltine covered in dust and delighted. He'll happily narrate what he's found to anyone who asks. This keeps going, unattended and in the background, session to session, unless the party explicitly tells him to stop — not a renovation that closes tonight, but one that *opens*: a multi-session unlock, components and answers TBD.
- The restocked shop (full list in **Section 7**).
- If anyone lingers near wherever the Obsidian Cypher is kept, a faint arcane hum that wasn't there before — Ovaltine's noticed it too, and it's part of what has him distracted tonight.

**Triggers:**
- *If Casus or Coriac let Ovaltine examine the Cypher* → this is the moment the existing trigger fires: "Casus investigates the Eld entity further → the entity makes contact. Agenda unknown until it does. Do not predecide."
- *If anyone pushes toward drinking the potion tonight* → still irreversible, still their call. Existing trigger holds: Ariel notices, proportional to intent, no longer island-bound to do so.
- *If they engage warmly with Amelia's nook* → no mechanic, just goodwill banked for later.
- *Default: Ovaltine keeps digging* → new active thread either way, sync to `bellwether-house.md`'s Threads section at wrap.
- *If they tell him to stop* → he does, immediately, a little crestfallen but not pushing back — and the diagram stays half-uncovered until someone changes their mind.

**Leads to:** Ovaltine walks them to the Ledger Room himself — Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — The Chance Encounters LLC Intake
[[bellwether-house]] — the Ledger Room

**Read-aloud:** A desk shaped like a ship's wheel, a logbook that writes on its own, and a short stack of sealed letters weighted down with a chunk of sea-glass. Ovaltine straightens them like he's presenting a court appearance. *"Chance Encounters LLC. Heroes for hire — now with international reach, thanks to a certain ship."* He's clearly delighted with the joke and only half joking.

**Loaded:** Three proposals flagged as "interesting." Ovaltine wants at least one greenlit — he helped build this and wants to see it work. The Cold Cargo letter is the obvious nudge toward actually using the Intimidator.

**Dig in — the letters:**

1. **"Cold Cargo."** A merchant needs a shipment of frost-amber moved through waters the [[storm-draugr]] survivors still haunt. Framed as an Escort-lane job for [[the-intimidator]] — the direct, low-friction way to put the ship's mission mechanic in front of the table tonight. Can also be taken personally if they want the fight instead.

2. **A sealed letter, signed.** Coal's mark — the same operative who ran Artcoth's tier during the ring job.
   > *Chance Encounters. Better than I expected, if I'm honest — Jimmy always said you had more in you than the job showed. Rare that he was right about anything.*
   > *I'm not here to relitigate old business. What's done is done, and you did it clean enough that I've kept my distance since. That's not nothing, coming from me.*
   > *One question, professional to professional: have you asked yourselves what she's actually doing right now? Not what she told you — what she's doing. I already know. You should find out too, for your own sake.*
   > *I'm not offering you a job. I'm offering you a reason to ask better questions before you're too far in to like the answers.*
   > *— C.*

   No ask, no threat — Coal reading as composed and genuinely respectful of their work, angling to turn suspicion toward Grimhook rather than recruit them outright. Not a job offer; a gambit.

3. **A stiff, formal letter, unsigned by its subject.**
   > *On behalf of Lord Thorne's household — and, I stress, at his direction, difficult as I imagine that is for you to picture — I am writing regarding his ward, Tansy, who has not been seen in six days. Discretion is paramount. His lordship would prefer this handled quietly, and would prefer even more that you not mention this letter to anyone who might repeat it. Compensation is generous. Details available on request.*
   > *— H. Voss, Steward*

   New NPC stub: [[tansy-thorne]] — halfling, ~15, missing six days. Nobody at the intake quite knows why Thorne has a ward in the first place; rumors vary, none confirmed. **Deliberately unresolved** — runaway, taken, or something else. Decide from how the party investigates, not in advance.

**Triggers:**
- *If they run Cold Cargo as a ship mission* → resolve live using [[the-intimidator]]'s Mission Mechanic (Escort lane, Standard or Bold tier).
- *If they take Cold Cargo personally* → this becomes a session spine in its own right; don't resolve it tonight.
- *If Artcoth reads Coal's letter* → private or shared beat, DM's call. Let the suspicion about Grimhook sit and simmer rather than resolving it — that's the point of the gambit.
- *If they take the Thorne case* → likely next session's spine given the "leave doors open" design; tonight is pickup, not resolution. Given Thorne's established hostility to the party, play the household's ask as reluctant/quiet rather than warm.

**Leads to:** Whatever they pick — or, if nothing grabs them, Ovaltine mentions almost offhand that he hasn't seen Lady Grimhook's people in the market all week. *"Not that it's my business. But her lamps have been on the same hour every night, which is either very disciplined or very staged."*
