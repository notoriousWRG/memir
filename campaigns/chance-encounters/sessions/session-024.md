The gala came a week after the last session, and the table opened on a recap for Craig: the Intimidator's first mission had gone well, Grull taking point with Tjorvi helping, for a payout of 450 gold. Casus had spent the week shaky and exhausted, waking unrested and walking the house like a ghost. Ovaltine was taking notes and mixing tonics, and some of them bought Casus four or five hours of feeling better. He looked graver as the days went on. Gareth had Ovaltine enchant his room against snoopers, and Ovaltine left the upstairs wards and alarms intact while switching off the main floor's for the party. As the lanterns came up, the party took positions: Artcoth and Shah Doh at the front door and foyer, Gareth lurking in the shadows and sampling the food, Coriac in the music room with Grimvald at the piano.

Bramwell announced the guests badly ("ARTCOTH, a WOOL MERCHANT, ARTCOTH, his WIFE, who is WEARING HIS CLOAK"), and Artcoth set him straight with the threat of the doors coming off their hinges: speak up only if something genuinely threatens the house. Gary, who runs the Anchor and Tap down at the docks, turned up with a crumpled invitation that had flown into his pub window from Artcoth's study. Artcoth let him in with a warning. Amelia made mischief all evening, knocking over glasses and writing "welcome" across the front windows. Grimhook still hadn't reached out, and she didn't show. Artcoth, filling in what he'd noticed over the week (a Perception of 16), recalled her manor: guards dismissed, only the study and bedroom lamps lit and switching on and off on a regular cadence, nobody ever passing the windows. He said he'd check the house after the gala, which will likely be handled offline unless the rest of the party wants to tag along.

Casus slipped through time three times. In the dining room he saw the old house: Amelia, healthy and substantial, at a table with Eleanor, then a loud noise from below, the ritual chamber floor open and smoke rising, a man walking out. He came to collapsed in front of Coriac, and no one else had seen any of it. When Artcoth clapped him on the shoulder he stood in a forest and watched Artcoth's arrow land in Jimmy, the man who led the ambush on the Ironspine road, and he blurred for a moment in the telling. The third time he stood knee-deep in water outside the house, a scaled tail sliding past and people running. Artcoth hauled him to Ovaltine, who answered "The potion doesn't give anything. It provokes what's there. A man who looks at time sideways," took Casus aside to rest, and later walked him upstairs. Out of Step was never used.

Four sets of visitors had their moments. Lord Thorne took Artcoth and Shah upstairs and said he'd come only to speak where nobody would hear: Tansy had been gone two weeks. He gave three details: the window latch was opened from the inside, she spent her evenings talking to his Draconic-speaking dragon skull, and her good boots were gone while her purse was left behind. Artcoth's Insight (17) showed he was holding something back, but his Persuasion (11) couldn't pry it loose. Thorne took offense, said his staff was at their disposal, and left. Artcoth said he'd be by within the week with help.

Two guests nobody announced arrived in feathered black cloaks. Shah and Coriac, passing a DC 20 Perception, caught only the hems of the cloaks as they slipped past Bramwell. These were Huginn and Muninn in human form. Coriac's Insight (21) told him the human shape wasn't their own. They asked Grimvald for a verse and he sang a piece of the party's own adventures (Jesse's choice, relayed by the DM). "It is him," said the smaller one. Coriac stopped the taller one reaching into his cloak, but the raven dropped a black feather into Grimvald's hand anyway: "Everyone sings for him eventually." The feather carries a place in the far north and a date about a month away. Passing Gareth, they stopped at his sword: "That's not ours," then "It was, once." Two plain feathers fell where the ravens took flight.

Brother Sölvi, a cleric of Thor, and Dame Hervor of the shining order arrived next. Sölvi had heard from Brother Ogle, the priest from Shah's earlier visit to the temple, that an unknown member of their order had come by, and went looking for Shah. He touched Shah's chain mail and it glimmered in answer; the armor, he said, was left at the temple by one of the Dawnbound's highest knights, who said only that "one day someone would need it." Hervor was hostile and asked what Shah wouldn't do even for friends; he answered that he wouldn't betray the brethren. Sölvi held her back: "this is his by right." Coriac, whose hammer and armor Sölvi also noticed, had done the welcoming.

Last, a man sat down across from Artcoth and Gareth. Artcoth's Insight told him this was the man behind the original commission through Jimmy and the flourished-C letter. Coal put his card in Artcoth's hand: "Jimmy always told me you had no vision." He remarked that Lady Grimhook was absent. When Gareth leaned in on his sleeve, a coiled-serpent tattoo with barbs behind its head showed at his wrist, and Coal rolled up the sleeve to display it: "Grimhook had this mark once as well. Did you know that?" Artcoth refused business on the night of the gala and told him to make an appointment or knock on the door.

The night broke on a shatter and a scream from outside. Water was pouring from the fountain, with black pottery shards in it (Coriac's natural 20 spotted them), and then heads reared up out of it, one after another. The crowd panicked. Shah kept people inside; Gareth pointed with Mage Hand and drove most of the guests toward the back door; Coriac told them to run but not too far. Coal, unmoved: "Sounds like someone may have had a bit too much." Artcoth warned him that if he was behind this he'd regret it. Coal answered only: "You have no idea." The session stopped with the creature up and roaring and the fight yet to start.
