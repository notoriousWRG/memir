**The law of the island tonight:** *it takes back from those who take.* The Scale, the junk hoard, the Stilled, and the vanishing wonder are all the same rule wearing different faces. Witness and you receive; seize and you lose. Let that, not dread, be the thing the night keeps teaching.

## 1 — The Charge
First light. Casus's dome thins to nothing and the island is waiting where it was. The party moves inland with [[ollo]] — eager, out of his depth — leaving the beach, the watching draugr offshore, and the White Gull behind them. The fog pulls back from the path ahead like it wants them to come. Something inland has been singing all night, too low to place. The survivors they're walking toward are not strangers: they are [[coriac]]'s own old crew, wrecked and scattered, and the captain glimpsed in the trees is the man Coriac once served under.

*If they do nothing:* the fog closes the beach behind them and nudges them inward — the island only offers one direction. Stall too long and a day burns: [[elara-venn]] and [[wrenn]] start making their own call back on the ship, and no one is looking for the missing captain.

## 5 — Scenes

### Scene 1 — The Spar Field · *Threshold (Wonder → unease)*
[[ariels-island]]
**Read-aloud:** The fog lifts to show a forest that was never alive — masts and spars from a dozen wrecks standing upright in the moss, angled like trees that grew toward something. Wind moves through them and they *sing*: a low chord, almost a sea-shanty, almost a voice. Every spar wears moss up one side only, all of it pointing the same way — inland.

**Battlemap layout:** Top-down plan of a drowned-forest clearing. A mud-and-black-sand flat forms the entrance along the bottom (south) edge; from it a narrow moss-lit path winds north, curving, to an open mossy clearing set left of center where a whalebone balance scale stands as the focal feature. Dense thickets of upright ship-masts and broken spars crowd the left and right margins and the top corners as difficult terrain, thinning to open moss down the middle. One lone mast stands oddly apart and too straight just east of the clearing; scattered single spars dot the open ground, all leaning and trailing moss toward the top of the map.

**Loaded:** The island opens with a gift, not a threat — *sounds that give delight and hurt not.* It wants them curious and moving inward, and it's reading their posture: read the place and it cooperates; force through it and the fog tightens (escalation tier 1). The song is a pattern, not decoration.

**Dig in:**
- The chord *repeats* — half a shanty, missing its turn. Hum/play it back (Performance) or pick it apart (Arcana/History) → the moss-direction sharpens into a true safe path; you skip the worst of what's ahead.
- Names and dates carved into the spars — wrecks across decades, and among them the name of Coriac's old ship. One cut is *fresh*.
- Ollo lights up — this is the kind of puzzle he lives for, and the song points exactly at the circle on his map marked *"don't go here yet."*
- One "spar" doesn't move with the wind. It's standing too still. → the **[[threshold-warden]]**.
**The puzzle — the Unbalanced Scale:** the Warden hangs a heaped pan and an empty one from its whalebone arms and will not open the path until the scale is **level**. *Stage it, don't solve it.* Hold only the triggers; let them find the middle. (Full framing in [[threshold-warden]].)
**If it comes to a fight:** [[threshold-warden]] — *instance of [[hollow-warden]], CR 5.* Procedural: nonlethal until struck, never pursues past its line. It would rather you balanced the scale.
**Triggers:**
- The pans level (however) → it lowers its arms, steps aside, goes still — *relieved.*
- Take from the loaded pan without replacing it → it reads theft; it wakes (and the island's law is watching — see the Stilled).
- Play the spar-song back → moss blazes a safe path; the island *warms* (clarity, not tension).
- Hack through / ignore the song → fog tightens, difficult terrain.
**Leads to:** the path opens toward the Hollowed Hull.

### Scene 2 — The Hollowed Hull · *The Absurd Court (Mischief, sad undertow)*
[[ariels-island]]
**Read-aloud:** An overturned ship lies keel-to-sky, big as a longhouse, its hull cut and braced into a hall. Smoke leaks from a crooked driftwood chimney. Inside, someone is singing — badly, gloriously, at the top of their lungs. A throne of lashed driftwood. A flag of stitched sailcloth flying a crown that someone painted from memory and got wrong.

**Battlemap layout:** Flat top-down floor plan of the inside of an overturned ship's hull with the roof and keel removed, so the curved wooden walls read only as a long oval outline enclosing the space, running bottom to top. A cut doorway breaks the outline at the bottom edge. Inside, the main hall fills the center and lower half: a lashed-driftwood throne on a low dais, a fire pit, scattered benches, and a heaped junk "treasury" along the left wall. The top quarter is partitioned off as a dim, frost-rimed back chamber where a cluster of still figures stands. Plank-and-sand floor throughout; warm firelight in the center fades to cold blue at the back.

**Loaded:** This is Group B's last camp — Coriac's old crew — and the survivors who *kept their minds* have crowned themselves rather than face what happened to the ones who didn't. [[onund]] holds court; [[tjorvi]] keeps the bit alive. They're drunk on something the island fermented and they are *very* funny — and they will not go to the back of the hull, because that is where the crew who **took** the good spoils are now. The comedy is the denial; the dread is one room away. Play the court for real laughs; let the cold leak in only when earned.
**Dig in:**
- **The court.** Önund demands tribute and fealty, "knights" a PC, and declares righteous war on the island's "usurper" (he means [[edrin-vael]] — his own old captain, a dot he won't connect aloud). Real intel is buried in the bluster: the warm dead air inland, the stone frame, the captain at the treeline.
- **The hoard.** A heap of junk he calls a treasury. The reason it's junk *is the lesson:* the crew who took anything that mattered are the Stilled. Önund hoards only what the island doesn't want back. Tjorvi will beg the party not to take anything.
- **The back of the hull.** Cold; the singing stops if anyone goes there. The **[[the-stilled]]** — crewmates frozen mid-reach, looping the moment the island took them for taking. Group A's missing crew, found.
- **Group B's account** — Tjorvi has the clearest one: who took what, in what order, how the forest answered.
**If it comes to a fight:** [[the-stilled]] — *reskin of Specter; minor for L7, heavy in weight.* **Passive unless someone takes from the back room.** Taking repeats the sin → they complete their reach toward the taker (Life Drain). Witnessing and *naming* them lays them to rest. This is a creative-pain beat, not an XP beat.
**Triggers:**
- Play along with the court → Önund warms, spills the intel, offers "alliance" against the usurper.
- Break the bit hard / mock them → Tjorvi cracks first; the jollity fails; the back-room cold seeps forward.
- **Take anything from the back room** → the Stilled turn; the island's law lands in the room.
- **Name a Stilled, truly** (Ollo or Coriac can; the clowns can't) → that one goes still for good. The grief, and the ledger.
- **Ollo recognizes a shipmate among them** → relational gut-punch. Decide how Ollo breaks (goes quiet like [[marek]], or won't leave).
**Leads to:** the clowns point inland at the "usurper" — or the party flees the cold — toward the warm air and the Circled Zone.

### Scene 3 — The Warm Air · *The Isle's Wonder (Wonder, then motivated twist)*
[[ariels-island]]
**Read-aloud:** Past the hull the trees stop leaning *toward* and start leaning *away*. The air warms by degrees and smells of ozone and rain that hasn't fallen. The fog pulls back like a held breath — and in the clearing ahead is something that shouldn't be here: a table set for a feast, or figures dancing in the mist, or just music, sweet and close and meant for them.
**Battlemap layout:** Top-down plan of a warm forest clearing. Trees ring the edges and lean outward, away from center; fog banks the outer margins and pulls back from an open middle of mossy earth. At the center stands a freestanding storm-grey stone frame about twelve feet tall, the focal feature, with faint lightning scorch radiating from its base. A low rise with a single vantage point sits at the upper-right edge. The ground reads warmer and clearer toward the center, dimmer and fog-choked at the corners.
**Loaded:** [[ariel]] stages a wonder — beautiful first, and *true*: it reflects what the party has been carrying. It curdles only if seized (the island's law again — take and lose). On the high ground at the clearing's edge stands the silhouette of [[edrin-vael]] — his **scry-shadow**, not his body — watching his old crew and Coriac. Wonder dominant; the twist is earned, never sprung.
**Dig in:**
- **The wonder.** Witness it (don't grab it) and it gives — a face, a memory, a sentence almost spoken. Seize/consume/dominate it and it vanishes like a snuffed candle, and the fog tightens.
- **The figure on the rise.** It is Edrin, perceived through the island's air — a presence that holds your eye and then isn't there. Approach directly → it withdraws (he isn't ready). Hold still and watch → it watches back, and he may speak a few words through it. His body is at the frame.
- **Coriac's thread — Ariel's observation (decided, below).** Ariel is near the edge of this. If Coriac *actively reaches* (as he did in the lightning strike — engaging, not commanding), her unfinished sentence finally lands: **"The storm follows you."** Run it from §9.
- **The threshold.** Ears pop; the fog pushes back against anyone crossing into the Circled Zone. Don't let them cross casually — that's the Reckoning.
**Triggers:**
- A PC reaches to *witness* the wonder → it gives a true thing.
- A PC tries to *seize* it → it vanishes; island tension rises a tier.
- Approach Edrin's shadow directly → it withdraws; the treeline clock ticks. Hold and watch → he engages, briefly.
- Coriac *actively* reaches toward Ariel → she finishes the sentence (see §9).
**Leads to:** the edge of the Circled Zone. The Reckoning is the next thing — likely next session's open.

- **The White Gull captain is a separate, current person** — missing, no body ([[white-gull-captain]]). He is *not* the treeline captain. Don't merge them.
- **Grimvald's Suggestion on the White Gull captain is unacknowledged.** Don't have anyone reveal it tonight unless you mean to light it.
- **Ariel reached Coriac *voluntarily*; Edrin doesn't know** (story spine). Edrin must not learn it yet — that's the Reckoning's detonator.

## 9 — Settled (decided before play)
The hard calls are made. Run from these; don't re-decide at the table.

- **Hollow Warden = a challenge, not a riddle.** The Unbalanced Scale. Stage it, hold the two triggers (level → open; take from the loaded pan → wake), let the players invent the middle. New principle now lives in `dm-philosophy.md` → "Challenges — Stage Them, Don't Solve Them."
- **Treeline captain = [[edrin-vael]]**, perceived as a scry-shadow on the island's air — not his body. And the crew here (A and B) are Coriac's old crew; Edrin captained them.
- **The Stilled = Group B who took spoils / cut the forest; the island took them back.** They loop in the back of the hull. They wake only if someone *takes* from that room; they rest if *named*.
- **Ariel's sentence = "The storm follows you."** An observation — what she sees most in Coriac. Lands only if Coriac reaches *actively* (engaging the storm, not commanding it — the lightning-strike posture). Layers, to play in order as he stays open:
  1. *Literal / wry* — chaos and trouble trail this crew; the weather has been hunting them since the crossing. (A small relief — she's almost amused.)
  2. *Divine* — he carries Thor's name well; the god comes with it. The storm follows because part of one already walks with him.
  3. *Personal (platonic)* — she reaches *him*, of all the mortals here, because he is the one who **engages the storm without trying to own it.** He channels; he does not command. That is the opposite of [[edrin-vael]], and it is why she chose him and not her captor.
  - **What it answers:** the question Coriac has carried — whether his bond to storms is truly his, or just Thor's borrowed weather, or luck. Her answer: it's a relationship, and he's been having it correctly. Guidance, if he asks for it: *"Do not bind it. Do not flee it. Stand in it." —* which is also her own plea about her captivity, said sideways.
- **Optional (improv-safe, decide only if reached):** Artcoth's gloves' one ability; Casus's Ovaltine reaction on the island.

## 10 — Ending Conditions
A satisfying stop: the party stands at the **threshold of the Circled Zone**, having read the singing field, survived the clowns' court, seen what the island did to the crew who took, and witnessed (or spurned) the wonder — with Edrin's shadow watching and Coriac one reach away from Ariel's first words. Don't push them across; let them arrive there and breathe.
**Hand-off seed:** *"At the frame's edge. Ariel waits inside it and has told Coriac the storm follows him; Edrin is near in the flesh and still doesn't know she chose Coriac. The crew's old captain and the bound storm, in one clearing — that's the Reckoning."*
