# Session 25 — The Serpent on the Green

---

## 1 — The Charge

The fountain is still pouring seawater, and the brood of the World-Serpent rises out of it, its heads swinging toward the last guests on the green. Upstairs, Hellen and two Circle cutters are already inside, past the main floor and working toward the Ledger Room. Casus is up there too, half out of time, with Ovaltine beside him and a case of unlabeled vials open at his feet. Coal hasn't moved from his chair across from Artcoth. His people are inside, his monster is outside, and he means to walk out the front door when it's done.

*If they do nothing:* the serpent kills a straggler on the green and the gala becomes a ledger entry. The Search reaches 4/4, so the Circle copies Grimhook's pages and leaves knowing she went to the Watchtower. Coal finishes his drink and vanishes. Casus slips away mid-stand, with Ovaltine spent and Amelia the only one watching.

## 5 — Scenes

### Scene 1 — The Green
[[bellwether-house]], the front green

**Read-aloud:** Lanterns swing on their strings as the last guests stumble for the gate. Water keeps coming out of the fountain, black and cold and far too much of it, and the heads come up out of it one after another on long necks, scales the grey of a winter harbor. Behind you a chair scrapes: Coal, settling in to watch.

**Loaded:** Most guests went out the back (Gareth and Shah saw to that). Stragglers remain: Gary, a drunk customs officer, and the loud elf playing terrified. The serpent bites at the nearest warm thing, and nobody has to be there for it to kill someone. The house wants its people back inside, and Bramwell will shout if the alarm is ignored.

**Dig in:**
- **Coriac** IDs it with **advantage**: a brood-child of Jörmungandr, grandchild of Loki. The rune on Mjolnir's Fury's handle goes warm.
- The pool doesn't drain. Detect Magic shows conjuration, sea-salt, and something old.
- DC 15 Investigation on the shards turns up the stopper, carved as a serpent circling a world: the Circle's mark.
- DC 15 Insight on the stragglers: the man in parrot silks is acting.

**If it comes to a fight:** [[serpent-brood]] (reskinned Hydra) + one tide-spawn (Water Elemental). It spreads its heads across targets and goes for guests nobody is guarding. **Only radiant damage stops regrowth.** Fire doesn't, including Gareth's blade, so this is Shah's fight.

**Baldr's people are still on the green.** [[dame-hervor]] (Knight reskin: Dawnlit Blade, 1d8 radiant 3×/fight, so a second source of radiant) holds the line on the stragglers, and [[brother-solvi]] (Priest reskin) blesses **three PCs** over the fight: Warding Light, Sunlit Strike (+1d8 radiant, stops regrowth), or Mercy. After his third blessing, or round 3, the tide-spawn's undertow takes him and **Hervor drops back** to him with Lightborn Step. That leaves Shah's question unanswered in the middle of the fight. Details in both NPC files.

**Triggers:**
- *Gareth draws or ignites the sword:* Feanoro answers in the flame, calm and warm: *"Messengers are tested too. Do not let crows tell you who you serve."*
- *Gareth draws first blood on the serpent:* DC 13 Wis save, or the corruption track advances one step.
- *The serpent kills a guest:* ledger entry at wrap, and the LLC's name takes the hit.
- *Search hits 1:* the alarm fires (Scene 2).
- *A PC goes inside:* that PC joins Scene 2. The green gets harder.

**Leads to:** Scene 2 (intercut) and Scene 3.

### Scene 2 — The Stair (Casus's Last Stand)
[[bellwether-house]], upstairs: the landing, the Ledger Room, Casus's door

**Alarm — Gareth's request, S24.** Gareth asked Ovaltine for added security, and he got it: a **mental alarm** keyed to the upstairs wards. When a non-resident crosses them, it sounds **inside Gareth's head** and nobody else's: a bell in the skull, loud enough to wake him from a dead sleep. Gareth knows *that* someone is in the house, and *where* in the broad sense (the landing). He doesn't know who. Everyone else only hears it if Gareth shouts. Give Zack the cue: *"You hear it. The bell in your head. Upstairs."* Whether Gareth tells the table, and how fast, is the creative pain: he's mid-fight on the green with his sword in the serpent.

**Read-aloud (if anyone goes up):** The house is warm and dim, and the lanterns on the landing are out. A small figure crosses it without a sound, and two broad shapes follow her. At the far end Casus stands in front of his own door with Ovaltine beside him, uncorking something with his teeth. Behind them the ritual-chamber floor hums, and the hum gets louder every time you breathe.

**Loaded:** Hellen wants the ledger. Ovaltine will spend what he has. Amelia is watching from the mirror. Casus is the only thing between them and the house's secrets, and every trick he has costs him time.
- **Searchers:** Hellen + 2 serpent-sworn cutters. Gareth's room is enchanted against snoopers, so they skip it.
- **Casus:** 2 levels of Exhaustion (2024 rules: −4 to d20 tests, −10 ft speed).
- **Out of Step:** reaction, ±1d4 to any d20 within 30 ft. Advances Unmoored immediately.
- **Homebrew: Hold the Hour** *(read to Chris):* "Once, as an action, you pull one room you can see out of time. Until the night ends, nothing in it can be opened, taken, or moved, and anyone inside is shoved out into the hall. Unmoored jumps to 3/4." **Creative pain:** he can save one room. Which one: the Ledger Room, Artcoth's room, his own, or the ritual chamber?

**Ovaltine's tricks.** He's a level 9 Conjuration wizard (use **Mage**, MM: AC 12 / 15 with mage armor, HP 40) who fights with his shelf, not his spell list. Each vial is **homebrew**, one use, thrown or poured as an action, and DC 15:
- **Bottled Squall:** thrown. 20-ft burst of wind: Str save or pushed 15 ft and knocked prone. Clears a landing; also blows out the lanterns.
- **Tanglemoss Tonic:** thrown. A 10-ft area of the stair sprouts grasping moss: difficult terrain, and Str save or restrained.
- **Dreamless Draught:** poured onto a rag or thrown at one creature within 20 ft. **Incapacitates one:** Con save or unconscious for 1 minute (damage doesn't wake them). He aims it at Hellen first, because she's the one with the pages. It's the one that actually stops the Search.
- **Last Bottle (sacrificial).** If Casus would go down, a PC would die, or the Search is about to hit 3, Ovaltine breaks the whole case of unlabeled vials across the landing. Everything within 30 ft of him except him and the PCs and Casus makes a Con save or is incapacitated until the end of its next turn, and the stair becomes difficult terrain for the night. **The cost:** Ovaltine goes to 0 HP, unconscious but stable, and Ovaltine's Oddities loses its whole stock (the party's 10% discount is moot until he restocks). It also doesn't count as a round for the Search. It's his call to make, and it comes out of his own shelf. If a PC pleads with him to hold it back, he'll listen. It's the sacrifice, not the death.
- **If Casus needs an anchor:** Ovaltine will offer to *be* the tether himself: his name spoken into the ritual circle, so Casus can aim back at him. It costs him a year of his own memory (DM's pick), which he'll pay without arguing. This is one of the doors in Scene 4's anchor challenge.

**Dig in:**
- Hellen carries a blank ledger-copy book and a serpent tattoo at her wrist.
- The ritual-chamber circle brightens toward Casus like a compass needle. DC 14 Arcana: it's reaching for him.
- Amelia's frost spells out on the landing mirror: *HE'S FADING.*

**If it comes to a fight:** Hellen (Scout), 2 cutters (Thug). They avoid the circle. Hellen breaks off the moment the frost starts.

**Triggers:**
- *Someone asks Amelia for help:* she frosts the stair (difficult terrain for intruders) and freezes a door shut. Hellen panics and loses her next turn.
- *Hellen is caught:* she trades one true answer for her freedom.
- *A searcher reaches Casus's room at Search 3 and the Cypher is there:* the Circle has the Cypher at 4/4.
- *Gareth ignores the alarm for 2 rounds:* Bramwell shouts it to Artcoth, loudly and badly.
- *Unmoored hits 4/4 mid-stand:* he goes there and then. Scene 4 plays without him; his three things are found where he stood.

**Leads to:** Scene 4.

### Scene 3 — Coal's Exit
Wherever Artcoth left him (the table by the window)

**Read-aloud:** Coal sets his glass down exactly where the ring it left was, stands, and buttons his coat as if the evening has ended on time. *"Thank you for the drink. I'll see myself out."*

**Loaded:** Moriarty doesn't stand where the fighting is. He's decided that Hellen, the cutters, the loud elf and the serpent are all expendable, and he has already priced them. If the party presses him, he'll say so, mildly: *"Hellen is upstairs. I'd hurry."* He is not going to be in danger tonight, and he has made sure of it.

**His exit — "The Last Move" (homebrew, DM-only item).** A black rook from a chess set on a chain, worn as a watch-fob, carved with the serpent-and-world. It's a one-use Circle waymark, set days ago. **Rule, table-ready:** "When he turns it face-down on the table (a bonus action, no check), at the start of his next turn he's pulled through the coil to a prepared circle somewhere in Ravencrest. Anyone within 5 ft of him can ride along or not; he doesn't care. The turn between is a sentence of dialogue and nothing else." It needs no components, no spell slot, and it isn't a spell, so it can't be Counterspelled.
- **What stops it:** physically taking the rook before he flips it (AC 18, Sleight of Hand DC 18; Artcoth's gloves and reflexes make this a real option); breaking it (AC 18, 5 HP) before the turn passes; or Dispel Magic (DC 15) cast *before* the next turn begins. Hold the Hour on the room he's in also works: he's shoved out into the hall and can't use the table.
- **The tell:** he sets it on its side, not on its base, in the thumb-ring his glass left. If anyone looks closely, the rook has no face.
- **What he leaves behind:** a ring of cold salt water where he stood, a smell of harbor, and his card. On the back, in his hand: *Ask her about her ring finger. Then knock.*

**Layers, each one only shows when Artcoth pushes through the one before:**
1. **Guest-right.** He's an undisguised guest. Bramwell let him in, and the town will hear how the LLC treats its guests.
2. **The scream.** If anyone lays hands on him, the loud elf howls **"MURDER!"** at the gate in front of every straggler.
3. **The leverage.** *"I'd hold me, if I were you. It would be so informative."* He means it: whatever they learn by holding him, he's already accounted for.
4. **The rook.**

**Triggers:**
- *Something genuinely surprises him* (Hold the Hour, Amelia, Hellen caught): one beat of silence, and then he offers a real trade, a true answer for his people's safe exit. He'd still flip the rook after.
- *They let him go:* he walks out the front door and the doubt is planted.
- *They block the door:* he flips the rook where he stands.
- *They take the rook:* he smiles, stays exactly where he is, and says *"Well played."* He is not in any danger, and he knows it. His next move is already running somewhere else (DM's call what).

**Leads to:** Scene 4.
