### Scene 2 — The Bind Breaks
*[[ariels-island]] — The Circled Zone*

**Read-aloud:** "The stone frame shatters from the inside — not an explosion, a release, the way a fist opens. The wind drops to almost nothing. In that nothing: a voice that hasn't spoken aloud before. Unhurried. Certain. A little wry. 'The storm follows you.' She's saying it to Coriac. She has been trying to say it since the crossing."

**Loaded:** This is the Forge Shard's resolution moment — the bond completes here if Coriac's defining act has landed (speaking for her, refusing to bind her, mediating at cost to himself). See [[forge-shard]] for the "Two Storms" mechanic once it triggers: tier 1 (storm-sense) unlocks immediately.

**Dig in:**
- If Coriac asks for guidance: *"Do not bind it. Do not flee it. Stand in it."* — her own plea about the bind, said sideways.
- Layer what "the storm follows you" means across the literal (chaos has followed this crew), the divine (he carries Thor's name and the god comes with it), and the personal (she chose him because he engages without owning) — don't front-load all three, let it land as Coriac sits with it.
- Edrin's ending resolves here per whichever trigger fired in Scene 1.

**Triggers:**
- *Edrin's ending was "consumed" (totem, killed in bear form)* → no last word; the totem burns out with him; play it uglier and sadder than a clean death
- *Edrin's ending was "shaky exile" (talked down mid-transformation)* → he reverts human, leaves, but doesn't get the composed line — he's just broken and gone
- *Edrin's ending was clean exile* → *"Tell the crew. Their captain is dead. It's better that they think this."* He walks into the interior and doesn't come back
- *Edrin's ending was clean drop* → already resolved in Scene 1's bind-snap

### Scene 3 — After
*[[ariels-island]] — Circled Zone → Hollowed Hull → the beach*

**Read-aloud (the Stilled):** "Back in the Hollowed Hull, time catches up with the four figures in the back room. Edrin's hold on the island was what sustained them. It's gone. They pass — quietly, without violence. Onund and Tjorvi are with them. When the party returns through the Spar Field, Tjorvi meets them at the door. He doesn't say much. 'They're at rest.'"

**Loaded:** Tjorvi and Onund's whole reason for staying is resolved — nothing left to keep vigil over. This is where Tjorvi can plausibly ask to come aboard, once the burial/goodbye beat is played, not before.

**Read-aloud (the beach):** "Elara Venn isn't on the ship — she's on the black sand with whoever she could gather, watching the interior. She stopped moving when the island changed. She reads your faces before you say anything."

*"He's not coming back."* Not a question.

If the captain's parting words reach her — *"Elara will know what to do"* — she holds it a long moment. *"I know."* She's the new captain of the White Gull. That's the fact the world ends the night on.

**Falling action — the payoff beats, played in this rough order:**

1. **Tjorvi and Onund** bury or honor the Stilled first — a quiet scene, not rushed. Once that's done, Tjorvi asks to come aboard something new rather than stay on the island. Earned, not handed over.
2. **The new ship.** As the bind breaks and Ariel's grip on the island loosens, one of the wrecks pinned against the rocks comes free on the tide — not the White Gull, a separate hull. She isn't handing them a reward; she's simply no longer holding the grip closed. It needs a name (let the table name it) and real repair before it's a going concern.
3. **Ollo** is offered a place aboard as navigator/cartographer — play it as a real offer, not a foregone conclusion, even though he's likely to say yes. He charted the whole island; this is the first time that's been useful rather than just witnessed.
4. **Dessa and Marek** stay on the island — a standing contact for later, not folded into the crew. Reason to come back to this water without needing to move everyone.
5. **The fog lifts, the air clears** — the island stops being hostile. First time the air smells like open sea since the draugr fight.

**Dig in:**
- Don't over-mechanize the new ship/crew tonight. It's a standing resource — a place to send them on small off-screen tasks between sessions later. Build the real system if and when it's earned.
- Grimhook is not part of tonight's beach scene, but the seed for next prep is: she's gone after the Pendant of Stars herself now — see [[lady-grimhook]] and Section 9 below.

**Leads to:** end of session
