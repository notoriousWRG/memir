*Ravencrest. First contact. The ruby ring job takes shape — and gets complicated.*

## Jimmy Quickhands

[[artcoth]] met [[jimmy-quickhands]], an old associate and low-level Circle operative, at a pub in Ravencrest. Jimmy's opening lines were easy and familiar, the way people talk to someone they've written off professionally but still enjoy watching. He warned them not to put the ring on. He quoted a minimum: *"I don't leave this pub for less than 10k."*

Jimmy laid out several approaches for retrieving the ring from [[lady-grimhook]]'s manor: bribe a guard with a gambling problem, get inside during an upcoming gala, break and enter, or have someone bring it out. He identified Aimon Young, a guard with a gambling debt at the Lucky Coin, as a potential lever.

## The Lucky Coin

[[artcoth]] and [[shah-doh]] drank at the bar. [[coriac]] and [[gareth]] ran dice at a casual table. Artcoth bought five rounds for Aimon and managed to snag the guard's papers before returning them to earn goodwill. Artcoth agreed to meet the watch captain, Bryce, the following morning. That meeting went poorly — Bryce didn't trust him.

A man they hadn't noticed was watching Aimon from across the room. He left first. Coriac spotted it. Gareth and Shah followed. Gareth pulled a knife before thinking it through; Shah pulled him back as city watch approached. Scarface — short dark hair, stubble, a small scar on his cheek — walked away smug. He works for someone in the [[carusso-crime-family|Carusso organization]].

## Ovaltine's cart

The party located [[ovaltine-jenkins]]' travelling cart. Someone tried to lift something without paying. His alarm ward caught it immediately. He made a deal: one scroll in exchange for right of first refusal on any interesting or unique items they bring to sell.
