*Arc 1 session numbering PROVISIONAL. Source: "Escape and Return" Notion page.*

With Zal'goroth defeated and the infestation cleared, the party gets out. [[ovaltine-jenkins]] plays a role in the escape — his specific expertise (experimental potions, latent magic manipulation) useful where a direct route wouldn't be.

The party surfaces back in the world above and delivers to [[lady-grimhook]]. What they retrieved or accomplished in [[duergar-city]] is handed over. In exchange, she points them toward the next commission: the [[pendant-of-stars]]. The mission that will define Arc 2 is established here.

Whether the pact with [[shalindra-aboleth]] was fulfilled to her satisfaction is unresolved. The party left the city; what Shalindra thinks of the outcome has not been made clear.

Arc 1 ends.
