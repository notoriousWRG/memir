*On the road toward the Ironspine Mountains. Jimmy Quickhands catches up with them. A smaller follow-on session tracks the aftermath in the city.*

## The ambush

Night watch ran: [[coriac]], [[gareth]], [[shah-doh]], [[artcoth]]. During Shah's watch, two figures entered camp: [[jimmy-quickhands]] and a half-orc thug named Gern. A third — a halfling named Hellen — had positioned herself in the woods.

Jimmy and Gern made noise. Shah tried to talk them down; they called out Artcoth. Artcoth came out. He tried to tell Jimmy he was working an angle. Jimmy didn't buy it.

> *"Gettin in bed with a mark? Even for you, this is a bad look."*

> *"Give me the ring, or I'm gonna have to take back something... a hand maybe?"*

Artcoth was under orders to take Jimmy out. He gave the signal.

## The fight

Gareth took out two of Jimmy's bandits. He saw [[feanaro]] over the bodies — glorying in it.

Jimmy drove through the combat and put Gareth down. [[coriac]] revived him.

[[shah-doh]], Coriac, and Artcoth converged on Gern: Coriac split the arm with Inflict Wounds, Shah took it off, Artcoth finished him from distance.

Jimmy broke for the tree line. Artcoth put an arrow through him at 140 feet.

## The note

Found on Jimmy's body: an encrypted message. The deciphered core: *"Enough excuses. I've been patient enough. Take care of them."* The writer is Coal — the pseudonym for a mid-level Circle operative whose real name is Nicholas. Decrypting the full note requires intelligence work over several days.

Hellen, the halfling in the woods, fled or was captured — her fate unclear.

## Gareth's vision

After the fight, Gareth saw Feanaro standing over the bodies. Not threatening — appreciating. Watching to see what Gareth would do next.

## Side session: Art and Gareth

*Smaller session — only those two players.*

Artcoth and Gareth ran into a street hustler's shell game in the city. Three rounds, escalating stakes: 1sp → 1gp → 50gp for an Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location.

They were being followed. A Circle shadow agent — Stealth +7, poisoned shortsword, 4d6 sneak attack, evasion — had been tailing them since the Jimmy incident. Three windows to spot the tail; a chase if they made contact.

The outcome (caught, killed, or escaped) was resolved at the table.

## Open threads

- The encrypted note: what else does it say? Who else is named?
- The Circle knows the party didn't deliver. Jimmy is dead. They'll know soon.
- Hellen — alive, loose, or dead?
- Feanaro is watching Gareth take lives and finding it interesting.
- If the shadow agent escaped: the Circle has direct observations of their capabilities and habits.
- The Ironspine mine and Zal'goroth's bracers still lie ahead.
