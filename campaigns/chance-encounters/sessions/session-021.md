Round one picked up again, the two absent players dropped straight into the fight. Coriac opened with Call Lightning and Channel Divinity, aiming the full weight of it at Edrin — Ariel intercepted the worst of it, still immune to lightning, still bound to shield him. Coriac tried to reach her anyway, warmth instead of a threat; she wasn't ready to hear it.

Ariel struck back under compulsion, arcing lightning through Grimvald and the others. Grimvald drove her off with Dissonant Whispers — the first direct hit on Ariel since Casus's Augury named that path Woe. She reeled ninety feet into the air, and for a moment it read less like tactics than betrayal: they'd come to free her and hit her instead.

The party turned on Edrin in earnest — Casus, Shah Doh, and Artcoth pressing an assault that should have ended him. It didn't. At the edge of dropping, he broke the totem he'd carried off the mage's body since his escape, and the corruption took him: a Werebear, fur wrong-colored, joints bent off true.

The fight that followed ran longer and uglier than the one before it — Coriac's storm arcing wide, Grimvald's Hex and Hellish Rebuke, Casus dropped by a lightning strike and brought back by a healing word. Casus went for the spellbook mid-fight and came up empty-handed.

It was Coriac who broke it. Charging the bear directly, invoking Thor aloud — *"Stormbearer, witness the power of Thor. Let the thunder break your bonds. Witness Mjolnir's fury."* — he drove Inflict Wounds home for 29, dropping the creature to a single hit point. As it fell, Ariel's bonds fell with it. She didn't wait for the party to finish him. She ran her own lightning through what was left of Edrin, and it ended there — no totem burning slow, no last word, just the storm taking back what it had been forced to hold.

The table stopped here. What's owed — the Stilled passing, the walk back to the beach, Elara, the new ship — is falling action for next time.
