*Grimhook turns the tables on Thorne. Then turns her attention to the party.*

## The aftermath

In the chaos after the gallery heist attempt, Thorne confronted the party as his animated armor began turning on the crowd. One of his staff introduced them as "Lord Freethorn Caterham of the Cadarn Forest Kingdom" to buy a moment. Thorne was unconvinced but occupied.

Meanwhile, [[lady-grimhook]] had moved. A lackey — disguised as one of her guards — teleported into the gallery and retrieved the Rod of Rulership while the party had the defenses tied up. Grimhook emerged from the crowd holding it.

She broke the rod's tip against the floor to gasps, then told the assembled guests it had been a prop all along — Lord Thorne's announcement of their new business partnership, a piece of staged theatre. She used the rod's remaining influence to make Thorne confirm the story to his own guests. He did, visibly struggling against it. She thanked everyone for coming, tousled Thorne's hair on the way out, and left.

## Behind closed doors

At Grimhook's manor, she told the party directly: they'd been useful precisely because they weren't professionals. Their clumsy heist attempt disabled the gallery's defenses and created the diversion she needed. A real professional got in and out while they were being watched.

She'd known what they were after. She'd let them think they had a plan.

Then she offered them work.

> *"You are expendable. But you've proven yourselves capable enough — and importantly, you seem to like earning money. Possibly even power. Both of which I can offer."*

## The new contract

Retrieve a set of enchanted bracers once worn by a dark shaman named [[zal-goroth]] — a master of blood magic and soul manipulation who once served as adviser to a powerful ruler before going into exile, who hid in an abandoned mine somewhere in the Ironspine Mountains. Grimhook's sources point there.

She did not explain why she wants the bracers. She did not explain what they do.

## Items

The party sold the Silver Raven figurine (1,000 gp) and the Elemental Gem (100 gp). [[shah-doh]] bought an everburning torch. [[artcoth]] collected his commissioned bow. [[gareth]] told the group about Grimhook's side offer — "I don't like to keep secrets." [[shah-doh]] said *"I owe you my life"* to [[coriac]].
