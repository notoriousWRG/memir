*The gala at Lord Thorne's estate. The rod humiliates Grimhook. The heist goes sideways.*

## The public moment

[[lord-thorne]] opened the gala with a demonstration. He raised the Rod of Rulership — glowing faintly gold — and used it on [[lady-grimhook]] before the assembled crowd: she trembled visibly against an unseen force until she walked to the balcony and knelt before him, reciting words of admiration she clearly did not mean. The crowd laughed. She steadied herself and walked out without looking at anyone. Thorne announced a post-dinner auction of the rod to the highest bidder.

Grimhook caught the party on the way out. She gave them a Silent Stone — a gem that creates a sound-suppressing barrier — and told them: *"Do not make a scene. That's my right."* She wanted the rod.

## The gallery

The party navigated toward the gallery using stealth and deception. [[gareth]] identified activation runes on the pedestal holding the rod. They made the attempt.

The animated armor responded immediately. The party underestimated what it would take to neutralize it. Gareth went down. [[artcoth]] disengaged and ran back to find [[coriac]] and [[shah-doh]] — Coriac had been keeping the estate's staff entirely occupied with stories and had become genuinely popular with them. When they reached the hall, the armor had blocked it. Coriac healed Gareth from distance, pulling him back from death saves.

The rod was not retrieved.

## Open threads

- Grimhook's humiliation was public and deliberate. She is not finished with Thorne.
- The party couldn't take the rod — but they disarmed enough of the gallery's defenses that someone more precise might have a window.
- Where does the ring factor in? The party went after the rod instead.
