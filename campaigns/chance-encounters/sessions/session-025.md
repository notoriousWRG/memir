The session opened mid-fight. Grimvald, played by Chris because Jesse was out, won initiative and went first. He cast Dissonant Whispers at third level from the doorway, and the brood failed its Wisdom save, took 20 psychic damage, and fled thirty feet backward, wrecking the fountain on its way. Artcoth, who had turned to Coal and told him to come along, got "this is your business, friend, not mine." Coal turned and walked off another way, and nobody saw him again that night. Artcoth ran to Brother Sölvi instead and asked him and his dame to pitch in. Sölvi was already moving toward the fight.

Coriac stepped out of the water and called lightning with Channel Divinity for maximum damage, and two heads burst. Casting through his god's power gave him a flash of knowing: the thing was descended from Jörmungandr, nemesis of Thor, and now it knew who he served. It turned on him with an unnatural rage. The hydra regrew to seven heads, and the regrowth healed it. Gareth walked out and missed with a blast of energy (natural 1, Coriac was in the way), then spent the next rounds throwing scorching rays. Shah used all his movement to cross the fountain, Misty Stepped in, and swung. Dame Hervor, with Sölvi's nod, ran across the water leaving white footprints, hit with a nat 20, and used Leadership on Coriac. Shah's Divine Smite withered three severed heads so they could not regrow, and Hervor's blade, lit with a matching glow, did the same to another. By then she and Shah had traded smirks.

Sölvi cast Bless on Hervor, Gareth, and Shah, and then used his sunlit strike on the brood itself. It began to glow with a faint radiant light, and the dark energy of Grimvald's Eldritch Blast changed to gold as it passed through, so the hits counted as radiant and the heads could not regrow. The water kept spreading with an undertow, and Sölvi failed two Strength saves in a row. The tide-spawn wrapped him and pulled him under. Coriac cast Control Water and parted it, but a writhing piece of the water would not move: a water elemental was in there, and it held on, so Sölvi could breathe but could not be freed until the hydra died. Shah cut off the last head with his sword glowing, and the water began to recede. The elemental released Sölvi and withdrew toward the fountain.

Upstairs, an alarm sounded in Gareth's head, the one he had asked Ovaltine to place. Casus lay glitching on his bed like a worn videotape, and Ovaltine was beside him. Casus cast Blink and Misty Stepped toward the secret passage, while Ovaltine drank a potion and went invisible. Two thugs and a scout, a halfling, had come through the window to search. Ovaltine fought them and was beaten up. He pinned one thug to the wall and took a hit before Artcoth came up the stairs and sneaked in a stab on the other. Casus returned from the ethereal plane, grabbed every vial from Ovaltine's desk, and Misty Stepped down the stairs, falling for a half-second through a green field into the staircase. Both thugs fled. Artcoth chased the scout but rolled a 13 against a DC 15 and lost her, though a close Insight check told him she was there as a distraction for what was happening on the green. The seal on her gear matched the shards of the amphora, and the Circle had been in the house only a short time.

Casus rounded the corner of the stairs and felt something snap. He asked Ovaltine if any of the vials could keep him here, and Ovaltine, speechless, could not answer. Casus emptied his pockets at Artcoth's and Ovaltine's feet: a vial of bear blood taken from Edrin's werebear form, a letter sealed in black wax, and the Obsidian Cypher. Then he vanished. There was no afterimage this time. Ovaltine's face was defeated, and the table ended on the three things left on the floor.
