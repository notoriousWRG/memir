# Session 22 — The Island Lets Go

---

## 1 — The Charge

The Circled Zone is quiet in a way it wasn't a minute ago. The stone frame stands open and dark, no crack of lightning behind it; Ariel is present — she doesn't need the frame to be seen anymore — but she hasn't spoken since the bind broke. Edrin is gone, burned out with the totem, no body to bury. Somewhere behind the party the fog is thinner than it's been since they landed. Nothing is chasing them. For the first time on this island, the only thing left to do is decide how to leave it.

*If they do nothing:* the island doesn't need them for any of this. Önund and Tjorvi have already buried the Stilled by the time anyone gets there; Elara takes the White Gull's helm regardless; the new wreck comes loose on the tide whether or not the party's watching; Dessa, Marek, and Ollo make their choices about which ship to board either way. The facts resolve themselves. What the party would lose by disengaging is the weight of being there for it — nothing you can put a number on, and everything the last five sessions were for.

### Scene 1 — What's Left at the Frame
*[[ariels-island]] — The Circled Zone*

**Read-aloud:** "The frame stands open, unlit. No wind moves through it. Where the fight tore the ground, the grass is already different — greener, like something old finally got to feed. Ariel isn't in the frame. She's just... here, the way weather is here, close enough that the hair on your arms already knows it. She doesn't perform silence for anyone's comfort. She doesn't perform anything anymore."

**Loaded:** Ariel doesn't owe the table conversation. If she speaks at all tonight, it's the same six words she already gave Coriac — *"The storm follows you"* — nothing new, nothing explained. What she does instead of talking is the scene: at some point, unprompted, she strikes the [[forge-shard]] with her own lightning. When the light clears, her name is burned into the metal in a script none of them recognize. That's the bond — offered wordlessly, sealed, hers to give and just as easily hers to end if anyone tries to command rather than ask (see [[forge-shard]] for the mechanic this unlocks).

**Dig in:**
- [[edrins-spellbook|Edrin's spellbook]] is on the ground near where the totem burned out — untouched, unclaimed, singed at the corners. Dark-touched, druidic-leaning magic; it doesn't open easily. See the item file for the decode mechanic — this is future material, not a puzzle to solve tonight, though a player can try one passage at the table if they want to.
- The totem left nothing but a scorched circle in the grass — no body to bury, no last word.
- If Coriac reaches for Ariel without trying to direct her, that's what earns the mark — engagement, not a request.
- Push Ariel for explanation or negotiation and she doesn't get angry — she's just not there anymore for a moment. Wind drops. She's gone, then back, whenever she decides to be.

**If it comes to a fight:** none expected here.

**Triggers:**
- Coriac commands rather than asks Ariel, in any form, after the mark is made → the bond visibly strains (the shard cools instead of warms) — small, immediate, teachable.
- Someone shows Edrin's spellbook's blank-seeming page to Ariel directly → she reveals one true fact about his descent, DM's choice — this can happen tonight, no check required.
- Someone else studies or reads the spellbook → see the item file's decode table.
- Casus considers using the Ovaltine potion here → Ariel reacts directly, proportional to intent. She's free now — this reads differently than it would have under the bind. Play it honestly, not as automatic punishment.
- The party lingers a long time without moving on → gentle, not ominous: the fog beyond the Circled Zone thins further, like the island exhaling.

**Leads to:** Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Back Room
*[[ariels-island]] — The Hollowed Hull*

**Read-aloud:** "You smell the turned earth before you see it — fresh ground outside the Hollowed Hull, a modest line of driftwood markers lashed with cord. Tjorvi's voice carries a low, half-remembered work-song, more chant than tune. Önund sits apart from him, close to one of the graves, hands empty for the first time since you've known him. No crown. No decree. Just a man at a grave."

**Loaded:** They did this without the party — a marker of what the vigil actually was for. The Stilled are already at rest; there's no fight here, no scene to solve. Tjorvi finishing the song is the spine of this scene. Önund's silence is the character beat.

**Dig in:**
- Ask Tjorvi about anyone specific and he'll name them properly, plainly, the first time all arc he's said it straight instead of through the bit.
- Önund's "treasury" of junk is stacked near the graves now, like grave goods — the tell that he knew the island's price the whole time and never said it out loud.
- If someone asks Önund about something real — barrels, bracing, how he actually held this crew together this long, not the crown — he answers plainly. First competent thing he's said all arc. That's the seed for Scene 3.
- Interrupting the song before Tjorvi finishes it costs something small but real — he'll pick it back up, but it takes visibly more out of him the second time.

**If it comes to a fight:** none. They're already at rest; this scene isn't a fight to have.

**Triggers:**
- The party waits out the song rather than interrupting → Tjorvi's easier with them afterward, first sign he trusts them with what's next.
- Tjorvi is asked directly to come aboard → yes, but he wants Önund settled first — not permission, just care. Won't leave a friend mid-grief.
- Önund is asked what he actually knows, sincerely, not mocked or indulged → he sets the crown down himself, unprompted — sober, plain, done with it. The new-start beat, not a punishment for the bit.
- The party mocks the funeral or rushes it → both of them go quiet and cold for the rest of the scene — recoverable later, but not tonight.

**Leads to:** Scene 3

### Scene 3 — What the Tide Gives Back
*[[ariels-island]] — the beach*

**Read-aloud:** "The beach is busier than it's looked since the wreck. Elara's already given three orders before you're in earshot — she doesn't slow down for you. Past the tideline, a second hull works itself free of the rocks, listing but afloat. Dessa's got a bag over one shoulder before anyone's said a word about leaving. Marek's not far from her, already sorting crates like he's done it a hundred times — for a ship that doesn't have a name yet."

**Loaded:** Two ships are leaving tonight, and this scene is where people sort themselves onto one or the other. The White Gull is Elara's — she takes command with real weight, grieves on her own time, and doesn't trust this party's chaos anymore; whatever this arrangement was, it closes here. Dessa's going with whichever hull actually leaves, and that's probably the Gull. The new wreck is Coriac's, in every way that matters yet — and Marek wants a place on it specifically, if he's welcome. Tjorvi and (if Scene 2 landed right) Önund are looking for the same thing. The party doesn't have to orchestrate any of this — just be present while it happens.

**Dig in:**
- The new wreck needs a name before anyone treats it like a ship — the table's call, not the DM's.
- It needs real repair before it's a going concern — seaworthy enough to limp home under tow, not ready for open water alone yet.
- Marek's theory about ships and unfinished grief (never said aloud to Dessa) pays off quietly if anyone asks him about the new hull specifically.
- The storm draugr who survived the beach fight are still past the breakers, watching — not hostile, just present. They don't come in for this.
- Tjorvi gives Coriac one half of a paired token before they part (see [[tjorvis-far-call-token]]) — a going-away gift, not a request.

**If it comes to a fight:** none expected — the beach fight is resolved and staying that way tonight.

**Triggers:**
- The party makes a moment of Elara's promotion, or frames it around Coriac's day → she gets curter, not warmer — gives orders instead of accepting anything that sounds like congratulations. The tell, not rudeness for its own sake.
- The party lets her just get on with it, no ceremony → the closest thing to warmth she shows tonight: a nod, a clean handoff, nothing more. She's still stepping back from the party either way — this changes how, not whether.
- Dessa is asked where she's headed → the White Gull, plainly, unless someone gives her a specific reason to pick the new hull instead. She's not sentimental about which ship, just which one's actually going.
- Marek is asked, or asks first → yes, immediately, visibly relieved — he was already packing.
- Someone offers Ollo a real place aboard, sincerely, not assumed → he says yes, visibly relieved, and starts talking charts before he's finished saying yes.
- Someone offers Önund actual responsibility — first mate, quartermaster, anything with real duty, not an audience → he takes it dead serious. First time all arc he's not performing.
- Tjorvi comes aboard → per Scene 2, once he's sure Önund's settled.
- The party disrespects the new ship or the crew assembling on it → Elara's the one who notices and says something curt about it — last thing she does before the Gull casts off.

**Leads to:** Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Two Ships, One Wind
*[[ariels-island]] → open water*

**Read-aloud:** "The White Gull takes the lead, Elara at the wheel for the first time — and behind her, under a skeleton crew and a patched sail, the new wreck follows on a line, closer to floating than sinking. The island doesn't fight either ship on the way out. The fog just... isn't there anymore, once you clear the reef."

**Loaded:** This is the light, homebrew beat — the new ship becomes a standing resource, not a subsystem. Nothing here should turn into bookkeeping.

> **HOMEBREW — Standing Resource: The Intimidator**
> Once named, crewed (minimum: one willing NPC aboard — Tjorvi, Marek, Önund, or Ollo, whoever ends up staying with her), and given a destination, the ship can be sent on a downtime task between sessions instead of sailing with the party: a trade run, a cargo escort, a message relayed. At the top of the next relevant session, roll 1d6 + the aboard NPC's best relevant bonus. **4+:** it delivers — modest coin, goods, or information, DM's call on flavor. **1:** a complication surfaces — a hook, not a punishment, for a future session. The result reports back over [[tjorvis-far-call-token]] if Tjorvi's the one aboard — no need to invent a separate delivery mechanism.

**Dig in:**
- If anyone asks what to name her, this is the moment — not a vote, just whoever says it first and it sticks.
- Ollo immediately starts logging the crossing the way he logged the island — old habit, new context.
- The Forge Shard stays warm the whole crossing — storm-sense picking up nothing but ordinary weather, for once.

**Triggers:**
- The party names the new ship → lock it in, use the name from here forward.
- Anyone asks Elara how she's doing → short, flat, not an opening — "Fine. Get the line taut." She's not grieving out loud tonight, and she'd rather everyone let her be.
- Önund or Marek, if aboard, start actually doing the job — checking the tow line, the galley stores — without being asked. Nobody comments on it. That's the point.
- Coriac tests the far-call token → it works exactly once as advertised, nothing more dramatic. Save the real payoff for a later session's downtime beat.

**Leads to:** Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Ravencrest, Again
*[[ravencrest]] — [[bellwether-house]]*

**Read-aloud:** "Ravencrest smells like a working port because it is one — salt, tar, someone's fish going off two stalls down. Bellwether House still stands at the edge of the Old Quarter, and there's a new sign in the window that definitely wasn't Artcoth's idea."

**Loaded:** This is a table-opening scene, not a plotted one. Ovaltine wants an audience for whatever he's managed in their absence. The house is safe and current. Nothing here is urgent — that's the point.

**Dig in:**
- Ovaltine ([[ovaltine-jenkins]]) has been working on the house per Artcoth's earlier requests, and possibly other standing party asks — **how far he's gotten, and any complications or standout successes, is a live roll at the table, not scripted here.** Let it land however it lands.
- Mail, rumors, and a light Grimvald-patron omen are all available here if the table wants to pull a thread — none of it needs resolving tonight.
- Grimhook is not present and shouldn't surface unless the party goes looking — she's off-page, already in motion on the Pendant. See [[lady-grimhook]] for her full hook set and what's discoverable if the party visits her manor specifically.

**If it comes to a fight:** none — this scene is deliberately low-pressure.

**Triggers:**
- Grimvald asks around about the voices / the barrel → a small, ambiguous omen surfaces (nothing conclusive) — seed only.
- Anyone asks Ovaltine about the Pendant of Stars or Grimhook → he knows less than he lets on, but redirects convincingly — foreshadowing only.
- The table starts weighing what's next (Liriel's ring, the Circle, Grimhook's move on the Pendant, the new ship's first task) → let it happen here; this scene exists to open that door.

**Leads to:** end of session, and the seed for S23 prep.
