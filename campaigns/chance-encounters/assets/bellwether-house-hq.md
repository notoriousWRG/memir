The party's headquarters, current and ongoing — who's there, what it does for them, what's in motion. For the house's history and haunting, see [[bellwether-house]].

## Who Lives Here

All six of the party — [[artcoth]], [[casus]], [[coriac]], [[gareth]], [[grimvald]], [[shah-doh]] — call it home, each with their own room. Not a base they visit between jobs; where they actually live.

**[[ovaltine-jenkins]]** lives there too, as resident caretaker and shopkeep — **Ovaltine's Oddities** now operates out of the house. **[[amelia]]** and **[[bellwether-door-knocker|Bramwell]]** round out the household as permanent presences rather than guests.

## Ovaltine's Deal

Both directions: Ovaltine gets workshop space and caretaker's rights, in exchange for fronting the party's gear. The party gets a standing **10% discount** on anything from Ovaltine's Oddities, and first look — early offers — on whatever "edge" magic he's tinkering with before it reaches the shop floor.

## Rest Boons

**Sanctuary** — passive, from the original cleansing (Eleanor's lingering blessing). Always active:

- **True Rest.** Once per long rest, regain one additional spell slot of 3rd level or lower.
- **Warm Walls.** While resting here, immune to fear effects; advantage on the first Wisdom (Insight/Perception) check to notice something wrong within the walls.
- **Steeped in Soulwork.** +1 to Arcana checks made inside the house, especially regarding soulbinding or undeath.
- **[hidden] The Bellwether Blessing.** The first time the party returns after a major death or loss, one automatic success on a death save or resurrection-ritual check. One-time gift — flag it in the ledger if it triggers.

**Anchored Rest** — added S22, part of the renovation. Choose one at the end of a long rest, on top of Sanctuary:

- **Anchored Mind** — advantage on one Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw before your next long rest. Declare after rolling, before the result is known. Especially effective against charm, fear, confusion, possession, memory alteration, or illusion.
- **Prepared Action** — advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw of your choice before your next long rest. Must be declared before rolling.
- **Steeled Resolve** — temporary hit points equal to proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier (minimum 1), lasting until depleted or your next long rest.
- **Focused Insight** — advantage on the first check made with one skill or tool proficiency you're proficient in, before your next long rest.

Subtle side effect: servants, locals, and contacts in Ravencrest already know the party's names — word travels fast out of a house with staff.
