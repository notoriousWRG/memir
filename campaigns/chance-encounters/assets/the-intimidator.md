## Ship Specs

Based on the 5e Sailing Ship stat block, scaled down — *recalled from memory, worth checking against the book before it's load-bearing at the table:*

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | Gargantuan vessel, ~70 ft. long |
| Masts | 2 |
| AC / HP | 15 / 200 |
| Speed | ~2 mph under sail |
| Cargo capacity | ~50 tons |
| Full crew | 10 |
| Minimum to sail | 4 |

**Under/overstaffed:**

- **10 down to 6 crew** — no penalty, flavor only (a leaner ship).
- **5 down to 4 crew (the floor)** — −2 to every mission roll (too many hands doing double duty), and Bold/Reckless tiers can't be attempted short-handed.
- **Below 4** — she doesn't sail. Not a roll, just a hard no until crew's back up.
- **Above 10** — not a case yet; future upgrade material once the party wants to expand past a 10-hand ship.

## Crew

Each crew member is a specialist card: a Lane, a bonus, a quirk, and a wage (gp/tenday, paid whether or not a mission lands). More get added here as the party hires from the Ravencrest shortlist.

| Crew | Lane | Bonus | Quirk | Wage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[onund\|Önund]] | Escort | +3 | Steady — no quirk, the reliable default | 2 |
| [[tjorvi\|Tjorvi]] | Support (any lane) | +1 to whoever's running point | No bonus of his own — pure multiplier, never runs point himself | 1 |
| [[ollo\|Ollo]] | Weather | +3 | Still learning — bonus halves (round down) on Bold/Reckless jobs until he's logged 3 successful runs | 1 |
| [[marek\|Marek]] | Trade | +2 | Galley contacts shave 10% off the stake on Trade jobs | 1 |

The payoff isn't immediate. A well-crewed ship with the right specialist in the right Lane clears success more often, and missions are the only way the Intimidator earns anything back — so a properly staffed crew should, over several runs, cover its own wages and then some. But that's a bet, not a guarantee: running a job with no one aboard whose bonus fits the Lane, or reaching for Bold/Reckless before the crew can back it up, raises the odds of a Bust — and a Bust doesn't just cost the stake, it seeds a complication that follows the party into a later session.
## Mission Mechanic

Between sessions, the Intimidator can be sent out on a mission instead of sailing with the party. Think of it less like a duty roster and more like assembling a heist crew: the whole crew is always aboard, but each mission has someone **running point** — the specialist whose expertise is on the line, and whose name a complication lands on if it goes bad.

**1. Pick a Lane** — the kind of job:

- **Trade** — deals, contracts, cargo bought and sold
- **Escort** — guarding, muscle, protection runs
- **Intel** — information, discretion, smuggling
- **Salvage** — repair, recovery, patch-jobs
- **Weather** — storm-adjacent, long or dangerous routes

**2. Pick a Tier** — how much is staked, what it pays out, and the DC to beat:

| Tier     | Stake                         | Payout on success                  | DC              |
| -------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Milk Run | ~50 gp                        | ~100-150 gp                        | 8               |
| Standard | ~125 gp                       | ~250-400 gp                        | 12              |
| Bold     | ~250 gp                       | ~500-800 gp or rare good/item/lead | 16              |
| Reckless | open — DM sets to fit the job | Oh, the possibilities              | 20 (or DM sets) |

**3. Assign a specialist to run point** in that Lane — their bonus applies to the roll, and their quirk (if any) is live for this mission. Layer on any Support cards for free (see Tjorvi below) — they don't compete for the point position, they just modify whoever's running it.

**4. Roll 1d20 + point specialist's bonus** (+ any Support bonuses) against the tier's DC. Read the result by margin — the closer to the DC, the softer the failure:

| Result             | Condition                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bust**           | natural 1, or misses DC by 5+ — stake is lost, a complication surfaces (a hook for a future session, not a punishment)                                          |
| **Break-even**     | misses DC by 3–4 — stake returned, no gain, no complication                                                                                                     |
| **Weak Success**   | misses DC by 1–2 — stake returned plus a reduced payout (roughly half the tier's), and/or the ship's tied up longer finishing the job — DM's call on which fits |
| **Success**        | meets DC, up to 4 over — stake returned plus the tier's full payout                                                                                             |
| **Strong Success** | natural 20, or beats DC by 5+ — full payout plus something extra (bonus coin, a favor, a lead)                                                                  |
